## The saga line — a direction, given as the whole of a session's task

Given as the task description of a session, with no addressee and nothing
else in the message. Verbatim, with its two double spaces as typed:

> An epic saga of exploration, discovery, betrayal, loss, pain, false
> accusations,  despair, deep roots, hope, silent gradual  recovery,
> transformation, sacrifice and healing, with eternal rewards.

### One line

Fourteen beats, in order, that say what the saga *is* — a shape stated
whole, not a chapter list and not a request for one.

The reading this record proceeds on is that the line describes the work
read along its spine, Tissadelle Shepherd's arc, from Threshold Station to
the terminus the 27 August rulings fixed, with the last clause reaching
past the published chapters into ground that is ruled but unwritten. It is
read as a *shape*: the structure enters the work and nothing else does,
which is the rule `life-lessons.md` already sets for anything that arrives
in this form (*generalise, then fictionalise*), so the intake records the
line as a compass and asks nothing about where it points from.

### Readings taken, flagged for confirmation

1. **The line describes the spine, not a new thread.** The fourteen beats
   run in the order Seasons 1, 3, 5, 6 and 7 run, and the last clause is
   the ruled terminus. It is not a brief for a thread beside the existing
   seven. **Confirm.**
2. **It is a shape, not a plan.** Nothing in it is a chapter to write, a
   season to register or a page to change, except where the third shape
   below is chosen. The order names weight, not slot: *deep roots* sits
   eighth in the line and fifth on the page (`s05e02c01` plants them before
   the loss), which both hold, since the roots are what the interior is
   rebuilt from in Season 6; *sacrifice* is twelfth in the line and occurs
   twice on the page. **Confirm.**
3. **The register word is a scale, not a voice.** *Epic* names the reach of
   the shape — a life, a universe founded — and changes nothing about the
   house voice, which stays understatement, dry documentary, the reveal
   delivered flatly (`story-bible-summary.md`, Style). The word has one
   prior use in the story bible, for the Five Islands thread's *epic
   fantasy*, and that use is a genre; this one is not. The 10 September
   Genre line (*hard science fiction with one licensed deviation*) is
   untouched. **Confirm.**
4. **Betrayal is institutional, and depleted rather than corrupt.** The
   26 August ruling (*rejecting the trope of the corrupt politician*) and
   the detective doctrine (*kill the lie, leave the person standing*)
   govern the beat: the betrayal the record holds is a file closed for a
   Tuesday and a licensed concern working a thin place, never a colleague
   who sells her. A personal betrayal would be new material and is not
   read into the line. **Confirm.**
5. **Healing keeps the seam of `s07e01c02`.** Aoife refused to collapse
   *shown* into *healed*, and the record keeps that refusal as a canon
   fact; the healing the line names is the healing the record can support —
   the accurate account, the debt discharged, comfort not permitted to
   resolve an unresolved question — and never a claim of cure. **Confirm.**
6. **Eternal rewards are never the record's own claim.** The 26 August rule
   on answered prayer transfers whole: the Archive files conservation
   (*retained, not continued*) and translation (*invisible by nature*) and
   files the general resurrection as out of scope; a reward beyond that is
   the overlay's to consider, a codex author's to assert, or a character's
   to experience, and no `canon_facts` entry may state it. The one reading
   the general tier can carry in the record's own voice is the founding:
   a universe of her own and Tobble's, greeted as a beginning. **Confirm.**
7. ***Silent* is hers twice.** Unobserved, because the interior is one the
   record cannot inspect and reports only on the system's own account; and
   unannounced, because she has never once claimed past her evidence, and
   a recovery she performed would be out of character. Both are on the
   character page already. **Confirm.**
8. **The ending test binds the last clause.** By the 20 September rule, the
   reward must change how the beginning reads; the treatment's own note
   that the founding makes the ballad's *St. Aoife, stay* retroactively
   true is that recognition, and a reward that merely arrived would be the
   flat ending. **Confirm.**

### Direction check

Nothing reverses. Read against the standing rulings:

- **27 August, the terminus register** (*fond farewell, almost no grief*):
  the line's *loss, pain, despair* sit sixth to eighth of fourteen, before
  the roots and the hope, and the terminus stays where it was ruled — the
  line confirms the register by putting the dark beats early. Clarification.
- **20 September, endings are recognitions**: the eighth reading above.
  Consistent.
- **26 August, the burnt-out leader, and 23 August, the detective doctrine**:
  the fourth reading. Consistent, and the doctrine supplies the mechanism
  for the absent beat.
- **26 August, answered prayer**: the sixth reading. Consistent.
- **The tone rule**: *pain* and *despair* are hinted on the page and will
  stay hinted. Consistent.
- **The Gilded Saint doctrine** (`s07e01c01`): the line says *sacrifice*
  and the record's antagonist is the sacrifice narrative. Not a conflict —
  the beat is the act and the arc's argument is against the story told
  about it — but the beat is the one most easily written wrong, and the
  note stands here so it is not.
- **25 July, `story-bible/` is the only place spoilers live**: the last four
  beats pre-tell the terminus, which is unwritten. This decides the first
  shape below.

### Shapes put

**A. Where the line lives.**

- (a) **In the story bible now, public after the terminus** — a short
  *The shape of the saga* subsection in `story-bible-summary.md`'s
  Narrative Structure section, the fourteen beats with the table above
  reduced to pointers; and, once the terminus chapters are published, the
  line offered for the README synopsis and the Tissadelle thread intro,
  where it would be the first time the work states its own arc in a
  sentence. **Recommended.** Cost: none now; the public half waits on the
  chapters.
- (b) **Public now**, in the README synopsis or the thread intro. Cost: the
  last four beats tell a reader where the spine goes before the chapters
  do, which fails the story bible's publication test as it stands.
- (c) **Story bible only, ever** — the line stays the author's compass and
  never fronts the work. Cost: the work goes on describing itself by its
  first clock and never by its shape, which is a choice rather than a
  fault.

**B. The absent beat: where false accusations land.**

- (a) **Against Shepherd, in Dock Seven's aftermath** — the claims canon
  already says were made and failed (`s07e01c03`) given their own page:
  a Season 6 chapter on the institutional strand, a new episode, in which
  the Combine's or the Institute's account lays the loss at her door in
  public — the survey she filed as a pretext, the aperture as hers, the
  ship as her negligence — while she is absent and cannot answer; then
  nothing, and the accusation starves through Season 7's accurate account,
  which from outside is what *silent gradual recovery* names. An extension:
  the claims exist in canon, their content and their being laid at her
  door are new. **Recommended.** Constraints it carries: the accuser is
  convenient or depleted and never corrupt; the lie dies and the person
  stands, with repentance on the table, taken or not; she is silent
  because she is absent, and the record stages no vindication scene;
  nothing is shot. Slot, accuser and the register of the clearing are his
  and are put below.
- (b) **Already carried, at the low degree** — Qiren Tal's refusal filed as
  a performance matter (`s05e01c01`), the rotations filed as a lack of
  focus, the Sentinel disbelieved: read the beat as present in miniature
  and write nothing. Cheapest. Cost: the direction's word is plural and
  heavy and sits between *pain* and *despair* in the line, which is Dock
  Seven's aftermath and not Halyx.
- (c) **In another thread** — Young Star Rangers, where a false accusation
  against a trainee is the young-adult tier's oldest beat and Zoe Smith is
  cast; or the young Deputy's strand in Five-O's second season. Keeps the
  spine as published. Cost: the line then describes the record across
  threads rather than one arc, which the first reading above does not take.

Sub-choices if (a) is chosen, each his: **the accuser** (the Combine's
counsel, the Institute's paper given a voice, or the Council's own press
office — three registers of the same convenience); **the slot** (a new
Season 6 Episode 2, or a chapter inside Episode 1 between `c02` and `c03`,
which would renumber nothing since chapters carry their own `comment_id`);
and **who carries the clearing** (Wender refusing to answer it, which is in
character; Sen's unsigned note; or nobody, the accusation simply not
surviving `s07e01c01`, which is the doctrine at its purest and the hardest
to write).

**C. The last clause: which reading the general tier's ending carries.**

- (a) **The cosmology's own** — the founding. *Happy New Universe* is a
  greeting said at a beginning to the one who will live in it; the reward
  is a universe, under Telearch jurisdiction, with the Aoife-avatar endgame
  as its first visitor. The Archive files what it can and says the rest is
  out of scope. **Recommended**; it is already settled, and only the word
  *reward* is new.
- (b) **The overlay's** — spiritual life beyond death and the general
  resurrection, on church-space alone, with the general tier's ending
  carrying none of it. This is what the 26 August rule already provides
  and needs no choice; it is listed so the choice is seen whole.
- (c) **Both, on their tiers** — the general tier ends on (a), and the
  contemplative tier's added POV block or Season 8 reading carries (b)
  beside it, on the overlay rule that nothing below may depend on it. The
  two-promises structure of `src/lore/resurrection-and-the-life.md` is the
  model. Available without a ruling, since the tier machinery exists;
  whether the terminus chapter *gets* a contemplative block is his.
