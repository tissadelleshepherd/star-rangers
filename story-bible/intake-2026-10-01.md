## The Federation's seat on the Oversight Council — the year

Verbatim: *"Add the year the Federation's seat was first filled."* The one
absence PR #903 left marked on `src/lore/the-oversight-council.md`,
delegated to this session to fix.

### What constrains it

The record fixes nothing between the charter (2723 UCSD) and the first
drift report (2815 UCSD) on the timeline. The Federation page says only
that contact came *decades after* the charter, through the Survey Corps'
fold-route mapping; that the material-support compact is *renewed by
assembly vote each generation*; and, since #903, that the seat is the
longest-standing of the non-voting ones and is refilled at each renewal.
The Council page says the Council answered *slowly and by seat*, and that
for *the first generation after contact* the Corps answered to a body
seated by one people. So the year must sit after contact, after at least
one compact renewal, and far enough before 2826 UCSD for *longest-standing*
to mean something.

### Taken: 2791 UCSD

Within a generation of a contact in the 2760s (left unfixed), after the
compact's first renewal, and a generation and a half before the record's
present — so the delegate sitting in 2826 is the second or third, which is
what *replaced at each renewal* needs. Alternatives considered: 2776
(earlier, leaving the Council less than one generation of a human-only
house after contact, which the page's *first generation* line would then
overstate) and 2805 (later, close enough to the present that
*longest-standing* describes little). The year is a number on three pages
and in the guide, and moves in one edit if he wants another. Contact
itself, the first renewal and the other two polities' offers stay undated.

**Direction check:** a choice delegated on a question he asked the day
before; not a change to any ruling. Realized on the Council page, the
Federation page and the canonical guide.

### Ruled, the same day: *"Approved"*

The year stands as his: 2791 UCSD, PR #905 (merged on the delegated
