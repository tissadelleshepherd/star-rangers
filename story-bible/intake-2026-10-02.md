## *Nai*, and whether it should be *Nyi*

Verbatim: *"Short form of Krenyi as Nai should be Nyi instead?"*

### The reading

The record never treats *Nai* as a short form of *Krenyi*. Every definition
(`src/glossary/krenyi.md`, `src/lore/krenyi.md`, the rank-ladder page, both
Krenyi character pages) calls it a state-marker meaning *still relevant
here*, placed before a name and explicitly not a title, a greeting or a
species name. Nothing derives it from the species name, so there was no
clipped form for *Nyi* to spell better. Two further grounds for leaving it:
the near-collision with *Naiad* is the stated in-world reason the formal
name *Krenyi* exists (`src/glossary/naiad.md`), so removing the collision
removes that explanation; and *Nai* reads at once where *Nyi* has no obvious
English pronunciation, against the reader-legibility rule for alien
vocabulary.

What the question did surface was a drift: three phrasings (the glossary
`short`, the lore description and opening line, and the S01E02C01 canon
fact *Obligers resist clarity; Nai demand clarity*) had let *Nai* read as a
second name for the people, against the body text's *not a title*. Put as
three choices: keep *Nai* with that tidy-up (recommended); rename to *Nyi*
as a full canon rename with a migration row, a sweep of eleven files and a
rewrite of the Naiad rationale; or keep *Nai* and coin *Nyi* separately
(advised against, since the Krenyi's whole posture is refusing to be filed
by name).

### Ruled, the same day: *"Option 1 with the tidy-up"*

A clarification in the CLAUDE.md sense. The five edits (two glossary and
lore phrasings, the canon fact now reading *the Krenyi demand clarity*, the
story-bible species note, the changelog) landed as PR #909, approved by him
the same day (*"Approved"*) and merged green. The spelling is settled.

**Direction check:** a clarification of the 30 September Krenyi rulings,
not a change to any of them; the Naiad entry's rationale stands untouched.

## The Krenyi's limitations, and the origin reading

Verbatim: *"Do Kyreni have any specific limitations compared to humans
except for being few in number and slow to reproduce, both of which were
evolutionary adaptations on their homeworld?"*

### The limitations, answered from the page

All already on `src/lore/krenyi.md`, and none needed a change: no outside
medicine (nothing donated, grafted or transplanted persists, so a Krenyi
hurt past self-repair cannot be helped as a human would be); blood
hazardous to a human; no interbreeding with any species and no romantic
attachment to humans; human emotional grammar learned rather than had, so a
novel social deception briefly defeats them; unable to un-notice, and so
unable to join a room's small social fictions; a sense that yields a
direction and never a number, earning no certification; unreadable to
humans by construction; three registers absent (deep fear, fury, grief that
does not resolve), which is why human entertainment finds no handholds and
shared play is closed as a way into company; poor keepers of anything whose
value has not yet been demonstrated; mythified in absentia, worst after
Champion selection; and grief-predation resistance that is *mostly*, not
absolute. Two things that look like limits and are not: they build and run
no agentic machines, filed as a posture; and the record states no cognitive
or physical ceiling anywhere, by the rule that minds are not ranked.

### The origin reading, which is the point of this section

**The premise is not in the record, and the record declines it on
purpose.** The page files the small population as *arithmetic*: a birth
rate far lower and slower than the human one (his correction of 30
September, *rather than infertility*) inside a lifespan of centuries. It
gives no cause. *An Origin the Record Doesn't Hold* (PR #856, 23 September)
states that no survey, charter or timeline places the Krenyi, that the
absence is theirs and not the Archive's, and that the entry stays empty on
purpose: filling it *with a reconstructed system, an inferred point of
origin, a plausible homeworld drawn back from their biology* would replace
a fact the Krenyi have stated (that they decline to be placed) with a
fabrication they have not. An evolutionary account of the rarity is exactly
a homeworld drawn back from their biology, so the Archive cannot carry it
as it stands, and a chapter cannot carry it as narration.

Two further points the reading turns on:

- **It is the Krenyi's own knowledge, withheld.** The page is exact that
  the Krenyi keep their own history in their own calendar and withhold only
  the *placing* in cross-species contexts. So if the rarity is an adaptation
  to a homeworld, that is a fact a Krenyi holds and chooses not to give,
  which puts it in the second of the 20 September kinds: a fact nobody in
  the record holds until the prose makes it, living in `story-bible/` until
  a chapter lands. Even then it would enter as what a Krenyi source said in
  their own reckoning (a POV block or a Krenyi-authored codex entry,
  canon-for-its-author), and the lore page's refusal to place them would
  stand unless he rules otherwise, because the page's discipline is the
  Krenyi's stated wish and not a gap in the Archive's knowledge.
- **Evolved or made is itself open.** The name *Quiet-Built* and the page's
  own phrase *not by discipline but by construction* leave the question
  whether the Krenyi evolved at all unasked, and the record's own
  refusals, with the Arilon reading the Fellowship records without
  adopting, lean on it staying unasked. An evolutionary account would
  answer it by implication.

His *"Approved"* the same day was taken as the go-ahead for PR #909; the
reading above, given in the same answer, stood unchallenged, and his next
direction (*"Record the Krenyi origin reading in the intake"*) is this
section.

### What stays his, put as shapes

The evolutionary-adaptation account is his and is not nothing: it is a
plausible authorial background. Where it lives is the choice.

- **(a) Authorial background, unrevealed (recommended).** Held here as his
  working account of why the Krenyi are few, never on an Archive page, with

  lore page then carries it as *what a Krenyi source said* and keeps its
  refusal to place them.
- **(c) Not a fact about the world.** The premise dropped; the rarity stays
  uncaused, as the page already has it.

## Diplomatic and polite, but essentially honest

Verbatim: *"Krenyi are diplomatic and polite but essentially honest"*

### The reading

A clarification, naming in one line a manner the page already described in
pieces: *not rudeness; it is efficiency*; *not usually unkind about it*;
*Nai* as a courtesy; liaison as *saying the material thing and declining
the rest*; and the Social and Relational list's *honest: they do not use
social deception*. What the line adds is the relation between the three.
Taken as: honesty is the floor and the manner sits on it. Diplomacy is the
choice of which true thing to say, to whom and when, which is what the
material-answer-then-decline already is. Politeness is the present-tense
courtesy, not preliminaries, so it coexists with *no small talk*. And the
manner never buys a tactful untruth: a Krenyi softens the delivery or
declines it, in words, and leaves the fact where it is.

Realized as a bullet and a paragraph in `src/lore/krenyi.md`'s *Social and
Relational* section, citing Syra's receipt (S01E01C03) and Toval answering
to *Cadet* as the cases the record already holds, and one clause in the
glossary body.

### Readings flagged

1. **Diplomatic never includes a white lie.** *Essentially* read as *at the
   core*, not *mostly*: the record keeps no case of a Krenyi saying a false
   thing for kindness, advantage or ease, and the paragraph says so. If he
   meant a people who will shade a fact for courtesy, that is a different
   people and a reversal of *they do not use social deception*.
2. **The manner is theirs, not learned.** Human emotional *grammar* is
   learned (the page's existing cost); the courtesy is the Krenyi's own,
   present-tense, and not an imitation of human manners.
3. **Declining is said, not silent.** A declined elaboration is declined in
   words, so what follows cannot be read as assent. This is kept distinct
   from the quiet *withdrawal of relevance* from a drifted claim, which the
   page and `language-design.md` file as a reading rather than a courtesy.
4. **Liaison stands.** The *In the Corps* line that liaison is what a Krenyi
   sensorium makes cheap gains its manner here and changes nothing.

**Direction check:** a clarification of the 30 September rulings and of the
page's own *not rudeness* line; nothing reversed. Syra's and Toval's pages
untouched, since both already show the manner.

### Ruled, the same day: *"Agreed"*

All four readings confirmed as drafted, *essentially* as *at the core* and
never *mostly*; PR #911, merged green. Nothing on Krenyi manner is open.

## Designed, perhaps; engineered from humans, no

Verbatim: *"They may have designed by a higher power but are not
genetically engineered from human ancestors"* (the Krenyi; the same evening
as the origin reading above).

### The reading

Two halves of unequal standing, and the page now keeps them apart.

**The negative half is a finding.** The record was already positioned to
state it: `src/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy.md` files humans and Krenyi as
*two peoples of independent origin*, and the Krenyi body says the same in
its own terms (an immunity that destroys human genetic material; no
interbreeding under any circumstance). What was missing was the sentence
on the Krenyi page itself, and the naming of the explanation it excludes:
the nearest frame invites *they were once human* above every other
account, and that is the one the record can rule out. Added to the origin
section as a paragraph and to the glossary body as a clause.

**The positive half is a reading, held and not adopted.** *May* is kept as
*may*. The page says that *built* has been read as a hand above them doing
the building, that the cosmology has tiers whose work attaches at
authorship (the made-minds page), so a designed people is a thing the
record could in principle hold, and that it does not hold this one: no
instrument has read a point of design in a Krenyi and the Krenyi decline
the question. A devotional reader keeps *designed by a higher power*; a
Cascade reader says *authored* and names no tier; the Archive says
neither, and files the reading as live and unexcludable on the Arilon
grounds.

**Direction check:** re-grounds the morning's premise (*evolutionary
adaptations on their homeworld*), which is withdrawn by this direction
rather than reversed by any ruling; the three shapes put under *The
Krenyi's limitations* are resolved by it and the `open-questions.md` entry
is marked. An extension of the 23 September origin section, which keeps
its discipline: the entry on *where* stays empty, and what was added is
what the Krenyi confirm (not human) and what they decline (made or not).
The *Quiet-Built* gloss as self-construction stands beside the second
reading of *built*.

### Readings flagged

1. **The Archive asserts no theology.** *Higher power* on the page is the
   devotional reader's phrase, kept in that reader's mouth; the Archive's
   own voice says only that the reading is live. This is the 26 August
   three-homes rule applied to a people rather than a prayer.
2. **No tier is named.** A Worldwright specifies laws rather than outcomes
   (`cascade-structure-and-limits.md`, settled 30 July), so a Worldwright
   is not the designer the reading points at; the made-minds page puts
   authorship at a higher tier, and the page cites it without placing
   anyone's hand on the Krenyi.
3. **The Krenyi's own refusal is unchanged.** They confirm the negative
   half as a material fact and decline the positive half with everything
   else about *what are you, really*.

### Put as shapes: what *higher power* means, if he means a tier

- **(a) Unnamed, as drafted (recommended).** The record keeps the reading
  live and names nobody; the devotional and the Cascade readers each keep
  their word for it.
- **(b) A Cascade reading in the Codex.** A named in-world source, Concord
  or Academy, arguing from the made-minds archetype that a people with a

- **(c) A devotional reading in the Codex.** A Fellowship or Communion
  voice reading *Quiet-Built* as *built by God*, on the overlay's own
  terms and at its own tier: the three-homes rule's Codex home, with
  canon no less settled after reading.

## Devout humans as Champions

Verbatim: *"Devoutly spiritual humans, like Saint Aoife, also make for
suitable champions"* (the same evening, on the Champions section).

### The reading

The complement to *readiest Champions in the record*, and the record
already held its halves. The Levril page says low-salience encounters
cluster among *the contemplative, the devout, and the deeply attentive*
because their disciplines train what rendering requires, *attention that
does not grasp*, and files that as rendering practice and not a faculty

attention. The Overfold says contemplatives reach the same reading by

body. And the abbey's telling of Aoife names the only fitness there is for

runs on what devotion *trains*, which is the pattern selection reads, and
not on creed or favour. Realized as a paragraph on the Krenyi page beside

### Readings flagged

1. **Suitable by discipline, not by creed or favour.** *Devoutly

4. **The devout person's own reading stays theirs.** *Chosen by God* has
