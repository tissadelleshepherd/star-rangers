## Fifth, later the same day — the Journal's reading level

A separate session, which began as a conversation on why mathematical
abstractions are misread in popular science and produced a Journal entry from
it (#714, extended in #715). When the entry had grown to three sections and a
fourth was asked for, Dermot, verbatim:

> Add that to the journal entry too or split the journal entry as needed.
> Journal entries are treated as contemplative reading level, so being
> impossible to misunderstand is more important than using plain language for
> the story bible itself and the journal entries

### What it says, in one line

The Journal and `story-bible/` are written in the contemplative tier's
register, and in that register precision outranks plainness.

### Readings taken, and how each was realized

1. **"Contemplative reading level" is a register, not a gate.** The
   contemplative tier in `edition-reader-profiles.md` is defined by depth,
   pace and exactness — the 800–1,200-word band, "length is earned by
   reflective work." Read as: write the Journal to that profile. *Not* read
   as: gate the Journal to the contemplative editions. Journal entries stay
   on every edition that carries the section; nothing in `lib/editions.js`
   or `lib/content-filter.js` was touched.
2. **"Impossible to misunderstand" over "plain language"** is read as three
   habits. A technical term or a proper name is preferred to a vaguer plain
   word, and is defined on first use. A claim that is only loosely true is a
   misunderstanding waiting to happen, so the pass checks facts and
   attributions, not only wording. And there is no `plain:` register for the
   Journal, unlike lore. Applied the same day to all four entries: three
   attributions in the first draft were found wrong under this test and
   corrected — Euclid's fifth postulate had been called intuitively obvious
   (it was the one postulate mathematicians doubted for two millennia; the
   entry now cites the 1820s and 1830s and Gauss, Lobachevsky and Bolyai), Cauchy had
   been credited with the picture-proof Weierstrass's function overturned
   (it was Ampère's 1806 claim), and Brousseau with coining *epistemological
   obstacle* (Bachelard's term, which Brousseau imported).
3. **"The story bible itself"** extends the same standard to the internal
   notes: intakes and working documents are written to be exact and
   verifiable, not simple. No existing note was rewritten on this; it binds
   from here.
4. **"Split the journal entry as needed"** is read against the band: a
   2,700-word entry is more than twice the tier's ceiling, so it split along
   its section breaks into four cross-linked entries of 860–1,200 words each,
   with the parables section as the fourth. The first keeps its slug and
   URL, since #714 and #715 already link it.

### Direction check

An **extension** of the 2026-08-31 amendment (the Journal carries the
reasoning; the changelog carries what changed) — it says what register the
reasoning is carried in. Consistent with the 2026-09-03 tier ladder (tiers
are registers and casts, not gates) and with the 2026-09-04 principle that
tiers are not content ratings. Reverses nothing.
