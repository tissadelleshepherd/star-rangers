## Second — "Teddy is curious and bright"

Dermot, verbatim, answering the face question the 6 September intake held
Teddy's portrait on:

> Teddy is curious and bright

### Direction check

An **extension** of the 6 September direction (*soft and warm, but never weak
or foolish*): the first thing canon says about a Pandoid face, and it says it
in the same register as the body — a quality, not an anatomy. *Strength,
gait, mass and face are all their own* still describes none of them.

### Read as

- **Curious** — the face is turned toward things; the expression is open
  attention. Consistent with everything the survey logged (the one Pandoid
  who comes up to the levels the party works) and with the chapters (the
  youngest goes up past the margin to see).
- **Bright** — alert and intelligent, read into the eyes; and, taken
  literally as the prompt now takes it, eyes that catch the fungus-light.
  Both readings were put in the prompt; neither asserts a colour.

### Realized, and what happened

The face clause was added to the queued prompt in `images.md` and two rounds
were run the same morning (`image-out/characters-teddy/round1/` and
`round2/`, two variants each), the second with ape anatomy negated by name.
**All four came back a great ape** — *upright, furred, heavy-shouldered, no
snout* is the generator's description of a gorilla, and negating the ape does
not move it. The naming caution in `species-design.md` forbids an ape's body
exactly as it forbids a bear's, so nothing is filed. Round 1's first variant
has the best face of the four: bright, open, plainly curious.

### Open, put as choices

1. **How the body escapes the two attractors.** (a) *He gives one body
   detail that is neither bear nor ape* — a positive feature the prompt can
   lead with (a ruff or crest, the set of the eyes, the proportion of the
   limbs, the shape of the hands), which is canon and his. *Recommended: it
   is the only shape that yields a Pandoid rather than a costume.* (b) *The
   face-turned-away frame* — the prompt as first queued, face in shadow by
   construction; asserts no face, but the direction just gave one, and a
   portrait that hides the thing ruled is an odd first portrait. (c) *Accept
   round 1, variant 1* — the best face, on a body the doctrine argues
   against; a reader will see a gorilla in a cave.

## Sixth and seventh, later the same morning — "alien alternatives to DNA" and "generalization of multicellular organic biological life forms"

Dermot, verbatim, two messages a few minutes apart while Teddy's portrait
was being filed:

> alien alternatives to DNA

> generalization of multicellular organic biological life forms

### Direction check

Two directions, read as one entry, because they are the same move made on
the two halves of one sentence — *multicellular organic biological life* —
and because the ground they extend is already one page. Canon holds, in
`cross-biosphere-taxonomy.md` and the `formtype` glossary entry, that DNA is
one solution found once on one world, that nothing obliges another
biosphere to use nucleic acids or to be built of cells, that no other people
is a metazoan, and that *life and mind are specified; their implementations
are not*; and in `the-shape-of-a-life.md` that the chemistry half is settled
elsewhere and only pointed at. What neither page does is say what the record
has *found* in DNA's place, or what *multicellular* generalises to. So this is
an **extension**: the negative (nothing recurs) already stood; the positive
(here is what the survey has met) is new, and it is built so that no named
species gains a fact its own page does not carry.

### Read as, and realized

Drafted as [What a Life Is Built From](../src/lore/what-a-life-is-built-from.md),
category Cosmology beside *The Shape of a Life*, in the Archive's voice with
a plain-register summary. Its shape:

- **Four things every life does** — a boundary, a metabolism, a record that
  copies with error, organisation — read out of the formtype's *self-
  maintaining, self-replicating, energy-processing structure* and stated
  as the survey's working requirements, with the rule reversed: the four
  recur on every surveyed world, not one of the solutions does.
- **Alternatives to DNA**, grouped by distance from the familiar: other
  polymers (other backbones, alphabets, handedness; Prismere's
  silicate-carbon and Sardain's non-water solvent as the anchored cases);
  no polymer at all (a *composition* whose proportions are the record;
  records kept on mineral surfaces); a partner instead of a code (Verdance,
  quoted from the taxonomy file, generalised); and the limit case, a record
  kept by telling (Fliade), which the survey files as inheritance too and
  marks as a translation.
- **Multicellular, generalised**: the requirement is cooperation,
  differentiation and replacement; the cell is Earth's way of getting them.
  Three other shapes on the record — modular at other scales, *continuous*
  (no units at all), *federated* (a population that is one body, cross-linked
  to *Persons, Counted Otherwise*). *Organic* generalised the same way:
  carbon everywhere the survey has looked closely, marked as an observation
  and not promoted to a law. The line the entry ends that section on:
  *every word in the phrase* multicellular organic biological life *is a
  translation except the last*.
- **What the survey does with it**: treat the requirement, not the
  molecule; never argue from substrate to standing (the Ilveth in the Corps
  as the closing example).

### Readings flagged for confirmation

1. **One entry, not two.** The alternative was a *Heredity* entry and a
   *Bodies* entry; one was chosen because the second direction's sentence
   contains the first, and because both are footnotes to the same
   taxonomy-file finding. Split if the two are meant to grow separately.
2. **The hard-SF content is real chemistry, unnamed.** Other backbones,
   alphabets and handedness are the alternatives Earth chemistry already
   knows how to build; compositional and mineral-templated heredity are the
   two serious pre-polymer proposals in Earth's own origin-of-life
   literature. None is named on the page, so the record commits to the
   classes and not to any Earth theory.
3. **Two unnamed worlds are asserted** — one with a compositional
   heredity, one whose earliest records were mineral surfaces — and one
   unnamed continuous-bodied organism *filed as a single cell for eleven
   years*. Each is a survey fact with no world attached, on the pattern the
   taxonomy file uses for the vessik's neighbours. Nothing is invented for
   any named species: Prismere, Sardain, Verdance and Fliade are quoted only
   for what their own pages say.
4. **Carbon everywhere, so far.** A new observation, stated as one: every
   biosphere examined closely is carbon-built, the survey has a chemical
   argument for why that is common, and it marks the absence rather than
   ruling out another element. Consistent with Tír na nÓg's and Prismere's
   pages; strike it if the record should say less.
5. **The Told as the limit case of inheritance.** The page says the
   survey has used *inheritance* for what passes by telling, and files the
   line between telling and body as a translation. It asserts nothing about
   Pandoid biology — no heredity, no substrate — only about the survey's
   usage, which the Fliade entry's *keep their record by speaking it*
   supports.
6. **Category Cosmology**, beside *The Shape of a Life*, rather than Physics
   or Institutions. The alternative was Institutions beside the taxonomy
   file; Cosmology was chosen because the page's spine is the formtype.

No image. The entry joins those awaiting a card (`images.md`, 5b); under
the Conventions an abstract illustration of this is a thing no camera can
take, so a prompt may be written when he wants one.

### Approved

Dermot, verbatim, on PR #733 as it stood: **"Approved"**. All six readings
confirmed — one entry; the chemistry real and unnamed; the two unnamed
worlds and the one unnamed organism; *carbon everywhere, so far*; the Told
as the limit case of inheritance; category Cosmology. The entry is canon as
drafted. Nothing from these two directions remains open.
