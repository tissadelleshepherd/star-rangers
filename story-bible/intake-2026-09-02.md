# Intake — 2 September 2026

One question, asked and ruled in the same session. Nothing here is undecided,
so `open-questions.md` gets no line.

## The question, verbatim

> Do the Star Rangers routinely accept non-emergency casual hitchhikers?

## Canon check

Nothing in `src/` addresses casual passengers directly. The settled record
answers it by implication, all pointing the same way:

- The charter's remit is humanitarian and operational — respond to boundary
  events, protect persons in those environments, gather and share data
  (`src/lore/formation-of-star-rangers.md`, `src/timeline/2723-star-rangers-charter.md`).
  A ride between ordinary ports is none of those.
- Every passenger the lore names is a mission passenger: the *Kalypsis Dawn*
  "carries delegates, evacuees and survey teams", and its service record is
  delegations under a mediation and civilians lifted from a failing outpost
  (`src/lore/kalypsis-dawn.md`).
- Courier work is "of last resort", and the Farline Courier Guild is the
  commercial alternative (`src/lore/fellowship-of-light.md`). Harmonic
  corridors make interstellar traffic routine and deep-space liners exist
  (`src/lore/ftl-mechanics.md`, `src/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`),
  so nobody needs a Ranger hull to travel in peacetime.
- Neutral-passage agreements rest on taking no side; a hull that carries
  whoever asks is one fugitive or one politician away from an accusation of
  favour. Funding is data licensing, contributions and route fees — no fares.

So the implied answer is **no, not routinely**. A lift happens as an
exception with a stated reason: an evacuee, a delegation under neutral
passage, a courier-of-last-resort run, or someone the Frontier Corps is moving
under its caretaker mandate.

## Options put

1. Leave it unstated — the published canon already answers it by implication
   and a reader cannot reach the wrong conclusion. *(Recommended.)*
2. Add a clarifying paragraph to the formation lore or the *Kalypsis Dawn*
   entry saying the Rangers are not a passenger service, exceptions named.
3. Carve a customary exception for Frontier circuits, where the circuit hull
   may be a young world's only regular caller. New material, touching
   neutrality; would need a lore proposal.

## Ruling

**Option 1** (Dermot, same day). The question stays unstated in canon. Don't
re-propose options 2 or 3; if a chapter ever needs a civilian aboard a Ranger
hull, give the lift one of the four stated reasons above and let the scene
carry it, rather than adding a policy page.
