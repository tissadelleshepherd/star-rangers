**Status: draft, unpublished, story-bible only.** Not a chapter file, no front
matter, no `comment_id`. It stays out of `src/` until Dermot decides where —
and whether — it belongs.

**Timing deliberately unfixed.** After Suvra Kel's entry into Corps service;
no season, no year. Location is Threshold Station, because Sen's desk is.

**What it leans on:** `src/characters/suvra-kel.md`, `src/lore/ilveth.md`,
`src/characters/sen.md`, and the plural-responsibility ruling of
`intake-2026-08-23.md` (drafted into the Plural Minds glossary entry in the
same PR).

**What it commits to if published:** Dail Coppen, a four-member plural rigger
(minor, no character page); Suvra Kel and Sen Lovax having met, closely enough
that Suvra knows Sen's arrangement — Sen's page says that knowledge is earned,
so publishing this commits to a prior acquaintance; the responsibility rule
applied in a hearing, including the diagnosis exception offered from outside

distance from Sardain" is deliberately unnumbered. The phrase *"one warmth"* deliberately
echoes S08E01C01's "one warmth among the warmths" — same record, same
instrument, different room.

::: pov suvra-kel

The hearing annex was kept at the comfort of the majority, and Suvra Kel had learned to hear that sentence the way the majority heard *weather*. In such air her crown read almost nothing. Walls at body-warmth, table at body-warmth, six people seated in it like lamps in a lit room — and one of the six a little brighter than sitting still could explain, which she noted, and did not answer, because it had not been said to her.

She wore the shell with the coolant loop running and spoke through the click-box on the table, and the annex had been told all of this in advance. Assessor Marchant asked their question a third time anyway.

"Which of them cut the interlock?"

Four days earlier the fitting bay on deck nine had been the best room she had seen since Sardain. Blackout, smoke to the deckheads, suppression staged in the corridor behind her — and everything in it legible: the fire loud in one corner, the structure cooling in an order that told her its bones, and two warmths behind the jammed hatch, low, going quiet the way the living go quiet, which is from the edges in. She had gone in ahead of the water, because the water that would save the bay was the one thing in it that could blind her. She had called the hatch. And Dail Coppen had come through the smoke with the cutter already lit, and had taken the interlock out at the collar, and the service space beyond it had vented its season of assay cores into the fire, and the two fitters had come out breathing.

That was the whole of what she had seen, and she had now said it three times.

"Assessor," she said, and heard the box translate the clicks into the flat voice it gave her, which she had stopped resenting. "I will spend this once more. There was one warmth at the interlock. It was Dail Coppen."

"Coppen is four people," Marchant said. "The yard is entitled to know which of the four destroyed its stock. If the board can establish that the member fronting at the time —"

"You are asking me to partition a body," Suvra said. "I do not have the instrument. A front changing is not a thing warmth shows me, and if it were, I would still have watched one body carry one cutter to one hatch. One warmth. It was theirs."

Dail Coppen sat at the end of the table and had not been asked anything for some time. When they had given their own account earlier they had spoken part of it in the doubled register their crewmates knew for a co-front, two of them at the window at once, and Marchant had made a note at that moment as if something had been conceded. Now Marchant turned from her, and their voice went kind, and their face — she could not help it; it was simply light — spent a little heat.

"There is another road," Marchant said to the room, gently. "If the system were to accept assessment — if the board could regard the act as one member's, an impairment, contained — the other three would walk out of this without a mark against them. The yard would not oppose it. I offer it as a kindness."

Nobody on Sardain would have spent warmth on a sentence like that. She watched the brightness of it sit on Marchant's face, and held her people's oldest courtesy, which had crossed the whole distance from Sardain without a scratch: what a body says has not been addressed to you.

The clerk of record, who had three people's stillness and one lamp's worth of body like everybody else, looked up from the desk.

::: pov sen

Cael had verified the timeline before the hearing convened, because Cael did not attend hearings without having done the arithmetic that made most of them unnecessary. Cutter on the interlock at the ninth minute. Hatch open at the tenth. Suppression released at the twelfth, which the bay's own log put within a minute of structural involvement. Two fitters, both breathing. One service space of assay cores, lost. The numbers closed. The numbers had closed before anyone in this room had opened their mouth, and Cael had already said so once today, inside, in the tone Cael reserved for proceedings that continued anyway.

Sen held the desk and kept the record, and entered Assessor Marchant's third question in the form the standard required: *asked; not a question this standard recognises.*

"For the record," Sen said, "the board is reminded of the position in this jurisdiction, which is the position in most. A plural system answers jointly and collectively for the actions of the body they share, whoever was fronting, and however many were fronting together. The board has no lawful interest in which member held the front at the ninth minute. There is no finding available on that question, and none will be entered."

"And the assessment road," Marchant said. "That is also the position, Archivist."

"It is an exception, and it is not a road," Sen said. "It is a finding of dysfunction, on a psychiatric diagnosis, with everything such a finding carries — standing, licensure, the right to be believed in rooms like this one. It does not spare three people. It re-files four."

Wyn had been quiet through the timeline, because timelines were Cael's, and was not quiet now. *Say who it's for.* And because Wyn was right, and because the record was better when Wyn was right out loud, Sen said it.

"The board may also wish to note whose burden the kindness lightens. A yard's liability is smaller if its stock was destroyed by an impairment than by a rigger's lawful judgment it now has to argue with. The offer is declined or accepted by Dail Coppen alone. But it should be weighed as what it is."

Dail Coppen said, in one register, "Declined."

The witness from Sardain had not moved through any of it. Sen had read her file — the watch priced in duration, the written notes her crewmates kept — and had concluded that the Archive and the Ilveth had arrived, from different directions and different physics, at the same desk: see everything, answer what was asked. *One warmth. It was theirs.* Three people had heard that testimony, and all three had, for one indivisible moment, wanted to shake her hand.

The board found as the standard directed. The breach of the yard's seal was entered against Dail Coppen, all four names; the two lives were entered the same way, in the same line, because the law that would not split the debt could not split the credit either; and the costs and the commendation went to the one name the four of them answered to together, which was the only name the law had ever had for them.

Sen closed the file and did not write the last thought down, which was Wyn's, and then Cael's, and then everyone's: *the Archive has been asked to partition a person before. The answer has not changed.*
