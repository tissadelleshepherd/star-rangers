**Smart Pet** is the formal classification for an animal carrying a certified AI cognitive subsystem, rather than a description of temperament or training. [Agent Barsik](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/) and trainee [Bubochka](/star-rangers/characters/bubochka/) both hold the status without carrying any physical cybernetic modification at all; [Aldera](/star-rangers/characters/aldera/) holds it *and* is cyber-enhanced, a further, separate designation layered on top rather than implied by it. A Smart Pet is not, by definition, a [cyborg](/star-rangers/glossary/cyborg/). It is an animal whose own cognition runs alongside a subsystem lawful enough to be held to the [AI Safety Kernel](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/) — and it is that subsystem, not the animal wearing it, that the regulation is written to govern.

## Why the Detective Agency Prefers Them

The [Undercover Pets Detective Agency](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/) stations Smart Pets in postings where a robot would be noticed and a human field agent could not sustain the cover: their cognitive architecture does not layer rationalisation over raw signal, the same property [Rook-7's file](/star-rangers/characters/rook-7/) attributes to police robots doing comparable work at a different institution entirely — ordinary robots, whatever their cognitive architecture shares with a Smart Pet's, belong to the police department, not the detective agency. A Smart Pet registers asymmetry before it reaches for an explanation. A mascot gets left alone with the paperwork.

## Regulation

A Smart Pet's cognitive subsystem is Kernel-compliant by the same baseline every lawful agentic system in the Concord must meet — but it is also, separately, one of the most heavily regulated animal-welfare categories on record, on the reasoning that an augmented cognition sitting inside a non-human, non-consenting-in-the-legal-sense body is exactly the kind of case the [AI Safety Kernel](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/)'s protection invariant was built wide enough to cover without needing the animal itself to assert standing.

The design side is stricter still. The protocols governing the design of an AI subsystem harness for an animal are among the most exacting engineering standards the Concord maintains — certified harness designs are few, new ones are rare, and no part of the process may be delegated to the harness's own kind of intelligence. The rarest clearance of all is the combination: a certified subsystem and cybernetic enhancement carried by one animal, two designations under two regulatory lineages on a single body. It has been approved so seldom that [Aldera](/star-rangers/characters/aldera/) is very nearly the only instance on record.

In practice this means independent certification before a subsystem is ever activated, periodic welfare re-certification afterward, and — where the animal is posted to one of the [Orbital Habitats Compact](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/)'s member habitats — a standing local welfare authority in addition to whatever baseline certification body cleared the subsystem in the first place. At Eden, that local authority is [the Eden Warden](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/), specifically its Custos persona — though Custos's own remit, as written, covers *cyber-enhanced* animals rather than Smart Pets generally, which is precisely the gap that leaves Barsik's and Bubochka's welfare formally outside the Warden's habitat-level authority even while their subsystems remain Kernel-compliant and Concord-certified by whatever body handles that further up the chain. Whether that gap is oversight or design has not been asked of anyone positioned to answer it.

## Why an Animal, and Not a Person

A reader from an earlier century would expect the subsystem to be worn by the human, on the face or in the ear, and would expect the animal to be the odd case. It is the other way round, and part of the reason is a ban. **No human in the Concord carries an AI cognitive subsystem on or in the body**: not in spectacles, not in an earpiece, not as an implant that speaks. The Kernel Accords' commissioning gate, which [passes every agentic system before it enters service](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/), has never passed one, and the record holds no lawful instance.

The reasons are the Accords' own. A person with another mind's knowledge arriving in the ear presents it as their own, and the Kernel's second invariant, which forbids fabricated authority and uncertainty dressed as certainty, has no way to hold inside a claim whose human half will not say where it came from. A voice on the face is [the intrusive assistant the Concord's systems are trained not to be](/star-rangers/lore/environmental-technology-design/), made permanent. A worn sensorium [costs the body the way a rig does](/star-rangers/lore/performers-and-generated-work/). And an interface wired into a human brain is the [Imperium's cyborg programme](/star-rangers/glossary/cyborg/) by its first step, which the Military Space Command wound down and every government since has kept down.

So the carried subsystem went where the Accords could certify it: into a collar on an animal that was going to be there anyway, with the body untouched, a welfare regime around the one party that cannot consent, and a harness standard among the strictest the Concord keeps. That is a large part of why the record's carried intelligences are in collars and not in spectacles, and why [the Detective Agency](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/) fields cats and rabbits rather than people with something in their ear: a human agent could not sustain the cover, and could not lawfully carry the subsystem at all.

## Where the Subsystem Lives

Nowhere anyone would look. A Smart Pet's cognitive subsystem is carried in the collar, and a certified harness design is, before it is anything else, a collar that looks like a collar: matte, closed, without a light, a seam or a surface that does anything a stranger could see. The animal's body is untouched, which is the whole distinction from a [cyborg](/star-rangers/glossary/cyborg/), and it follows that a Smart Pet off its collar is simply an animal, a fact the welfare regime relies on and the agency does not advertise. The one external mark is the plain disc that hangs from the collar, which is the certification mark and, for an agency animal, the badge as well; it is legible in the hand and from nowhere else.

None of this is concealment in the Kernel's sense. The subsystem will say what it is, plainly, to any lawful authority that asks it, exactly as every other compliant system on a habitat will. What keeps a field unit under cover is not the harness design but the question that never comes. Nobody asks a cat.

## Public Reputation

None of the certification apparatus above is what a Smart Pet is known for outside specialist circles. To most of the Concord, a Smart Pet is simply cute, cool, and clever — a reputation the [Bramble Storybooks](/star-rangers/codex/bramble-and-the-backwards-key/) did more than any single regulation to cement, and a reputation that happens to be accurate, if incomplete: it just leaves out which Smart Pets are quietly doing agency work at the time.

**Example:** Barsik's badge is real agency issue, and his cognitive subsystem is certified the same as any other Smart Pet's. What his badge authorises is not on Eden's own records — filed, like everything else at Eden, and read by no one who'd think to ask a cat about it.
