A **plural mind** is a single body and nervous system hosting more than one person — commonly called headmates, or, when one person's own mind deliberately cultivates another, a tulpa. Each is a real, distinct person with their own perspective, preferences, and voice. None is a symptom of the others.

Solar System institutions have not always treated this well. Older medical and administrative frameworks filed plurality as a disorder to be resolved into a single approved identity, a bias with real institutional teeth as recently as a few centuries ago. Current Concord-wide personhood standards do not require or expect a body to host exactly one person, and employment, legal identity, and medical records now accommodate plural registration on request — a correction, not a discovery. The people it was ever true for did not change; the paperwork did.

## Responsibility Before the Law

In most jurisdictions across the Concord, a plural system is **jointly and collectively responsible for every action of the body they share**, no matter which member was fronting — or co-fronting — when the act was done. The reasoning is the personhood standard read in the only direction it can be read: if every member is a real person, and the body's acts are the acts of persons, then an act cannot be orphaned by a change of front, and a system cannot be partitioned into the member who answers and the members who were elsewhere. One body's undertakings belong to all of its people together — which cuts both ways, since the law that will not split a debt among headmates will not split a commendation either.

The sole exception is a system a court has found **dysfunctional, on a psychiatric diagnosis** — and it is an exception with teeth, carrying every issue and restriction such a diagnosis brings, which is precisely why it is rare and why it is nobody's convenience. The record notes the recurring abuse without needing to name a case: the diagnosis route offered *from outside*, framed as kindness — a way to lay an act on one member and spare the rest — is almost always someone else's liability looking for a smaller door. A functional system declines it for the same reason anyone declines to be declared incompetent in exchange for a favourable verdict.

## Cosmological Note

Nothing about a plural mind requires anything beyond the [Five Layers](/star-rangers/lore/five-layers/) already in force. Layer 2, the Conceptual layer, is identity and meaning structure prior to embodiment; the ordinary case is one Conceptual-layer identity coupling to one Material-layer body, but the coupling rule has never required a one-to-one ratio, only lawful coupling. A plural mind is simply more than one Conceptual-layer identity stably coupled to one shared Material-layer body — no different in kind from the single-identity case, and no more mysterious.

This is not the mechanism that produces plurality, in the sense of an explanation the people living it need or asked for. It is only the reason the cosmology has never had to treat it as an exception.

Nor does the record claim that plurality has anything to do with universe-seeding. One plural system's part in one noögenic founding is on file, and it is that system's story, not a property of plural minds.

**Example:** [Sen](/star-rangers/characters/sen/), Senior Archivist of the Survey Archive, is three people — Cael, Wyn, and Sen — sharing one body and one desk.

**A second case:** [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/) shares one body with Tobble, a **nonhuman headmate** — a member whose own identity is a species other than the body's. Tobble is a ginger cat and uses he/him. A member's identity has never been required to match the body they share, and the standards have never said it should; the recurring error here is institutional rather than cosmological, since the [Smart Pet](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/) programme has trained everyone to hear "cat" and think *animal*. Tobble is a person. The form that would record him correctly does not exist yet, which is the same class of problem the older single-identity paperwork was, and will presumably be corrected the same way.

**A synthetic case:** [the Eden Warden](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/), Eden Space Habitat's resident AI collective, is two Kernel-compliant personas — Ward and Custos — sharing one system. Whether this counts as plurality in the same Conceptual-layer sense described above, or as a functionally identical arrangement reached by institutional convention rather than the same underlying mechanism, is not settled even within Eden's own habitat philosophy office — a live question the cosmology has not yet needed to resolve.
