**Robot** is the record's plain word for an embodied machine: a body built to move and work in a place, which the record calls a *frame*, or in the older catalogue word a *chassis*. The record already uses it this way. [Rook-7](/star-rangers/characters/rook-7/) is a bipedal robot on an older civilian chassis, [Xenon](/star-rangers/characters/xenon/) is a maintenance robot whose patients are other robots, and [Lumbarian](/star-rangers/characters/lumbarian/) is a heavy repair robot built to work open vacuum. A mind with no frame of its own, such as a ship's navigational intelligence or a habitat collective, is a [systems mind](/star-rangers/lore/embodied-cognition/) and not a robot. [The Eden Warden](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/) has no chassis and no mobile unit; what it inhabits is the habitat's structure.

**The word names the frame, and the mind is named apart.** Where only the machine matters, *robot* is enough. Where the person matters, the record names the mind separately from the frame it works in: [Jeeves](/star-rangers/characters/jeeves/) is a [Kernel-compliant](/star-rangers/glossary/kernel-compliant/) mind in a domestic frame, the standard domestic-model frame of the Eden welfare program's catalogue, and he has never requested another. The separation is the law's own. Commissioning is required of [any system and every robot](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/), but [commissioned standing](/star-rangers/lore/commissioned-standing/) is granted only to a system commissioned as a mind: a commissioned pump is still a pump, a machine is disposed of and a mind is not, and the record refuses the vocabulary that would blur the two. Kernel-compliance likewise certifies the construction of the mind, not the kind of machine that carries it.

**A robot's likeness follows its role.** No robot is made more machine-like than its role requires. A maintenance machine such as Xenon needs hands, not a face. Where a machine is plainly mechanical, its role is the reason: Jeeves's frame is human-shaped because it was built to work a human-shaped kitchen, and its two round dark lenses make no attempt to be eyes. A machine built to be read by people goes as far toward their likeness as its work needs and stops short of disguise. [Lucene-9000](/star-rangers/characters/lucene-9000/) is built to look as human as a machine can be made to look, and no further, and the honesty is carried by disclosure: hers, made early and without ceremony. The record files her design class as a *social gynoid*, a mobile humanoid built from the frame outward to be read by people and to read them back. The [Orbital Habitats Compact's](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/) filings call Eden's embodied assistants *mobile AI humanoids*, and the Eden Warden, which holds welfare authority over them, counts them as other people, never as its limbs.
