An **enrolled text** is a constitutional instrument kept under the register conventions the [Solar System Concord's](/star-rangers/lore/solar-system-concord/) compact made standard: a **founding enrolment**, entered once and never edited in place, together with the complete, ordered record of every amendment enrolled against it — each in [difference form](/star-rangers/glossary/difference-form/), each beside its predecessors and never over them.

The defining property is that the current wording is not the authority; the record is. Any reader may take the founding enrolment, apply the amendments in order, and must arrive at the same current text — and a consolidation that disagrees with that derivation is a defect in the consolidation, always. Authority rests on re-derivability rather than custody, which is why the compact outlasted every regime that enforced it: custody of a document can be seized, and re-derivability cannot.

The register also keeps what failed. Defeated, withdrawn, and returned proposals stay filed as *not enrolled*, in the same form and with the same filed reasons as the amendments that carried — the law's null results, kept because a register of enrolments alone is a history written by the winners.

Full doctrine: [The Enrolled Text](/star-rangers/lore/the-enrolled-text/) (lore). For the proposal to read three centuries of the register as data, see [The Amendment Atlas](/star-rangers/codex/the-amendment-atlas/) (codex, valid-for-its-author).
