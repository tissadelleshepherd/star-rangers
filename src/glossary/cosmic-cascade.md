The **Cosmic Cascade** is the fixed ordering of causal authority in the Grand Ensemble Multiverse. It is ontological, not political — a structural description of how causality resolves, not a claim about which tier is most virtuous.

Causality resolves from higher tier to lower tier and does not reverse.

The full hierarchy is:

> [Hyperomnium](/star-rangers/glossary/hyperomnium/) → [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/) → [Conseleme](/star-rangers/glossary/conseleme/) → Metawrights → Formwrights → Archewrights → [Worldwrights](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) → Universes with [Concordants](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/) (Concordance) → [Telearchs](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/) → [Mediarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) → [Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/) ([Frenar](/star-rangers/glossary/frenar/)) → [Levrils](/star-rangers/glossary/levril/) → [Champions](/star-rangers/glossary/champions-heroes/) / Heroes

No tier can override a higher tier. No moral authority is encoded in the hierarchy. Champions are sentient material beings — organic or artificial — chosen by Levrils.

The Cascade is mostly, not purely, hierarchical. Worldwrights and Telearchs are parallel branches under the Archewrights (creation and continuity, as peers), and the [Mediarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) are co-created, standing under both the Telearchs and the [Concordant Principals](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant-principal/). Descent is creative rather than imperative — higher strata create and define the layers beneath them more than they command them. Time in the Cascade runs in three registers: the Celestials and below are immanent in Material-layer time; the middle strata (Mediarchs — commonly Exarchs — Telearchs, and Worldwrights) exist in **conceptual time**, real sequence that is not the Material clock; only the strata above the Worldwrights are eternal outright. The one crossing between registers is the [Avatar](/star-rangers/glossary/avatar/) exception: a higher stratum incarnated as a limited person at Celestial level or below, like its source without being the same person as it.

**Example:** A Worldwright can establish a new Concordant; a Telearch operating within that Concordant cannot un-establish it, no matter how disruptive it proves. Causality in the Cascade runs one direction only — down, never up.
