**The Told** is the name the [Pandoids](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) of Fliade carry for themselves, rendered in the record's translation register. *Pandoid* is the survey's coinage and is kept in every filing; this is what the people say, as far as the record holds it, which is by way of the *Below the Roof* thread rather than by anything the survey has been told.

It is a name of the same kind as every other name the people have. Among the Told a person is called by **the first true thing said of them that others went on repeating** — a telling, worn down to a name by use. [Stone-First](/star-rangers/characters/stone-first/) moves a stone before entering a place, every time. [Carried-It-Sleeping](/star-rangers/characters/teddy/) carried the survey's relay through the squeeze and it woke, and they held it, and it slept. [Went-Round](/star-rangers/characters/went-round/) goes round a thing rather than through it. The people's name for the people is made the same way: the first true thing said of all of them is that they were *told*. A small one is told the first warm seep, the long dark, the coming of the up-people, and every telling before those back to where the tellings run out, and when they can tell them all without a mistake they are one of the Told. There is no other way in.

**A name grows.** It is not replaced when a truer thing is said of a person later; the truer thing is added, and the name gets longer. A long name is a grown one, and what it shows is clarity and maturity — the person has been seen more exactly, and has become more. The record renders a grown name the way it renders the first one, in translation with hyphens, and does not shorten it.

The name draws the line the people draw. A runner is not told anything. The up-people — the survey — are, in the people's reckoning, *people who have not been told*, which the Told hold to be a different thing from not being people, and easy to drop.

**What the record does not hold.** No phonetic form. The people's speech has not been recorded in any medium the Archive keeps, and the thread told from inside is a telling rendered, not a transcription; the record translates their words the way it converts their intervals into [UCSD](/star-rangers/glossary/ucsd/), and marks that it has done so. Whether the people's word is one word or a phrase, the record has not been told. The record's *they* for an individual is its translation default, not a claim about a pronoun system of their own.

See [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) for the people, and the *Below the Roof* chapters — [*Nobody Looked Up*](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c01/), [*Carried It Sleeping*](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c02/), [*Someone to Ask*](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c03/), [*Waiting to Be Asked*](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c04/) and [*Theirs to Move*](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c05/) — for the name in use, including a telling that is a small one's own and names nobody, because it is about somebody else, and the first name the record has watched grow: *Stone-First-Who-Waited*, said in the warm the night the up-people moved a stone.
