The **Council of Harmonarchs** is all of a universe's [Concordant Principals](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant-principal/) sitting together. Each governs one [Concordant Zone](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/) alone; in council they govern the universe.

It is **not a Cascade tier**. There is no separate class of being called a Harmonarch-in-council — it is the same minds in their second seat, which is why the Council appears nowhere in the Cascade spine between the Principals and the [Telearchs](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/).

## The Council Is Not the Authority

A [Universal Authority](/star-rangers/glossary/universal-authority/) is the Council **plus a presiding seat**, and the distinction is load-bearing rather than pedantic. The seat is held by the universe's **founder** — its originating seed mind, where the universe was raised by [noögenic seeding](/star-rangers/lore/noogenic-seeding-system/) — or, where there is no founder, by a **lead Harmonarch** the Council seats from among its own members. Same office; only the occupant's origin differs.

Neither part is an Authority on its own. A founder without a Council is not an Authority, which is precisely what makes a [Noögenic Protouniverse](/star-rangers/glossary/noogenic-protouniverse/) *proto*: the seed mind has arrived and the seat has an occupant in waiting, but no Concordants have stabilized, so there are no Harmonarchs to seat around it.

## How It Decides

Through a **Consensus Field** rather than by counting votes — the mechanism by which a plural formation reaches one position without a tally. A Consensus Field is *how the Council decides*; a founder is *who presides while it does*. The two are not alternatives, and records describing an Authority as singular and records describing it as plural are describing the same institution from different angles.

## Two Seats

Every Harmonarch holds its own zone **and** a seat in council, and the two writs differ in reach: a Principal's ends at its zone boundary, the Council's is universe-wide.

This is what "a Principal answers to its Universal Authority" actually means — subordination to the body it is a member of, not to a superior being standing over it. **A Harmonarch outvoted in council is bound by the decision inside its own zone regardless.** That is the whole of the Authority's hold on a Principal, and it is enough.

**Compare:** the Star Rangers' own **Council of High Captains** — every serving High Captain sitting together, holding an authority none of them holds alone. The resemblance is structural rather than borrowed; neither institution is modelled on the other.

**Example:** A continuity measure that costs one zone and protects the other eleven is exactly the decision a single Harmonarch cannot make and a Council can. The Principal who argued against it administers it at home the following day, and nothing has been done *to* it — it is a member of the body that decided.
