**General operations** is a service track of the Star Rangers, shown in slate blue on the rank tab. A track is not a corps. It is assigned independently, so a Ranger of any corps may hold the general operations track, and a track can change while the corps stays the same. [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/) came in through the Music Corps' college, opened her service on general operations, rotated to the Survey track and back, and never changed corps to do it.

On a post, the **general operations department**, commanded by a Chief whose working title there is **Chief Ranger**, runs the watch across every corps aboard. Threshold Station's general operations watch on Dock Seven is the record's first example. Because a Chief who has commanded such a department has already done, on a smaller scale, what a Starwarden is asked to do, the track is the usual road from Chief to a first command. It is the usual road, not the only one: a Chief from any track may be given a command posting.

The name also files a kind of mission. A mixed group of specialists sent on a shared task is either a **general operations** mission, which may be led by a Chief, or a **special purpose** mission, which is led by a Starwarden or above; beyond that rank line, which filing a mission takes depends on its context. Neither is a combat formation, because no branch of the Star Rangers is military.

General operations names a track, a mission or a department, never a section. Teaching at the Academy and serving as a Planetary Representative are not normally counted as general operations. The full account is in [Rank and the Chain of Command](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/), *Track Is Not Corps*.
