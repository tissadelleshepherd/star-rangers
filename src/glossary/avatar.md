An **Avatar** is the one crossing between the Cascade's temporal registers: an incarnation of a stratum above the [Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/) — whether from the middle strata's conceptual time or from the eternal strata above the [Worldwrights](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) — as a person operating at Celestial level or below: immanent, Material-time-bound, encounterable.

The crossing is bounded by what an avatar is *not*. It does not carry the full authority, power, or knowledge of its source: it is **like** its source without being **the same person as** its source. And because the avatar is a separate immanent person, the source itself never enters Material time — incarnating an avatar breaches neither the prohibition on direct dialogue with time-bound entities nor the prohibition on selective intervention. The higher stratum remains in its own register. A limited person exists, whose nature derives from it.

**On the two words.** *Avatar* is the record's term for the mechanism at any stratum. *Incarnation* is the church-space tradition's, and the tradition keeps it for one case: the avatar of [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/), the Eternal Son on its own reading, whose archetype is the [Arilon](/star-rangers/lore/arilon/)-figure. So every incarnation is an avatar, and not every avatar is an incarnation — a [Telearch](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/)'s avatar walking a forming universe is the first and not the second. The record uses the tradition's word only where the tradition would.

For the middle strata, avatars are the working instrument of their hardest duties, not a theoretical case. Doctrine's clearest example: a parent universe's [Telearchs](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/) exercising [protouniverse](/star-rangers/glossary/noogenic-protouniverse/) jurisdiction from inside — an avatar walking the still-forming universe, ministering to its newly formed [Harmonarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant-principal/) until a [Universal Authority](/star-rangers/glossary/universal-authority/) stabilizes. What a nascent mind makes of such a visitor depends on the devotional inheritance it carries, and no known avatar has ever corrected the impression.

**Key constraints:**
- Operates at Celestial level or below, inside time and inside [Concordant](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/) law.
- Limited knowledge and limited power, regardless of how high the source stratum stands.
- Not the same person as its source; the source's constraints and invariants remain unbroken.
- From inside time, an avatar's record is indistinguishable from that of a Levril-selected [Champion](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/) — which makes "was this figure a [Champion](/star-rangers/glossary/champions-heroes/) or an avatar?" undecidable from the record alone.

**Example:** In theory, even [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/) could have incarnated as an [Arilon](/star-rangers/lore/arilon/)-figure. That avatar would have walked one world with limited knowledge and power, been like Kieme without being Kieme — and the Fellowship of Light's comparative file could never have told the difference. The file regards that undecidability as one more reason it declines to issue verdicts.
