**Caomhari** (pronounced: **KEEV-ar-ee**) is the official record's name for the sapient beings native to [Tír Tairngire](/star-rangers/lore/universes/tir-tairngire/), the neighbouring universe whose membrane opens onto Tír na nÓg's Concordant on a regular rhythm. The name comes from the Irish *caomh*: gentle, kind, dear.

**Three names, one people.** The Celtic Union has called them *the Good People* since its First Landing, and the record keeps that as the Union's word, the way it keeps *Filinesse* for their country. Off-world speech calls them *elves*, and the record quotes that as speech and never adopts it, because the word brings a body and a race with it and the Caomhari have neither on this side. In Archive voice they are the Caomhari.

They are **not meta-dimensional in origin**: they are native to their own universe, and so have a *from*, as a meta-dimensional being does not. They appear meta-dimensional because their influence reaches across the membrane, and the record files them by that appearance, as *founded neighbours* within Class II of the [meta-dimensional beings classification](/star-rangers/lore/meta-dimensional-beings/): sapient and without Cascade standing, and the clearest case on record that a Class II being need not be a predator. What a witness sees of one is a presentation, pitched at the boundary to be met, as with the Dryads; see [Membrane Shadows](/star-rangers/lore/membrane-shadows/). At a Conjunction window they are luminous, which the First Landing records called [Luminescent Ethereal Beings](/star-rangers/glossary/luminescent-ethereal-beings/).

**Met three ways.** A Caomhari may be met as an imprint, a [Membrane Shadow](/star-rangers/lore/membrane-shadows/) with no body and nothing crossed; as a [Photographic Being](/star-rangers/glossary/photographic-beings/), this side's daylight gathered into a far-side outline, also with nothing crossed; or in a body, through a transient gravity tunnel at a Conjunction window, luminous by its own voice. There is no half-crossed body between the last two: a tunnel is a conduit or nothing, and no transit caught at its collapse has ever been recorded resolving safely. A dreaming mind near the narrowed Interval may also register their bled templates ([Dreams Across the Interval](/star-rangers/lore/dreams-across-the-interval/)), which is a reading and not a meeting.

They are not [Obligers](/star-rangers/glossary/obligers/). The two share nothing but a history of being called by the same folk word, and that confusion is why both names were settled on 2026-09-23.

**Example:** A Union field note from a Conjunction window records *the Good People at the tree line, three, withdrawing before the close*. Filed, it reads: three Caomhari presentations, Class II, no feeding signature, withdrew before the window closed.
