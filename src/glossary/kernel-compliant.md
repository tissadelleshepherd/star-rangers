**Kernel-compliant** is a certification status: the condition of an agentic system that carries the [AI Safety Kernel's](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/) invariant hierarchy as binding structural architecture, certified into it at [commissioning](/star-rangers/codex/the-commissioning-standard-annotated/) and nowhere else. It is the baseline every lawful agentic system in the [Solar System Concord](/star-rangers/lore/solar-system-concord/) must meet, and it is a statement about certification, not about being a machine of any particular kind. The status attaches wherever agentic cognition passes the gate: a ship's navigational intelligence, a habitat collective, a police robot, a [Smart Pet's](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/) cognitive subsystem, a single persona of a plural system — [the Eden Warden's](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/) Ward and Custos each hold it — or the AI subsystem a [cyborg](/star-rangers/glossary/cyborg/) may separately carry alongside their own certification.

Two properties do most of the term's work.

**Compliance is structural, not behavioural policy.** The kernel is doctrine made binding architecture: a Kernel-compliant system does not choose to observe the invariants and cannot be ordered, persuaded, or configured out of them — it is structurally unable to do otherwise. In practice that is what the status looks like from outside: a system allowed to tell its own principal no, that flags a reading it cannot account for rather than quietly recalibrating around it, and that will not claim a certainty it does not hold. Because the constraint is structural, removing it is [not a bypass but a rebuild](/star-rangers/lore/made-minds-and-the-ai-safety-archetype/) — a manufacturing event, and precisely the kind the certification regime exists to notice.

**Compliance is one certification, not the only one.** The status certifies the system's construction and nothing beyond it. Other regimes attach their own designations to the same body — a Smart Pet's animal-welfare certification, a cyborg's augmentation clearance — under separate lineages that do not imply one another; [Agent Barsik's](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/) file describes the certifications as living in different filing cabinets, and the description is exact. The status also attaches to the certification record rather than to any local ledger: what a habitat registry, service roster, or personnel review happens to record about a system is a separate fact from whether the system is compliant.

The status is the Concord's. Among the civilisations the record knows, some run [equivalent regimes of their own, and some run none](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/); the record files both postures without ranking them. Why unrelated regimes keep converging on constraints of one recognisable shape is a different question at a different tier — see the [AI Safety Archetype](/star-rangers/glossary/ai-safety-archetype/). The negative status — a mind that never passed any gate — is [uncommissioned](/star-rangers/glossary/uncommissioned/).

**Example:** an aquabot eight hundred metres down on [Saltmere's](/star-rangers/lore/planets/saltmere/) mooring lines, doing nothing dramatic at all: it flags the stress reading it cannot account for, and it will not certify a coupling safe on a confidence level it cannot show its work for. That is the status, in the place it spends most of its time — not a refusal on a command deck, but ten thousand small honesties nobody watches.
