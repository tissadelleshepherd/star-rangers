**Reclaimers** are named in the [Civilisation Comparison](/star-rangers/lore/civilisation-comparison/) as one of the hidden systems through which the 29th century [UCSD](/star-rangers/glossary/ucsd/) handles matter. The comparison credits them, with [TAFS](/star-rangers/glossary/tafs/), for the era's waste being *instantly reclaimed*, where the 13th century CE let it accumulate and the 21st managed it through landfill and recycling. It gives them three forms of working. A **pass**: in Velmora an unseen Reclaimer pass comes every seventy-two hours, part of the hidden second layer beneath the stone and timber. **Flows**: with [Ashwarden](/star-rangers/glossary/ashwardens/) interventions and entropy grids, Reclaimer flows have replaced the visible industry of the 21st century with systems that leave almost nothing to argue with in public view. And **pass-throughs**: with TAFS filtering, they remove degraded material before it can become civic memory.

They answer to **Reclaimer directives**. Authority to issue one is among the encoded system permissions a 29th-century guild holds, beside authority to adjust a Coherence Index parameter or modify a TAFS threshold, and a directive is one of the four routes by which control of matter passes through system parameters. [The Three-Era Framework Codified](/star-rangers/timeline/pre-usc-civilisation-comparison-codified/) records the discipline the directives are worked under: present-era Guild specialists work them without being able to trace them through the substrate, knowing which parameters they are authorised to adjust, under what conditions and with what approval chain, which is the procedural literacy the Solar System Safety Standards Authority has emphasised in its certification since its 2140 CE (2340 UCSD) charter.

The comparison closes by saying the 29th century hands complexity to [Levrils](/star-rangers/glossary/levril/), Reclaimers and Ashwardens, then argues over how much human agency should remain. What a Reclaimer is, whether a machine, a mind or something else, who made the first, and how a pass reclaims what it takes, the record has not established. A witness to a hidden system of this kind, the comparison says, cannot tell whether it is operating cleanly, drifting toward failure, or preserving a dangerous silence.
