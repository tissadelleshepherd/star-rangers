**Telearch** (pronounced: **TEL-ee-ark**; plural: **Telearchs**) is the Cascade tier above the [Mediarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) and [Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/). Though the Cascade spine lists Telearchs after the [Worldwrights](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/), the two are **parallel branches under the Archewrights** — continuity and creation respectively, created and defined as peer functions: a Telearch does not derive from a Worldwright.

Telearchs are supervisory agents responsible for cross-Concordant continuity governance and enforcement delegation. Their function is coordination at scale: they allocate enforcement scope and continuity budgets across multiple [Concordants](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/), and coordinate responses to illegal cross-boundary manipulations.

The retired legacy designation **High Celestials** most often meant this tier — though older records also used "higher celestials" for the force-governing [Mediarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/), which is why the term was retired as ambiguous; see [High Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/high-celestials/) for the disambiguation rule. The correct current spelling is **Telearch**; earlier records may also use *Telerarch*.

**Constraints:**
- Telearchs do not bypass Concordant law.
- Telearchs must preserve continuity over local preference outcomes.
- Telearchs cannot revise the hierarchy order.
- A [Noögenic Protouniverse](/star-rangers/glossary/noogenic-protouniverse/) produced by a mind within one of a parent universe's Concordants remains under that parent universe's Telearch jurisdiction — and the higher Cascade strata reachable through them — until the protouniverse stabilizes a [Universal Authority](/star-rangers/glossary/universal-authority/) of its own. In doctrine, that jurisdiction is exercised from inside: an [Avatar](/star-rangers/glossary/avatar/) of the Telearch walks the forming universe, ministering to its newly formed Harmonarchs directly.

## How a Telearch Reaches Inside a Universe

A Telearch is native to no universe and supervises from outside, which leaves a problem: a force has to be administered from *within* the thing it runs through. The Cascade answers it twice, at different depths.

**The standing answer is the [Mediarch](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) tier. A Mediarch is a proxy Telearch** — the Telearch line acting inside a universe, scoped to a single force-domain. This is the Mediarch tier's substance rather than a resemblance, and it turns three otherwise-bare assertions about that tier into consequences:

- **Co-creation** is not two parents of equal kind. The Telearch line supplies what a Mediarch *is*; the [Concordant Principal](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant-principal/) line supplies the zone-scoping that gives it somewhere to act — substance and jurisdiction, not a merger.
- **Continuity prevails on conflict** is not an arbitration rule imposed from above. Continuity is what a Mediarch substantially is, and a proxy cannot rule against the office it proxies for.
- **The dual obligation** is not divided loyalty. Zone-local operation of a force runs with the zone's Principal; that force's continuity across boundaries and universes runs up the Telearch line. A proxy answering locally for local matters and to its own principal for everything else is an ordinary arrangement.

**Why force-continuity runs up this line specifically.** Continuity across membranes is a trans-membrane fact, and the Telearch branch — native to no universe — is the only tier that is itself trans-membrane. [The Common Manifold](/star-rangers/glossary/common-manifold/)'s 20+ band encodes the nonlocal boundary conditions that Worldwright and Telearch operations must satisfy, which is to say the continuity branch already operates where the between is. A resident office cannot hold what does not stop at its edge. See [What Crosses the Interval, and Why](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) for the same division stated from the physics side.

**The exceptional answer is an [Avatar](/star-rangers/glossary/avatar/)** — a separate limited person, crossing a temporal register into Material time at Celestial level or below, sent for a particular duty (see the protouniverse constraint above). A Mediarch, by contrast, stays in conceptual time above the Celestials and is a standing office rather than a person sent down. Two answers to one problem: one permanent and structural, one occasional and personal.

**Example:** When a boundary-zone anomaly begins affecting three adjacent Concordants at once, coordinating the Celestial response across all three is Telearch work — allocating enforcement scope and continuity budget across a scale no single Concordant's Celestials could manage alone.
