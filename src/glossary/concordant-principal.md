A **Concordant Principal** — commonly called a **Harmonarch** (plural: **Harmonarchs**) — is the governing mind of a single [Concordant Zone](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/). It administers the zone's physical and [Etheric](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) rule-set — keeping the zone's conditions coherent day to day — and answers to the universe's [Universal Authority](/star-rangers/glossary/universal-authority/). "Concordant Principal" is the formal office title; "Harmonarch" is the common form, and both are canonical.

**Every Harmonarch holds two offices at once.** Alone, it governs its zone. Sitting with every other Harmonarch of its universe, it *is* part of the universe's Universal Authority — the **[Council of Harmonarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/council-of-harmonarchs/)**, which together with the universe's founding seed mind (or, where there is none, a **lead Harmonarch** drawn from the Council itself) constitutes that Authority. So a Principal answering to its Authority is answering to the body it sits in, not to a separate mind above it. The Council's writ is universe-wide where a Principal's ends at its zone boundary, and a Harmonarch outvoted in council is bound in its own zone by the decision anyway.

The office is stewardship, not legislation. The rules a Principal administers are the [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/)'s design; the Principal keeps them running and does not author or amend them.

**Key constraints:**
- A Principal's writ ends at its zone's boundary — which is why boundary zones between Concordants fall to no single Principal's tidy jurisdiction.
- Subordinate to the Universal Authority of its universe; distinct in kind from [Telearchs](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/), who supervise continuity from outside the universe rather than governing within it.
- Co-defines, with the Telearch line, the [Mediarchs](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) of its zone's forces: zone-local operation of a force runs with the Principal, while continuity across boundaries runs up the Telearch line and prevails where the two conflict.

**Example:** When a zone's Etheric coupling drifts out of tolerance, restoring it is Principal work. When the drift starts affecting the neighbouring zone across a boundary, it stops being any one Principal's problem — the Universal Authority balances it, and if it crosses universes, Telearch supervision takes over.
