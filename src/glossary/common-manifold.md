The **Common Manifold** is the higher-order spacelike and timelike structure lying beyond any single universe's dimensional floor. It governs cross-[membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) coherence, causality buffering, and fold survivability, and every universe the record has ever reached in the [Grand Ensemble](/star-rangers/lore/ensemble-multiverse/) is seated in it — which is what the name records, and it is a statement about the record's reach rather than about the Ensemble: the manifold is a [Formwright's formtype](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/), common to its formcluster and not beyond it, and a differently-woven formcluster — [Wholecloth](/star-rangers/lore/wholecloth-formcluster/) is the reported case — is seated otherwise. Universes that agree on nothing about their own dimensional structure still meet here.

Each **coherent universe** — whatever its origin — occupies its own bounded [cosmic membrane](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) within the manifold, separated from its neighbours by [the Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/). A [Concordant](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant/) Zone is a region *inside* a membrane, never a membrane in its own right, and one [Worldwright](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) authors many primary membranes.

**Why Survey Corps says "post-11."** Our home universe's floor is eleven — three extended dimensions, seven [compact](/star-rangers/glossary/compact-dimensions/), one of time — and the manifold begins where that floor ends, so the Corps has always written it as the **post-11 manifold**. Both terms are canonical: "post-11" is the working shorthand and remains correct in Survey Corps usage, "Common Manifold" is the formal name.

The shorthand is parochial by construction, and knowingly so. Eleven is a local fact rather than a cosmic constant; analysts seated in a universe with a different floor would count from their own number and mean this same structure. The Corps names it from where it happens to be standing — the same habit that gave the setting three names for one boundary phenomenon (see [Overfold](/star-rangers/glossary/overfold/)).

That parochialism has one operational edge, and it is not academic: instruments calibrated to eleven dimensions cannot resolve a boundary event whose far side has a different floor. Survey Corps grades those as a distinct hazard class from ordinary membrane proximity.
