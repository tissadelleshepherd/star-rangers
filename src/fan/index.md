<h1 class="page-title">A Fan Work</h1>
<p class="page-intro">
  This is a fan fork of <em>Fian Ilchruinne</em> — unofficial, non-commercial, and not the
  record. Everything of the story here is Dermot R. Cochran's, carried under the
  <a href="/star-rangers/about/">licence's</a> Fan Works Policy and changed in no way. One
  thing in this repository is not his, and this page exists to say what it is and who says so.
</p>

<h2 id="the-picture">The picture</h2>

<figure class="fan-work">
  <img src="/star-rangers/images/IMG_1786938939320.jpg"
       alt="A woman with long curly red hair walking barefoot towards the viewer along wet sand at sunset, in a black leather jacket open over a pale satin blouse and a short black skirt, the low sun on the water behind her and two distant figures further down the beach" />
  <figcaption>
    <strong>Shore leave.</strong> Generated, 17 August 2026, and uploaded here uncaptioned.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

<p>
  The main site noticed it, and described it exactly: <em>an uncaptioned, AI-generated picture
  of a red-haired woman walking a beach at sunset</em>. It read the obvious reading —
  <a href="/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/">Tissadelle Shepherd</a>, off duty —
  and then declined to make it, on the grounds that the identification was the maker's and not
  the record's. That is the right call for a record to make, and it leaves a sentence with
  nobody to say it.
</p>
<p>
  So: it is her. That is who it was meant to be when it was made, and the caption it never had
  is the one above. Nothing follows from this. The record is not bound by a fan's reading of a
  picture a fan generated, and it should not be — which is the whole reason the sentence is on
  this page and not on that one.
</p>

<h2 id="what-it-is-not">What it is not</h2>

<p>
  It is not evidence. The Archive holds no image of any officer's leave, there is no beach in
  the corpus that this is, and nothing here establishes what Shepherd looks like, where she
  spent a day off, or whether she owns a leather jacket. The record's own portrait of her is on
  <a href="/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/">her page</a>, and where the two
  disagree, hers is the one that counts.
</p>
<p>
  It is also not a picture of anybody. It was made by a third-party generator from a text
  prompt that no longer exists, in one pass, with no reference and no sitter; the generator's
  watermark sat in the bottom-left corner until it was cropped away on 15 October 2026. The
  main site's standing formula for everything in this category is <em>referenced, not
  endorsed</em>: a link is a pointer, not approval. Being noted there confers nothing, and
  neither does being described here.
</p>

<h2 id="why-a-fork">Why a fork at all</h2>

<p>
  Because the licence says you may. Fan fiction clones of the site are explicitly allowed,
  non-commercially and clearly labelled unofficial, and the
  <a href="/star-rangers/forking/">Forking This Site</a> guide is written for exactly this —
  the engine is MIT, the story is not, and the two are kept apart in the directory tree so the
  line is easy to see and hard to cross by accident.
</p>
<p>
  This fork takes the record's updates as they come and adapts to them. It does not send
  anything back, except where something is plainly broken in both places. What grows here grows
  on this side of the line, and is marked, as this page is, as nobody's but its maker's.
</p>
