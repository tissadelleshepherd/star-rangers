Not everything that crosses the Interval registers on an instrument. Some of it registers on a sleeper.

## The Channel

Adjacent membranes interact in subtle ways through the Etheric layer, and the carrier is already on the record: the [trans-concordant constraint field](/star-rangers/lore/concordant-membranes/) — the manifold band that carries each membrane's archetypal templates — lets a neighbour's templates become locally detectable wherever the [Interval](/star-rangers/glossary/interval/) narrows. Instruments read that as Membrane Resonance and Intermembrane Bleed. What this entry adds is the second kind of detector.

A mind is a Material-layer process with [lawful Etheric coupling](/star-rangers/lore/physics-comparison/), and dreaming is the state in which that coupling runs least supervised — the same state meaning-field pressure already registers in. So near a narrowed Interval, a dreaming or deeply unfocused mind can register bled template structure from the membrane next door, experienced as dreams and visions whose architecture is not local: geometries with the wrong grammar, weather obeying another season's logic, a sense of rooms built to proportions no one here sits inside. The sleeper is not travelling and nothing is visiting. The neighbourhood is simply audible from a bed close enough to the fence.

## Legibility: The Archecluster Condition

Whether such a dream carries *content* or only strangeness is an [archecluster](/star-rangers/glossary/archecluster/)-scoped fact. Templates are readable as imagery only against a shared archetype vocabulary — so where the two membranes lie in one archecluster, the neighbour's structure arrives as something a dream can *render*: places, weathers, figures, wrongness with a shape. Across an archecluster boundary the bleed is still measurable as field structure, but the dreaming mind has nothing to read it *as*; the accounts on record from such sites describe formless pressure — a dream about nothing, remembered mainly as weight.

## The Shared-Telearch Condition

The channel runs strongest where one [Telearch](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/) holds continuity across both membranes, within the same archecluster. This is doctrine restated, not a new power: continuity is what a Telearch substantially *is*, and its mandate spans multiple Concordants. Two membranes held by one continuity office have their template-fields kept mutually coherent — and coherence is the difference between a channel that carries structure and one that carries hiss. Where the record's clearest dream-adjacency accounts cluster, the working expectation is a shared continuity office behind the pair; where the offices differ, the accounts thin toward the formless kind even inside one cluster.

## What This Is Not

- **Not communication.** There is no addressing, no reply channel, and no fidelity: every attempt to use dream-adjacency as signalling has failed at the first step, because nothing about the channel selects a recipient or preserves a message. A dream is not a dispatch, and a dream is never evidence about who sent it — nothing did.
- **Not shared.** No two sleepers have the same dream, and no instrument, rig or discipline puts one person's dream in another's sleep; the record holds no case and no mechanism. The one way a dream crosses from one mind to another is the oldest one: it is made into something for a waking person — told, drawn, written, sung. That is a work, with an author and a date, weighed the way any work is, and it is what art and creative fiction have always been for. It is not a channel, and the dream arrives in it as the author's account, never as the dream.
- **Not transit.** What crosses is template structure, never matter and never a mind. No one has ever been *in* another membrane by dreaming of it.
- **Not predation.** This entry describes weather, not visitors. A presence in a dream that engages — bargains, asks, answers, feeds — is not template bleed, and belongs to the classifications the record keeps for [things that engage](/star-rangers/lore/meta-dimensional-beings/).
- **Not a verdict on any recorded vision.** A mechanism being available assigns no particular account to it. The record's oldest visions at thin places remain exactly as resolved as they were — the [Aoife scholarship](/star-rangers/lore/saint-aoife/) is the worked example of the record declining to rule on a witness who declined to rule on herself.

## Scope

Filed per [The Scope of Physical Law](/star-rangers/lore/the-scope-of-physical-law/): the carrier is manifold structure, formcluster-scoped; legibility is archecluster-scoped, since shared templates are what make a neighbour's structure readable; and the strength of any given channel is a pairwise fact about two membranes and the continuity office that holds them, which is as narrow as a scope gets while still crossing an Interval.
