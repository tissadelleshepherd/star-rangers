**A sett** is the pattern a tartan repeats: the order and width of the coloured threads, the same in the warp as in the weft, so that the stripes cross into squares. On [Aethelrock](/star-rangers/lore/planets/aethelrock/) the cold-weather cloth is tartan-woven, and each of the five landholding clans weaves its own sett. They are in daily use, not kept for ceremony: the scarf at a clan head's throat on a quayside at first light is the same cloth a herder wears walking the grazing marches.

Wearing one is a choice and a custom, never a uniform. Nothing obliges a member of a clan to wear its sett, nobody is turned away for wearing another's, and a coat in plain weatherproof cloth with the sett only at the collar or the scarf is the commonest way to see it. The cloth carries the clan; the technology under it stays visible, as everything on Aethelrock does.

## The five setts

- **Dubhghlas**, holders of the world's most arable ground: moss green and brown.
- **Aonghas**, of the eastern granite highlands and the mining claims: dark red and grey.
- **Bryneth**, of the northern high country: dark blue and black.
- **Ó Ceallaigh**: sea green and grey.
- **Ceridwen**, holders of the fold-relay station: teal and ochre.

The record gives the colours and does not say what, if anything, they mean to the clans who wear them. [Clan Avalon](/star-rangers/lore/planets/aethelrock/#clan-avalon-a-fellowship-without-ground), the cross-clan bardic fellowship that holds no valley and no Ridgemoot seat, has no sett on record; its members come from all five clans.

## Off the world, and in the Corps

Aethelrock's setts are the only patterned clan cloth the record holds on any world. An Aethelrock Ranger does not wear a sett on duty, because the charter's neutrality clause keeps every mark of origin off the uniform ([Rank, Insignia, and the Ranger Uniform](/star-rangers/lore/rank-insignia-and-uniform/)). Off duty, the scarf goes back on.
