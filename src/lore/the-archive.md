The Archive is the record, and the institution that keeps it. Every page of lore and of the glossary is written in its voice; every document in the [Codex](/star-rangers/codex/) sits on one of its shelves or is filed by it from a shelf it does not own. It is older and larger than the [Star Rangers](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/), and no corps owns it: the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) keeps an Archive Branch, and the branch is named for the institution it serves rather than the other way around. What the branch undertakes on the Corps' behalf is that whatever a surveyor logs will still be legible, attributable and versioned when the surveyor is not.

This page gathers what the record already says of itself, from the pages where it says it. It adds nothing, which is the one way an archive may write its own entry.

## Where It Keeps Things

The record keeps its documents where the world keeps them. Its working library is the **Survey Archive at Threshold Station**, kept under the [Senior Archivist](/star-rangers/characters/sen/): the general holdings, the doctrinal working-paper series, the verification case files, and the reconciliation binders beside the Dock Seven incident file. The rest is held where it was made and filed from there — the station's own offices, the [Orbital Habitats Compact](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/)'s registry of standing instruments, the homeworld's institutional archives, the [Celtic Union](/star-rangers/lore/celtic-union-of-planets/)'s cultural holdings, a hill's circulating pages — and each entry's shelf-mark says which. A recording that survives only by being passed post to post is a holding too, and is filed as one.

A document is filed in its author's own notation, with the UCSD conversion noted beside it: [the original is the document; the conversion is the courtesy](/star-rangers/lore/timekeeping-and-the-common-record/). A survey's transit lag goes on its cover, so that [the local record outranks the file](/star-rangers/lore/weather-on-other-worlds/) as doctrine rather than as apology, and [every coastline measurement carries the ruler that took it](/star-rangers/lore/coasts-and-other-edges/).

## How a Claim Becomes a Holding

The Archive believes nothing at the door. Everything arrives as a claim, carrying its source on its face or not coming in, and the door's whole function is to record that something was claimed, by whom, from where, on what date — perfectly, and without opinion. That is the first article of [the Verification Doctrine](/star-rangers/codex/the-verification-doctrine/), posted at every desk, and the rest follows from it: standing is explicit and travels with the holding (*received*, *admitted*, *corroborated*, *settled*, *disputed*, *superseded*); evidence moves standing and nothing else does; and the shelf appends and never overwrites. There is no rung called *deleted*. A superseded holding is a holding about what the record used to hold, and the record of its own errors is the most consulted shelf in the building.

The same discipline runs, in other uniforms, through [the enrolled text](/star-rangers/lore/the-enrolled-text/) of a constitution and the commissioning of a machine; the Senior Archivist has argued that [the three are one discipline](/star-rangers/codex/three-disciplines-of-the-record/), and that it is older than any of them.

## What It Marks, and What It Grades

Where the record stops, it says so. [Mark the absence; do not explain it](/star-rangers/codex/marked-absences/): *the file is thin* commits the record to nothing but the truth, while *the file was lost in the evacuation* commits it to an evacuation, a loss and a cause, none verified. A marked absence is itself information — a reader who meets one knows the gap is real, that someone competent has looked, and that looking is not finished. The absences are not all one kind, and the record says which: sealed, unsettled, live, provisional, unverifiable.

Where the record holds something, it says what the holding has survived. [A claim stands on what it has beaten, not on what it fits](/star-rangers/codex/standing-claims/), and the grade belongs on the entry rather than in the reader's head: *verified* for a reading whose rivals were named and tried, *consistent* for one that fits while untried rivals fit equally, and the rest in their places. None of the gradings is a reproach. The error they catch is never that a reading is believed on partial evidence, which is ordinary; it is that a reading is filed one rung above what it has survived.

## Its Voice

The Archive writes in the [canonical terms](/star-rangers/lore/glossary/canonical-glossary-and-migration-guide/), and where a term has drifted it keeps the migration on record rather than correcting the past silently. It dates in UCSD and leaves the author's own date beside the conversion. It files every physical claim [at the scope it is a fact about](/star-rangers/lore/the-scope-of-physical-law/) and no wider. It renders other calendars, other grammars and other peoples' names into its own register as translation, and says so, and a people whose own forms it keeps untranslated is making a statement by it.

It does not speculate: what it has not verified it files as unverified, what a source conjectures it files as that source's conjecture, and the fiction in its holdings it files as fiction, told and labelled, with no quarrel and no standing.

Two things its voice does not do. It holds no classification under another tradition's words — it carries a devotional idiom where a source uses one and declines the creed, and it keeps the tradition's own vocabulary in the tradition's mouth, in the Codex, attributed. And where the record holds an argument, [it holds both sides and the cases where the cost came due, and not a verdict](/star-rangers/lore/what-nobody-certifies/).

## What It Is Not

It is not the Corps' record, though the Corps keeps a branch of it. It is not a classifier: [the Sentinel sat in the archive for generations](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) as unclassifiable EM noise because every instrument asked whether the signal matched a known protocol and none asked whether it had phrasing — the record keeps that humiliation deliberately, as the case every Survey school now teaches. And it is not a guide that is sometimes wrong and leaves the wrong entry standing. Where it was wrong, the superseded reading sits beside the corrected one, dated and signed, with the dates between them on the shelf; and where it does not know, it says less rather than guessing. What it refuses to carry at all, it [lists in its own voice](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/).

## What the Record Has Not Established

The Archive has not established when it began, and has not been asked to. It is older than the Corps, and the Corps' founding is dated; its own is not, and the Senior Archivist's doctrine on absences would apply to any date this page offered. Nor has it established whether every holding it files from — the Compact's registry, the homeworld's archives, the Union's — answers to one authority or only to one practice. The practice is what the record can show, and this page shows it. The rest is marked.
