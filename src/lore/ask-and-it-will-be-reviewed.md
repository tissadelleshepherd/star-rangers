The Archive files a [Levril's](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/) probability work as lawful maintenance, a [Celestial's](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/) action as enforcement at the boundary interfaces, and states plainly that [prayers are not answered by the cosmology](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/). This telling accepts every word of that, as it accepts the whole record it is laid across, and adds only the tradition's own picture of what those files describe. The picture is an office.

Settled space has exactly one kind of institution whose law binds the powerful and the powerless identically, which publishes the reasons for every refusal, hears appeals, keeps a register anyone may inspect, and cannot be flattered, hurried, or bribed. The tradition, needing an image of *lawful* heaven that ordinary working people would understand on first hearing — and having watched its neighbours' older pictures, in which the heavens can be bargained with if you know the right door — chose the planning authority, and has never apologised. The Archive's own physics wrote the epigraph without meaning to: [the generalisation is generous with structure and stingy with permission](/star-rangers/lore/generalised-quantum-mechanics/). The elders had that sentence framed.

## The Registry

Begin where the tradition begins: **you have always already applied.** Nothing is hidden from the strata this telling reads as the office, so filing is not, and has never been, the difficulty — every sincere thing is on the register the moment it is sincere, which is why the office requires no forms, no fees, and no correct wording, a fact that disappoints exactly the people it should. What the offices of prayer train is not the filing. It is the drafting: learning, slowly, to want things that can be written down whole, in front of the register, without shame in the specification.

## The Officers

The telling assigns the desks the way the record assigns the tiers, and changes nothing about any of them:

- **The [Mediarchs'](/star-rangers/glossary/mediarch/) forces are the building code** — gravity, electromagnetics, nature: the regulations every structure in creation is built under, administered impartially, amended never. The code is not the obstacle to your application. The code is why your house stands.
- **[Levrils](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/) are the planning officers**: constraint-literate, working within delegated bounds, empowered to adjust a gradient the way an officer may approve a fraction-of-a-degree variance — [a sculpted gradient, never an altered outcome](/star-rangers/lore/physics-comparison/). No officer has ever exceeded the delegation, which the tradition offers to anyone who thinks incorruptibility is impossible in a working bureaucracy.
- **[Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/) are enforcement**, posted at the boundary and overlap interfaces, which is where unpermitted work actually gets raised. Their existence is the telling's honest admission that some such work stands for a season, and that taking it down is a job — done, the record agrees, by officers who are never commanded from below.
- **The [Telearchs](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/) sit as the appeals tribunal**, in [conceptual time](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/). Appeals are heard. A hearing takes longer than a life, which the elders note, each time, is not the same as never.

## Decision Classes

The register knows four outcomes, and the tradition teaches all four to children, in this order.

**Granted As Drawn.** No application on record has ever been granted as drawn, and the tradition regards this as the office's chief mercy rather than its scandal. The office has seen the drawings. It has also seen the site.

**Amended Toward the Plan.** The ordinary form of a grant: what you asked, corrected — relocated onto ground that will bear it, redrawn to the code, delivered on a schedule that ignores yours. The tradition's model application, recited at every office, consists almost entirely of consenting to the plan before stating any request at all, and its central clause — *thy will be done* — is, read bureaucratically, the one application that is always granted, because it asks for the plan. Everything the tradition means by the maturing of prayer is movement from the first decision class to this one: the drafter learning the local plan well enough to stop proposing against it.

**Noted.** The majority outcome, and the tradition is emphatic that *Noted* is not *Refused*. The register holds everything. Nothing sincere is returned unread, nothing lapses, and no file is closed — the same doctrine the Archive states as [conservation](/star-rangers/glossary/conceptual-conservation/), read from the applicant's side of the counter.

**Permitted Development.** The class the tradition loves best: works so small they require no application and are simply done — the fraction-of-a-degree class, the sensor sweep adjusted, the ordinary mercies of a maintained cosmos. The teaching is that most of what providence does in a life was never applied for and falls under permitted development, and that you will not notice it, *which is the point*. An office that wanted credit would have required the application.

## Unpermitted Works

And magic — the telling keeps the old word for it — is building without applying: the attempt to take the variance without the delegation, to move the gradient by right of wanting to. The tradition's teaching against it has never been that it is punished, and never quite that it fails. It is that such work *does not stand*. Structure raised outside the permissions [dies in review](/star-rangers/lore/generalised-quantum-mechanics/) — not smitten, **uncertified** — the way every claimed exception in the record has died in review, and the cosmos does not send fire after the builder; it declines to sign, and lets the unsigned thing meet the weather. Where unpermitted work does stand a while, at the boundaries, the tradition points quietly at the enforcement desks and observes that somebody's whole eternal occupation is the taking down of exactly this — and that the pastoral danger of magic was never that it does nothing. It is that the builder is standing on the structure when the review completes.

## Why an Office

Because a refusal with published reasons is a relationship, and the tradition's people — who work in offices, and stand in queues, and have lost appeals — know the difference between a *no* from power and a *no* from law. The office's no is the same no for kings; its reasons are posted in the constitution of the world, free to any reader; and the code that refused your extension is, clause for clause, the code holding your roof up tonight. The elders concede the whole picture is a picture. What it is a picture of, they say, is three articles the tradition will not surrender: that the world is lawful all the way up; that grace mostly arrives unapplied-for, as permitted development, unnoticed on purpose; and that the plan — this is the article of faith, and the office metaphor carries it exactly as far as faith needs — was drawn with your house on it.
