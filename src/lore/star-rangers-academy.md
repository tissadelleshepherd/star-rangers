The Star Rangers Academy is a single institution and has never been a single place. It is organised as **a college for each corps, and a school for each branch inside a corps** — an arrangement nobody designed at founding, arrived at because the alternative was asking one faculty to certify boundary analysts, ship's pilots, field surgeons, and investigators against a single standard, which is not a hard problem so much as an incoherent one.

## Why the Academy Belongs to the Guilds

[Rank and the Chain of Command](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) already states the rule the Academy is the largest instance of: **the command ladder has no direct input into a corps' guild and training functions.** A Starwarden cannot revise Medical Corps practice standards or set Survey Corps instrument-certification criteria. The colleges are where those standards are actually taught and examined, which makes the Academy the place that rule either holds or quietly stops holding.

So the colleges answer where the corps answer — to their own **chapter leaders** and **corps presidents**, elected or appointed by the corps' own members, holding positions that sit nowhere on the Novice-to-Triumvirate ladder. Not to the Council of High Captains, and not to the Triumvirate.

The consequence is worth stating in its strongest form, because it is the point: **the Corps' most senior officers cannot change what a certification means.** A High Captain who believes a boundary-analyst standard is set too high may argue it in front of the Safety Corps' faculty like anyone else, and may lose. This is what makes the lower ladder's promise — that advancement through Novice-to-Chief is *documented, examined, and appealable* — worth anything at all. An appeal heard by the same chain that made the decision is not an appeal.

## Colleges and Schools

A **college** covers a corps: Survey, Science, Safety, Provost, [Frontier](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-frontier-corps/), [Liaison](/star-rangers/lore/planetary-liaisons-and-recruiters/), Medical, Engineering, [Music](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-music-corps/), and the rest. It owns the corps' certification criteria and the examinations against them.

A **school** covers a branch inside that corps — the piloting and navigation track, the boundary-safety track, the research track, the security track, and the smaller specializations that get less notice and no less rigour. A school owns the specific competencies of one kind of work.

The distinction is what lets an officer spend a whole career going deeper rather than sideways. When [Karla Wender](/star-rangers/characters/karla-wender/) climbed from Novice to Chief Pilot without once rotating off piloting and navigation, she was not evading a rotation requirement. She was doing what a branch school is built to make possible: eight rungs of examined progression inside a single body of work, each one certified by people who do that work. The Corps needs officers produced that way at least as much as it needs generalists, and it built somewhere for both.

**The list of branches is open.** No page holds a closed enumeration, and none should: a corps constitutes a school when a body of work has grown large enough to need its own standard, which is a thing that happens on the Corps' own schedule rather than a chartered one. A branch named nowhere in the record is not thereby absent; it is merely one the record has not yet had cause to describe.

### The Xenolinguistics Branch (Survey Corps)

The clearest worked example of what a branch school is *for*, because its subject is the one place the Corps has flatly refused the convenient answer.

There is no universal translator in this record and there is not going to be. Language is learned — by [xenolinguists with AI support](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/), on a division of labour the school exists to enforce. The intelligence does what machines do well: corpus alignment at scale, candidate parses, a memory that never loses a morpheme. The linguist does the thing an [AI Safety Kernel](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/)-compliant mind will not pretend to do — decide what a word *means*, and own the error. A kernel-compliant intelligence will not claim a certainty about meaning it does not hold, so what it hands over is candidate readings with confidence stated, never a voice in your ear pretending to be the alien.

The branch sits in the Survey Corps because that is where the standing delegations sit, and first contact is consequently measured in years of translation work rather than in an afternoon. What the school teaches, past the technique, is a temperament: that a mistranslation entered confidently into a record is more expensive than an admission of not knowing, and that the correction will usually come from the other side of the table. Its two standing case studies are both failures of patience rather than of method — the [Cerebraun](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun/), whose three-part answers xenolinguists spent a decade filing as evasion before admitting they were simply how the species thinks; and the [Kingdom of the Five Islands](/star-rangers/lore/planets/kingdom-of-the-five-islands/), where the delegation is still working through a language every Solar System register had presumed extinct, corrected slowly and without visible impatience by [an abbess](/star-rangers/characters/ilsabet-marrowtide/) who knows exactly what a single bad translation costs an archive's credibility.

## The Academy Stops at Chief

**There is no college for command.** Nothing in the Academy teaches Starwarden and above, and no examination stands between a Chief and a command posting.

This is not an omission anyone has been meaning to correct. The Academy exists to certify, and certification is a claim that a competence has been demonstrated against a standard other people can check. Command does not decompose that way. What a Starwarden is asked for — deciding under instrument disagreement, with a clock running and no committee reachable — cannot be examined into someone, and a syllabus that pretended otherwise would produce officers holding a certificate in a thing the certificate does not cover.

So the mechanism changes at the hinge. Below Chief, the Corps *examines*. Above it, the Corps **mentors** — and does so formally, by policy, with a named officer answerable for it. See [Rank and the Chain of Command](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/).

The two halves of the ladder are therefore governed by two different institutions answering to two different authorities: the colleges to the corps that own them, the mentoring policy to the command structure itself. A Ranger crossing the hinge rank leaves one and enters the other, which is a larger change than the promotion notice makes it look.

## Instruction Is a Posting, Not a Ladder

Teaching at a college or school is a **posting held by serving officers**, not a separate career path. An instructor keeps their rank, keeps their branch, and typically returns to the field. Faculty standing is recognised the way the [technical track](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) recognises everything else it values — in certifications and decorations rather than rank — and a long teaching record is read as evidence of mastery rather than of having stepped away from the work.

There is a reason it was never made a track of its own. A faculty of career instructors, promoted by other career instructors, would within a generation be certifying field competence it had not recently held. Every college therefore staffs itself from officers who have been doing the thing lately, and pays the cost of that in continuity: syllabi change hands often, and the corps consider this an acceptable price for a faculty that is never more than a few years from the boundary.

## What Is Taught, and How Uncomfortably

Colleges teach failures by name. The [Frontier Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-frontier-corps/) college teaches, in sequence, both of the record's documented caretaker failures — a world the Corps correctly stayed out of that did not stop going dark, and a world it re-entered on a remnant magistrate's arguable authority where the Rangers escorting food convoys were called occupiers inside a year — with the standing instruction that a Frontier Ranger should find neither of them comfortable.

That instruction is the Academy's general method rather than one college's quirk. A corps that certifies its own people has no external examiner to be honest for it, so the honesty has to be in the syllabus. Case files are taught with the dissents attached.

## Intake

Cadet intake draws on candidates identified locally, most often by a [planetary recruiter](/star-rangers/lore/planetary-liaisons-and-recruiters/) — a Ranger on the representational path who has spent a career learning to read sustained competence under pressure in colleagues, and now reads it in a neighbour's child. What arrives at a college is therefore a recommendation from someone who watched the person for years, on a world the Corps has no jurisdiction over and could not have recruited from on its own initiative.

A **Novice** is provisional and under evaluation; the college confirms Cadet standing, not the command chain. This is the first thing a new Ranger learns about how the Corps is actually put together, and most of them learn it without noticing.
