The record recognises minds. It declines to define them, and the two facts are stated together because the second is what keeps the first honest.

Everything the record holds about sapience in general is already on file at its own tiers: [that mind is a permitted kind of thing at all, independent of what carries it, and that no template anywhere specifies a shape for the carrier](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/). What this page adds is the operational half — what the Corps and the Concord actually do with a question of the form *is this a person?* — and the first thing they do is refuse a checklist. Not out of modesty. Out of the [documented failure mode](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/): a definition of sapience written by one species is an instrument, and an instrument answers the question it was built from. Every criterion humans have ever proposed turns out, on inspection, to be a human trait promoted to a law — the observer's own shape, mistaken for the thing observed. The record keeps the list of these, deliberately, as the page's working content.

## The Markers That Do Not Settle It

Each of the following has been proposed, somewhere in the record's history, as *the* mark of sapience. Each has at least one counterexample already on file, which is why none of them is doctrine and all of them are teaching material.

**Language shaped like ours.** Recursive, syntax-heavy, serial, acoustic-or-written — that is a description of human language, not of communication. The record holds peoples whose speech runs [in percussion and water-borne chemistry](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/), and its archives held one such people's traffic filed as *biological noise* for want of a protocol match. A signal is not less a sentence for arriving in a register no human grammar anticipates.

**Artifacts.** A technology [need not look like one](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/), and a people [need not build much at all](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) — an oral record in cultural memory leaves a surface survey nothing to photograph, and secreted mineral architecture reads as geology to every instrument that asks about chemistry. Absence of visible industry is a fact about visibility.

**One self per body.** Concord personhood standards settled this at home before any survey needed it abroad: [a body may carry more people than the form has boxes for](/star-rangers/glossary/plural-minds/), and the record's wider position is that [personhood need not even resolve to individuals](/star-rangers/lore/the-shape-of-a-life/) — persons may be counted otherwise. A survey that goes looking for the bounded single self is looking for a human arrangement, not for mind.

**A familiar substrate.** [Made minds are minded the way anyone is minded](/star-rangers/lore/made-minds-and-the-ai-safety-archetype/) — there is no separate template for artificial sapience, and there is none for any particular chemistry either: [no biosphere owes the survey nucleic acids, cells, or one shared molecule](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/). Sapience is indifferent to what carries it, and an assessor who is not indifferent has stopped assessing the mind.

**A familiar tempo.** A [cold chemistry runs deliberate](/star-rangers/lore/ilveth/); a [light-limited metabolism cannot force an afternoon](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/); a people may deliberate across seasons and answer once. Speed of response measures metabolism. It was never measuring thought.

**Our kind of cleverness.** Symbolic abstraction, formal mathematics, cumulative external record — these are things humans are good at, and [the record refuses the ladder that would make anyone's strengths the scale](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/). A mind may hold, as ordinary competence, capacities no human approaches, while lacking the ones a human test would examine first. Capability is a shape. So is mind.

## What the Record Does Instead

Recognition, not measurement: **per lineage, through communication, taken as long as it takes.** The [Survey Corps trains every branch](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) that sapience might not be physically obvious until after communication is well established, and the [Frontier Transformation Protocols](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/) set **no sapience threshold** and no window after which the question may be declared closed — the extended surveys exist precisely to fund the patience the method needs. What well-established communication demonstrates is not a score on anyone's scale but the thing itself, encountered: something on the far side of the exchange that models, intends, answers, and can be *wrong about you in ways that require it to have a view of you*. The determinations, once made, are findings — the record tells persons from [animals](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/) firmly, and a refusal to keep a checklist has never once been a refusal to keep the distinction. Kinship is a finding about descent; personhood is a finding about the mind; and the mind is found by meeting it — the one instrument in the survey kit that both parties shape, which is exactly what disqualifies every instrument only one of them built.
