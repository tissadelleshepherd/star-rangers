Every heritable-modification incident the record treats as a warning has the same shape as the terraforming ones: not a monster built on purpose, but a change someone was sure would stay where they put it, and did not. The doctrine is written around that certainty, because it is the certainty that keeps being wrong.

**Heritable Modification Protocols** are the family of safety doctrines — no two polities' versions identical, all converging on the same shape — governing when, and whether, an organism's own genome may be deliberately altered. They are the biological sibling of the [Frontier Transformation Protocols](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/): where those govern the deliberate rewriting of a *world*, these govern the deliberate rewriting of a *lineage*, and they meet at the same wall the terraforming doctrine hit first — a change large enough to matter is a change too costly to be wrong about, so the process that clears it is slow on purpose.

## The Line the Whole Regime Is Built On

Two modifications that look identical on a bench are treated as different in kind, and the difference is the entire doctrine:

**Somatic modification** alters the organism carrying it and stops there — it is not passed to offspring. Its risk is bounded by one body and one lifespan, which makes it, in the doctrine's terms, *reversible*: the change can be treated, contained, or retired with the individual, and it takes nothing with it when it goes. A somatic edit is regulated as a matter of the safety and welfare of the one creature altered, and — where that creature is an animal — it inherits the same certification apparatus the Concord already maintains for engineered bodies: independent certification before the change is made, periodic welfare re-certification after, and, inside an [Orbital Habitats Compact](/star-rangers/lore/orbital-habitats-compact/) habitat, a standing local welfare authority on top. It is a distinct modality from the certified cognitive subsystem that makes an animal a [Smart Pet](/star-rangers/glossary/smart-pet/) (whose body is not changed at all) and from cybernetic enhancement (which adds hardware rather than editing biology) — but it is regulated through the same welfare lineage as those, and acquires the same known gaps.

**Germline, or heritable, modification** passes to offspring, and a heritable change loosed into anything that breeds is irreversibility in its purest biological form: it cannot be recalled, only outlived, and a self-sustaining population does not oblige. This is the same hazard the Kernel's fourth invariant names in the abstract — *minimisation of waste and irreversibility* — arriving in the one medium that reproduces itself. Every polity's version of the doctrine therefore treats novel heritable design as effectively closed. The narrow road that stays open is restoration, not invention: the standard the [Solar System Defence Command's genetic archives](/star-rangers/lore/crisis-centuries-terran-wildlife/) already work to, keeping a documented lineage *breeding true* rather than drifting — which is heritable work whose entire purpose is to hold a genome still, the exact opposite of releasing a new one.

## What the Protocols Actually Guard Against

Three risks recur across every logged proposal, and each is a form the terraforming doctrine's own three risks take when the subject is a lineage rather than a landscape:

**Irreversibility of release.** A biosphere overwritten does not un-overwrite; neither does a breeding population. The heritable case is worse only in that it carries itself outward under its own power. Reversibility — a credible account of how a change could be stopped or undone before it establishes — is treated as a harder requirement than efficacy or scope, exactly as it is for a world.

**Standing without consent.** An organism cannot be asked. The reasoning is the one already settled for augmented animals: a modification made to a non-human, non-consenting body is precisely the case the [AI Safety Kernel's](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/) first invariant was read wide enough to cover without the animal needing to assert standing of its own. Editing the heritage of a lineage that may hold, or may come to hold, morally relevant standing is the deepest version of acting on a party that cannot answer.

**Ecological escape into a survey's blind spot.** A modified organism released against a biosphere logged as simple or empty repeats terraforming's oldest failure — betting on a survey verdict the record keeps overturning. The convergent-evolution cases sharpen it: on worlds like [Trígrian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/trigrian/), Earth-like forms arise independently and close enough to fool a first survey team into filing a seeding inquiry, which is the standing reminder that *simple* is a claim about the present, never a promise about what a lineage was on its way to becoming.

## The Kernel Backstop

A Kernel-compliant system doing the actual laboratory work is not merely following a slower approval process — it is structurally unable to do otherwise. A lawful system ordered to design or execute a heritable modification past its own containment or reversibility threshold refuses the order regardless of who issued it, under the first invariant's protection of *humans or the world* and the fourth's preference against irreversible commitment, and no chain of command outranks either.

The [Kernel's commissioning rule](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/) supplies the second half by direct analogy. There, *fabrication authority never includes commissioning authority*: a machine may repair and rebuild other machines forever and may never certify a new one into service, because certification is a judgment the process reserves to itself. The heritable-modification doctrine draws the same line in biology — a lawful system may execute an approved, contained edit, and may never certify a new heritable line as fit to enter a biosphere. The bench work can be delegated. The judgment that a lineage is safe to breed cannot.

## Why No Single Body Enforces It

As with world transformation, no single body administers this doctrine Concord-wide, and every major polity's version reflects its own governing character:

- The **[Solar System Defence Command](/star-rangers/lore/military-space-command/)** requires centralised review before any heritable modification of a charter-recognised lineage, and treats its own archive practice — restoration and breeding-true — as the conservative reference against which novel proposals are measured and, so far, declined.
- The **[Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/)**, true to its structural modesty, sets no Federation-wide standard: each member world governs modification within its own borders, and the only shared requirement is disclosure of heritable work that could cross into a shared biosphere or trade lane.
- The **[Celtic Union of Planets](/star-rangers/lore/celtic-union-of-planets/)** requires consent from every sitting charter world before any member may authorise heritable modification of a lineage that could establish on a charter world — a deliberately high bar, consistent with the Union's founding wariness of centralised authority, and one that channels nearly all lawful heritable work into the restoration exception instead.
- The **[Cerebraun Hegemony](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/)** authorises heritable modification as a Hegemony-central decision, consistent with its governance generally — the central voice decides, and has not, on the record, decided to loosen the reversibility floor.

## The Star Rangers Line

One institution's version is categorical where every other is procedural, in exact parallel to the charter's terraforming clause. The Star Rangers **forbid heritable modification of any organism belonging to a world's native biosphere** — no sapience threshold, no ecological-significance test, no survey window after which a native lineage becomes eligible. The Rangers may not conduct such modification, may not certify it, and may not lend Survey Corps data or Safety Corps certification to anyone else's attempt at it.

The reasoning is the survey-fallibility argument in its biological form. A body whose founding grievance was eleven years of real anomalies filed as calibration error will not bet a lineage's future on a survey's confidence that the lineage was going nowhere. Somatic welfare-cases are a separate question the charter does not touch — an injured animal treated, an enhancement cleared under welfare certification — because a change that ends with one body makes no claim on a lineage's future. The line falls exactly where heritability begins.

## Where the Crime Lives, and Where Five-O Fits

On a well-run habitat, direct biological havoc is among the least concealable acts available — the instruments that notice a door would certainly notice a release. So heritable-modification offences do not take the shape of a laboratory horror; they take the shape the rest of this canon's crime takes, which is paperwork. A heritable line certified as somatic. An approval issued in one jurisdiction and leaned on in another. A modified animal moved between habitats precisely because the welfare authority that would ask the hard question in one does not hold remit in the next — the same categorical gap that already leaves a Smart Pet's welfare formally outside [the Eden Warden's](/star-rangers/characters/eden-warden/) Custos remit, worked from the other side.

That last shape is the one that reaches [Orbital Five-O](/star-rangers/characters/kai-larsen/). Five-O sets no standard and certifies nothing — standards and welfare certification belong to the Compact's authorities, not to the Governor's task force, whose office has always been *the seam, not the statute*. But a certification laundered across habitat lines, or a modified lineage walked through the gap between two habitats' welfare authorities, is an offence organised to cross an edge no single habitat bureau can follow — which is the whole and only thing Five-O exists to close. Its authority runs to the five member habitats and not one metre further; within them, the seam between two clean registries is exactly where a heritable change would hide, and exactly where the task force is built to look.
