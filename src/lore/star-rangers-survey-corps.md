The Survey Corps collects. That sentence is quoted on more pages of this record than any other institutional fact in it, usually by way of explaining what somebody *else* does — [the Science Corps explains what was collected](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-science-corps/), the [Safety Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-safety-corps/) investigates what went wrong with it, a station manager decides whether an anomaly in it goes upstream. This page is about the collecting.

The Survey Corps is a specialist corps of the [Star Rangers](/star-rangers/lore/formation-of-star-rangers/), governed as a guild like all of them — its instrument-certification criteria, training standards and protocols are its own, and [no command rank can revise them](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/). Its work runs from [boundary monitoring at Threshold-class stations](/star-rangers/codex/survey-corps-protocol/) to planetary survey to the standing delegations through which the Corps meets other peoples. What holds it together as one corps is a single discipline applied to very different subjects: **observe under protocol, log with provenance, and claim no more than the instrument showed.**

## A School per Branch

[The Academy](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-academy/) is organised as a college per corps and a school per branch, and a corps constitutes a school when a body of work has grown large enough to need its own standard. The Survey Corps has constituted a good many, which is what a mandate as wide as *everything observable* does to an institution. The branches whose standards the rest of the record leans on hardest:

**The Archive Branch** keeps the record itself. The branch is named for the institution it serves rather than the other way around — the Archive is older and larger than the Corps, and no one corps owns it — and the branch is the Corps' standing undertaking that what its surveyors log will still be legible, attributable and versioned when the surveyors are not. It is the branch that stamps [every coastline measurement with the ruler that took it](/star-rangers/lore/coasts-and-other-edges/), files a survey's transit lag on its cover so that [the local record outranks the file](/star-rangers/lore/weather-on-other-worlds/) as doctrine rather than as apology, and marks where knowledge stops instead of extending a guess over the gap. Its practitioners will say, when pressed, that theirs is the only branch whose subject can be damaged by bad surveying after the survey is over.

**The Planetology Branch** reads ground: [rock, fire and ice](/star-rangers/lore/stone-fire-and-ice/), [rivers and lakes](/star-rangers/lore/rivers-old-lakes-young/), [deserts](/star-rangers/lore/the-ledger-and-the-truce/), [coasts](/star-rangers/lore/coasts-and-other-edges/), [islands](/star-rangers/lore/the-cost-of-the-crossing/), [the underground](/star-rangers/lore/where-the-weather-stops/), [soil](/star-rangers/lore/the-thin-inheritance/). Its house doctrine is that terrain is rates wearing the costume of places, so a planetology file is dated the day it is drawn — and its standing humility, learned on [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), is that a surface survey says on its own cover what it did not reach.

**The Meteorology Branch** takes everything that moves faster than ground — [atmospheres](/star-rangers/lore/weather-on-other-worlds/) and [oceans](/star-rangers/lore/tides-without-shores/) both, the fluid envelopes read as one subject because they trade heat and water with each other continuously. It is the branch most shaped by the [no-faster-than-light-communication](/star-rangers/lore/ftl-mechanics/) discipline, since its subject outruns its reports: there is no interstellar weather service and cannot be, so the branch's real product is not forecasts but *observers* — locally posted, locally calibrated, keeping the long records that cannot be imported. Its house lesson is the covering note filed with the storm-severity classification the [Five Islands](/star-rangers/lore/planets/kingdom-of-the-five-islands/) forced on it: *the sky was not being dramatic; our instrument was being provincial.*

**The Ecology Branch** counts who lives there. It keeps the [cross-biosphere taxonomy](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/), reads [a forest as a bet and grass as a strategy](/star-rangers/lore/the-forest-is-waiting/), and files the tenants of every environment down to [the ones in the dark](/star-rangers/lore/where-the-weather-stops/). It is the branch whose files say *habitable* least and *habitable for* most, because its whole subject is the fit between a life and a place — and its founding discipline, inherited from the [Crisis Centuries](/star-rangers/lore/crisis-centuries-terran-wildlife/), is that a census taken carelessly becomes the only record of what was lost.

**The Xenolinguistics Branch** is described [where its school is](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-academy/): the branch that learns languages because there is no universal translator and is not going to be, sitting in the Survey Corps because that is where the standing delegations sit.

The list is open, as branch lists are: a branch named nowhere in the record is not thereby absent, only not yet described.

## The Seams Are Deliberate

The branch boundaries fall along the record's own [natural archives](/star-rangers/lore/stone-fire-and-ice/), and one of them is shared on purpose. A glacier's layered years and a cave's held temperature are Planetology's; a tree's living rings are Ecology's; and [soil](/star-rangers/lore/the-thin-inheritance/) — stone by substance, tenancy by function — carries both branches' stamps on one file. The Corps has been asked more than once to settle which branch owns it, and has declined each time, on the reasoning that a subject genuinely made of two things is best surveyed by both of them. The seam is not a flaw in the org chart. It is the org chart being honest about the world.

## Sapience Is Not a Sight

One piece of doctrine is trained into every branch rather than owned by one, because the cost of missing it is the highest the Corps knows: **sapience might not be physically obvious until after communication is well established.** Nothing about a body settles it — [anatomy establishes descent, never mind](/star-rangers/lore/cross-biosphere-taxonomy/), and a people can resemble [the animals beside them](/star-rangers/glossary/simian/) closely enough that inspection files them together — and nothing about artifacts settles it either, since a people may build little, build underground, or [keep their whole record in living memory](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) and leave a surface survey nothing to photograph. What settles it is the talking, and the talking takes as long as it takes: a surveyor is trained to treat *no evidence of sapience* as a statement about the survey, not about the world, for exactly as long as communication has not been seriously attempted — which is the posture the [Frontier Transformation Protocols'](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/) extended windows exist to fund.

The training leans on the record's own humiliations, deliberately kept. The [Federation's](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) boundary outposts logged three Ranger survey passes before anyone crossed a comms channel — the Corps itself was, for a while, the non-obvious party. And [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c01/) sat in the archive for generations as *unclassifiable EM noise* because every automated classifier asked whether the signal matched a known protocol and none asked whether it had phrasing — the finding that took a newly made Principal one listen, and the case every Survey school now teaches under the same heading. The error runs in both directions, and the doctrine holds both: a census that files a person as an animal, and a survey that files a conversation as weather, are the same mistake — **the instrument was answering a different question** — and the corrective is the same discipline as everywhere else in the Corps: mark what has not been established, and do not let the absence of an answer stand in the record dressed as one.

## What a Branch Does Not Own

The [division of labour](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-science-corps/) holds inside every branch: the Survey Corps collects, and the Science Corps explains what was collected. A Meteorology Branch surveyor logs the storm under protocol; what the storm *means* — instrument fault, new phenomenon, or a provincial scale — is a Science Corps determination, even when one officer on a small post holds both certifications and does both jobs from the same chair. The branches own observation standards, instrument certification, and the continuity of the watching. They own no conclusions, and their standing in the record rests exactly there: a body that claims nothing is the one body everybody's claims must cite.
