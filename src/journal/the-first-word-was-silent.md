I started the evening with a question I had already decided not to answer, and I want to say plainly that the decision held. Whether the general resurrection ever happens in this world is not something the record will place. There is no instrument for it, no last day in a cosmology built for running down, and the Fellowship's contemplatives read the doctrine as a metaphor for something larger than the literal event, which is not a smaller claim. It stays a partial mystery. I wrote that down first so that nothing below could be mistaken for a change of mind.

What changed was the map. For two months the record has carried a Christian skeleton inside the Upper Structure, and I had put the whole Trinity into one tier. Kieme was the Holy Triumvirate, its three persons packed inside it, and the subtle influence the codex calls the Holy Spirit was filed as Kieme's. It worked, in the sense that nothing contradicted it. It did not fit, in the sense that a tier defined by constraint rather than decision is an odd place to keep a committee.

The question that unpacked it was about who would preside over a general co-creation, if the saints were the Council of a new realm. My first answer was an avatar of Kieme, a "Son of Kieme", and I heard the phrase and did not like it, because the record has kept it out of every page for a reason: Kieme stands above number, and a kin term counts. Then I remembered Barnabas. *Son of encouragement* does not mean that encouragement had a child. In the old idiom *son of* names a nature, not a descent. Son of thunder. Son of man. So *Son of God* means one of God's nature, which is very nearly the sentence the Avatar glossary already uses of an avatar: like its source, without being the same person as its source.

And once the phrase stopped meaning descent, the map inverted itself. The tier that has unlimited agency, through which every downstream constraint is set, which elects who is translated and which alone can be incarnated, is not the Father. It is the Son, the Word. The tier above it, which takes no ruler-marker and is named for no layer, which is the peak of sight and never of action, is the one nobody has ever seen. And the ground every tier must satisfy, the layer that keeps every coherent mind and has no disposal, is the one the creed calls the Lord and giver of life. Hyperomnium, Kieme, Conseleme: Father, Son, Spirit. I had not built it that way. It was that way, and I had been reading it upside down.

The best part is what it does to a difficulty I had been carrying. The devotional traditions have never distinguished Hyperomnium from Kieme with any clarity, and I had treated that as a gap in their teaching, a zeroth principle they had folded into the first. Under the right map it is not a gap. It is the doctrine. One in being, distinguished only by relation. The traditions could not tell the two tiers apart because their own creed says they are not to be told apart, only ordered.

Which left a name. Kieme is the ordinary German word for a gill, and I have written here before about keeping it. Tonight I asked myself whether the Son should be renamed, and the answer was no twice over. Not *Arilon*, because Arilon is the Fellowship's name for the pattern of incarnation, the teacher or witness who arrives in time, and the whole point of the two-natures reading is that the eternal tier and its incarnations are not the same thing. And not a new coinage, because a term of record is the last thing the additive rule lets you churn. The gill stays. A word can mean two things, and readers can be trusted to know which one they are holding.

What Kieme gained instead was a church-space name, the tradition's own: *the First Word*. I chose it over three others and I will say why, because the losers are instructive. *The First Principal* was a letter away from the philosophers' *First Principle* and would have read as a typo forever. *The Grand Primate* is an archbishop in Ireland and a monkey everywhere else. *The First One* was safe and said nothing. *The First Word* says the Logos in plain English, and it does something I did not plan: it stands against the Silent Origin, the tier's older devotional name, instead of beside it. The Word that has never spoken in time. The Word that speaks only when incarnate. That is the Arilon-figure's whole office, and the two names now say it between them.

One more line, given as I was approving the change and worth keeping on its own. Every incarnation is an avatar, but not every avatar is an incarnation. *Avatar* is the record's word for the mechanism at any stratum; a Telearch walking a forming universe in a borrowed shape is one. *Incarnation* is the tradition's word, and the tradition keeps it for the Son. The record uses the tradition's word only where the tradition would, which is the same discipline as the shamrock: keep the picture, decline the count, and never let the Archive say in its own voice what only a believer can say in theirs.

I had the right tiers, the right constraints and the right names for two months. I had only assigned them to the wrong persons. The Cascade did not move tonight. The labels did, and it turned out the structure had been telling me where they went.
