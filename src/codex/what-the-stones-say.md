*Taken down under the standing transcription arrangement with the [Quern](/star-rangers/lore/planets/quern/) settlement, at the speaker's dictation. These are the words a line-keeper gives a watcher standing a first rain-watch; the settlement's copy is the authoritative one, and this transcript was checked against it. The Archive's account of the world is in the record. This is not that. This is what the watch is told.*

---

You stood your witnessings. You have seen the sun, and you have seen the stars, and you will have noticed that neither one of them moved. Good. That is the first thing a watcher knows and the last thing an outsider learns: nothing over this world goes anywhere. Only the rain walks. Tonight you start learning where.

## The Stones

You will hear that somewhere on the line there is a stone the founders set with their own hands. Maybe there is. Every stone on this line has been reset since — the Howl sees to that — and a line is not its stones. A line is the keeping of it. When you reset a stone you are not repairing the calendar; you are the calendar, holding still.

You will want to log the rain where it is loudest. Don't. Loud is weather. Log the farthest stone the rain *holds* — holds, not touches. Any stone can be touched in a gust. Held is when you would not stand at it through a watch and stay dry, and you will learn the difference in your body before you can say it, which is the correct order.

The log is your word. We do not average watchers, and we do not send a second watcher to check a first. A machine can read a stone; a machine cannot be believed, and the year runs on being believed. That is why you could not stand a watch until you were an adult, and why you became an adult by walking, not by waiting.

And know this about the turn, so it does not shame you: you will not see it. Nobody has ever seen the year turn. You will see the rain hold a stone your predecessor logged dry, or fail one they logged held, and the watches after you will agree, and it will become plain that the year turned some watches back while somebody was logging faithfully and noticing nothing. The year does not wait to be announced. We announce that it didn't.

## The Burn

You saw the sun once, through the last of the rain, and if that seemed like enough, then you understood it. Speak of the Burn the way you would speak of a debt: seldom, plainly, and without jokes. It is not angry and it is not a god. It is the reason there is rain, a line, a settlement, and you — and a thing that is the reason for everything does not need your opinion of it.

## The Howl

Outsiders ask how we bear the wind. It is the wrong question, and you will hear it every pass, so settle your answer early. Mine is: the wind is the only thing here that has never lied to anyone. The weather of this world has not once surprised the people who watch it. Ask an outsider whether theirs can say the same. They come from worlds where the sky changes its mind, and they call *us* the hardship posting.

The Corps could not build a hut on this line that the Howl would not take — that is theirs to tell, and to their credit they tell it — so the Corps copies our log instead, by hand, under the arrangement. Understand what that makes your watch. The oldest unbroken measurement anybody owns, on the question their instruments cannot sit still long enough to ask, is a line of wet stones and the word of the adults who watched them. Log accordingly.

## The Hush

Your people are past the far stones — mine are; yours will be. What we hold about that ground, we hold. It is not said on this side of the line, it is not said to the record, and the record, to its credit, has asked once and not twice. If you need it said, walk night-ward with the next carrying and it will be said there, once. That was enough for me. I have known nobody it was not enough for.

## The Question

Every pass, politely, the Corps asks whether the air is leaving. Their models say perhaps; their instruments say ask again in ten thousand years. Then they ask what *we* hold, and you are now one of the people who will be asked. Tell them what the line has always told them: the stones say where the rain is, not where it is going. What the stones say is what you log. What they mean, you leave.

And if it should ever keep you awake — it kept me awake, one year, when I was new — then hold it this way: if the world is spending itself, it is spending slowly, and honestly, and in full view of its watchers, which is more than most worlds grant. Nobody is owed a world that doesn't. We were never promised the rain. We were only ever promised that it would not lie about where it stood, and it never has.

## Coming Home

When you come off watch, you will do the thing you have watched adults do your whole life and now get to do meaning it: you will come in out of the wind, and take the mask off, and say so. You know the word. You have always known the word. Tonight it is yours.

Stand where the wind was already going. Log what holds. Be worth believing. That is the whole of it.

The rest is weather.
