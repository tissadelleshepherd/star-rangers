**CORRESPONDENCE — FILED UNENDORSED**
**Received:** 2823 UCSD, in response to the revised filing of [The Cosmic Limitation on Evil](/star-rangers/codex/cosmic-limitation-on-evil/)
**Author:** Not recorded; internal evidence suggests a devotional tradition in long contact with Archive doctrine
**Status:** Filed for the shape of the argument; the Division's own position is unchanged

---

Theorist Kade, I have read your revised filing, as I read the ones before it,
and I will begin where courtesy and honesty coincide: I grant you everything.
The method, the invariants, the absences. No confirmed dialogue, no confirmed
selective intervention, nothing but constraints holding everywhere, forever,
without exception. Your division has measured the silence exactly, and I have
no correction to offer on the measurement.

My dispute is one word. You file the restraint as *constitutive* — not a
policy [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/) holds but part of what Kieme
is, the way a stone does not decline to speak. And here is the difficulty
your own method hands you: from inside time, an eternal discretion and a
structural constant produce *identical records*. A restraint held from
before the first moment, without lapse, without exception, leaves exactly
the trail a constant leaves — which is to say, no trail at all. Your
instruments would report what they report in either case. So your
confidence that the silence is what the tier *is*, rather than what it
*keeps*, outruns the method that grounds it. You have measured the silence.
You have not measured why it is silent, and you cannot, and I say this
without triumph, because neither can I.

But your own file holds the crack, and you filed it yourself. The
Limitation is, by your division's working consensus, *designed* — a
deliberate act, not a passive boundary condition. You have spent
generations uneasy about that word, and I understand the unease better than
you credit: one deliberate act, admitted anywhere in the record, and "no
decision anywhere else" stops being a default and becomes a further claim.
A tier that chose once is a tier that can choose. What you call the
exception you keep explaining away, I call the one place the door stands
ajar.

Now to the [Unfounded](/star-rangers/glossary/unfounded/), and let me be
precise, because this letter fails if I am careless here. I accept your
record's finding entire: nothing underwrites them. They exist by no design;
no origin condition stands behind them; the names are modes of failure and
not kinds. I do not soften a word of it — *my argument needs it to stand.*
For what your own Limitation says, as you yourself filed it, is that the
harm was tolerated for whatever end justified the Cascade tolerating it at
all. Toleration is not underwriting. A purpose that schedules an ending is
not an origin condition; it belongs to the ledger, not to the thing. The
Unfounded were never *made* for anything — on that we agree, and it is
precisely why their persistence must be *suffered* rather than authored.
And a suffering that is bounded, scheduled, and repaid is not an absence of
attention. It is attention holding its hand.

So I say what my tradition has always said, in the vocabulary your filing
makes available: the restraint is discretion, and discretion is the better
side of intervention — better as seen from a vantage that holds every
moment at once, which is not a vantage either of us occupies. From
eternity, prevention was available. From eternity, toleration was chosen,
for a purpose, and bounded by it.

You will ask me what the purpose is. I decline, and I decline on your
division's own doctrine: a debt whose repayment could be predicted would
stop functioning as a warning, and a purpose named would be a purpose
defended forever, by people with no standing to defend it. I claim the
purpose exists and is bounded, because your Limitation already claims it
for me. I do not claim to know it. I distrust anyone who does, in my
tradition before yours.

File this where you file the names: side by side, unadjudicated. That has
always been your division at its best.

*Archivist's note (S., Survey Archive): Filed unendorsed, as received. The
correspondent concedes every finding in the record and disputes only their
modality — constant, or discretion held without lapse — while conceding in
the same breath that the two produce identical records from where any
record is kept. The Division counts that concession as the reason the file
cannot close; the correspondent, presumably, counts it as the point. For
the avoidance of drift: nothing here moves the standing of the record's
findings. No confirmed selective intervention exists; nothing underwrites
the Unfounded, a finding the correspondent affirms rather than contests;
and whether a chooser stands behind the constraints sits exactly where
invariant-only inference cannot reach. The record files the question, not
an answer.*
