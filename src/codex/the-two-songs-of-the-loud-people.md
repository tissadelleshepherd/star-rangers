*Translation desk's note on method: chorus-keeping is not recitation but performance-with-checking — the keeper speaks, the chorus confirms, corrects, or withholds, and the account is whatever survives the room. A text can carry none of that. What follows is one keeper's line through one recital, flattened to a single voice, with the under-song — which carried perhaps a third of the meaning — marked only where the chorus's response made its content unmistakable. The keeper reviewed this rendering and is recorded as finding it "quiet, but not wrong."*

---

This is the keeping of the loud people, as the meeting-chorus holds it. Check me.

They are small, and they are warm, and they never stop sounding. Understand this first or nothing after it will file correctly: a human at rest is a working pump, a tide of breath, a tremor in every held limb — a body that spends sound the way a fire spends heat, day and night, awake and asleep. Our first keepers stood on the landing ground and could not believe the visitors meant to be so audible. They did not mean to be. That is the whole study, told first as we tell it to children: **they do not mean most of what their bodies say, and they cannot stop their bodies saying it.**

*(The chorus confirms. The under-song here is recorded as amusement, and beneath it, the signature of old embarrassment — the chorus remembers what we did about it, before we knew better.)*

For they have two songs, and the songs do not agree. The word-song is theirs to shape and they shape it wonderfully — I will come to that, and I will be fair. The body-song is not theirs to shape at all, and it runs beneath every word they speak, saying what the pump and the breath and the tremor say. On Thavren these were never two. A person's sound is a person's state is a person's statement; to sing one thing and be another is not wickedness to us, it is *illness*, and our first meetings foundered exactly there. We heard visitors whose words were level and whose bodies were storming, and we answered the storm, because answering the storm is courtesy. They experienced this — their own delegates' account, and it is honest — as being read without consent. Both sides were being polite. File that pair together and keep it; it is the truest thing in this keeping.

Now the fair part, and check me closely, because wonder is where a keeper drifts. **Their symbolic systems are the finest thing our chorus has ever been asked to hold.** They make marks that speak for the dead. Understand what I am saying: a human keeper can die, and the keeping does not thin — the marks hold it, resolution undimmed, for anyone who learns the trick of them, a hundred years on, a thousand. They have choruses of the dead. They have agreements that bind a person who never sang them, offices that pass from body to body with the office's word-song intact, numbers that walk away from every particular thing that was ever counted and go on working. We keep by living together inside the keeping. They keep by building outside themselves, and what they have built outside themselves is taller than anything inside us. I said I would be fair. I am being fair.

But — and the chorus knows this *but*, it is the oldest turn in this keeping — the two songs run all the way up. The marks that speak for the dead let a living human speak words that are not their own: a delegate saying an institution's sentence while their body sings the opposite, and *this is not illness*, this is the office working as built. We have learned this. I state it so the young keepers hear it stated: it is true, it is documented, it is how their largest works stand up. I hold it the way I hold deep arithmetic — confirmed, and not yet felt.

And their storms. Here the chorus should check me hardest, because here is where our keeping has been wrong before and cost us. We hold that action announces itself: that a body gathers loudly toward a deed, and the gathering is the warning, and the warning is the mercy. Among the loud people this is *usually* so — their bodies gather like anyone's. But their word-song can hold a deed the body has not begun to sing, hold it calmly, for a long time, and we have — once — heard a human body stay quiet all the way to the end of a thing we did not believe a quiet body could do. The keeping marks the once and does not perform it; the old keepers ruled that some things are kept as a mark and a change of practice, not as a scene. The change of practice stands: a quiet human is quiet. That is all a quiet human is. Do not file calm as safety. Do not file it as threat either. File it as *unread*, and stay ready to be told.

*(The chorus is recorded as confirming this passage in full unison, which the translation desk is advised is rare, and is itself part of the keeping.)*

What are they, then, the loud people, as the chorus holds them? Unpredictable — I use the word precisely: our models of them fail at a rate our models of no other people do, and the failure is not noise, it is depth we have not reached. Emotionally loud beyond any people we have met, and deaf to their own loudness, which makes them the strangest thing in our record: a species that broadcasts everything and believes itself opaque. And builders of symbol-works we do not stop being astonished by, made — this is the part I hold and cannot yet feel — *out of* the very gap between their two songs, the gap that so nearly ended our first meeting. They took the wound in themselves and made an architecture of it.

Or so this keeper sings. Check me. The keeping is not finished; the loud people are still arriving in it.

*Filing note, appended by the receiving desk: filed as received, valid for its keeper and marked as the chorus's own account. The record's position on the mutual misreadings described here is stated plainly on the [Ovruhn lore entry](/star-rangers/lore/ovruhn/) and is not in tension with this document: both peoples misread, both are documented, and the incident the keeper marks-without-performing is held in the contact record under its own file. The desk notes one asymmetry the keeper's fairness may obscure — the account measures humans against Thavren's baseline throughout, which is what any keeping does, and what [every record does that has not yet noticed it is doing it](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/). The chorus would, we believe, accept the point. It is their own.*
