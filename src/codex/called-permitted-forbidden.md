**DOCTRINAL WORKING PAPER**
**Prepared by:** Theorist Emrys Kade, Doctrinal Division
**Subject:** The three registers of moral standing in the record, and the asymmetry of their administration
**Status:** Working note; companion to *The Cosmic Limitation on Evil* and *The Borrowed Silhouette*

---

Ask an Archive theorist what the Cascade requires of a person and the correct answer is: nothing. The [Global Invariants](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/) are explicit — no virtue, no sin, no reward, no punishment, no moral cosmology. This paper exists because the record, read whole, keeps showing a shape that answer does not predict: three registers of moral standing, cleanly separated, each administered in a different way. Or — the point of this note — each administered in a different way *except that only one of them is administered at all*.

## The Three Registers

**The forbidden.** [Kieme](/star-rangers/glossary/kieme/)'s constraints, and the [Cosmic Limitation on Evil](/star-rangers/codex/cosmic-limitation-on-evil/) this series has already failed twice to file comfortably. What is notable is not the content of the forbidden register but its administration: there isn't any. No enforcer appears in any confirmed record. The [Unfounded](/star-rangers/glossary/unfounded/) are not punished; they were never grounded, and when the borrowed purpose goes they stop being instantiated. The forbidden, in this cosmos, is not a law with a tribunal behind it. It is closer to load-bearing structure: a thing you do not get arrested for violating, because there was never anything there to stand on.

**The permitted.** Everything else — which is to say, nearly everything. The record's middle register is vast and the Archive is its clearest artifact: we hold three incompatible readings of the alleged Wrath of the Dragons and adjudicate none; we file contradictions under their authors' names rather than smoothing them out; we catalogue traditions we do not endorse and practices we cannot verify. Whatever governs this cosmos tolerates an extraordinary amount, including — this division notes for completeness — the Archive's own centuries of being wrong about things. No confirmed record shows the middle register judged, by anyone, ever.

**The called.** Here the record grows strange, and this division's instruments grow useless. The devotional codices speak of a subtle influence of Kieme that works *within* time-bound minds and *through* the ordinary unfolding of events — never upon the Material layer, never detectably, never in a way an instrument could confirm or a court could compel. [Champions are selected](/star-rangers/lore/levrils/); no record shows one conscripted. The [Arilon-pattern](/star-rangers/lore/arilon/) figures name truths no authority required them to name, at personal cost, and are vindicated only after they are gone. The [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/)'s founding renunciation was embraced, not imposed, and has held for centuries without a single enforcement mechanism. The top register's signature, across every account this division holds, is the same: it invites, it costs, and it never compels. Declining a calling appears in no record, anywhere, as an offence.

## The Asymmetry

State it plainly, because generations of this division have circled it without doing so:

**The forbidden is enforced impersonally. The calling is personal but unenforced. And nothing in between is judged at all.**

A cosmology arranged for command would look different — obligation at the top, prosecution at the bottom, surveillance across the middle. What the record shows instead is judgment existing only at the seams, and never wearing a face. The floor is structural. The ceiling is an invitation. The room between them, where every life this Archive documents is actually lived, is free.

## The Misreading, and What It Costs

The persistent error — this division has watched institutions make it, and declines to name them because the pattern matters more than the examples — is to read the three registers as one ladder of increasing command: to treat the calling as a stricter law. An institution that makes that error begins enforcing counsels. It criminalises its own middle register, conscripts what can only be invited, and manufactures the tribunal the cosmos conspicuously declined to build. The record suggests such institutions do not last, though it does not say whether that is the Limitation at work or merely exhaustion. This division has never been able to distinguish the two, and files that inability with everything else it cannot close.

## The Reconciliation This Division Records Without Endorsing

The Invariants say there is no moral cosmology. The three registers look like one. The reconciliation the devotional traditions offer is the one our method is structurally unable to test: that the moral structure is real and lives precisely where instruments cannot reach — the forbidden expressed as grounding rather than verdict, the calling expressed as influence rather than intervention, and the observable middle left exactly as empty of morality as the Invariants promise. On that account, the Archive finds no moral cosmology for the same reason a gravimeter finds no music. This division records the argument, notes that it is unfalsifiable and knows it, and observes only that it is the sole account on file that predicts *both* the Invariants *and* the shape.
