Rosamund Hallam wrote for readers at home. *Lanterns Past the Edge of the Map: A New Londoner in the Celtic Union*, printed in [New London](/star-rangers/lore/new-london-space-habitat/) in 2821 UCSD, is a traveller's book about the [Celtic Union](/star-rangers/lore/celtic-union-of-planets/), and its fourth chapter, *[The Elves of Wood, Sky and Water](/star-rangers/codex/the-elves-of-wood-sky-and-water/)*, is the one the Survey Archive keeps. In it Hallam sorts what the Union calls the Good People into three kinds by where they are found, as a field guide sorts birds: the wood, the sky and the water.

## Known History

- Travelled in the Celtic Union as a guest, on the coast and in the uplands of [Tír na nÓg](/star-rangers/lore/planets/tir-na-nog/), and talked with Union people from further out.
- Saw none of what the chapter describes, and says so on its first page.
- Was told by three people in two coastal towns that the Good People are seen at the tide line on the night of the Conjunction: the first Union accounts in the Archive's holdings to place them there, now carried in the record on the [Tír Tairngire](/star-rangers/lore/universes/tir-tairngire/#who-crosses) page.
- Was asked by a Union host to leave out every date, and printed none.

## Character Notes

Hallam uses *elves* knowingly, asks the Union's pardon for the word once, and then uses it throughout, because it is the word a New London reader already has. The book is sincere and a little too neat: it lets three homes decide three kinds. It is also careful about its own standing. It says the sorting is the author's own and offers it as a reader's first map rather than the last, and its one new fact was gathered by asking the same question in two towns of people who did not know one another. The [receiving desk's filing note](/star-rangers/codex/the-elves-of-wood-sky-and-water/#filing-note-appended-by-the-receiving-desk) finds the sorting wrong in kind on every count and right about where to look three times out of three.

The record holds nothing of Hallam beyond that chapter: no species stated, no age, no other book, no year for the journey beyond its coming before the 2821 printing, and no word on whether Hallam ever went back.
