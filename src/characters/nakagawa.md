Hesper Nakagawa leads the [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) survey, and reads the ground, which from the outside looks like standing still. The survey's doctrine on that world — slow everything down: the longest windows, the lightest presence, nothing on the surface that the planet would keep if the station were abandoned — is doctrine Nakagawa had a hand in writing and has since had to keep, which is the harder half.

## What the Record Holds

The record has Nakagawa's voice at length in one place: the countersign withheld from [Instrument Specialist Egede's proposal to collar the Anstolik](/star-rangers/codex/proposal-to-collar-the-anstolik/), filed complete at Nakagawa's own request because a refusal kept without the thing it refused teaches nobody anything. The refusal turns on one sentence — *the instrument walks downward* — and on the finding that the collar's most confident output on Fliade would not be a poacher but a [Pandoid](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), correctly classified, logged, and relayed to a station they do not know exists. The same logic is the one the party's own relay unit later applied, at zero range, to the thing that was carrying it.

Two smaller acts are on [Individual Four's page](/star-rangers/characters/teddy/). The evening the unit's thermal channel showed a warm upright profile at the edge of the working light for eleven minutes, Nakagawa read the party's entry and struck out the word *young*: the survey has no method for ageing a Pandoid, *smaller* is the measurement, and a guess wearing a measurement's clothes leans on every reading made after it. And when the unit was found standing at the line where the party's tracks stopped, Nakagawa filed the reading that a line had been drawn — with the party's own instrument, at the party's own furthest mark, by somebody who had watched where the party stopped and thought about it — beside Egede's reading that a dropped thing had been returned, chose neither, swung the light once into the dark as far as it reached, and ordered the unit lifted. No unit has been placed past the line since and the party has not crossed it.

From above, [*Eleven Minutes* (S11E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c01/) gives the standing order that followed, in Nakagawa's own words: the log says, every shift, that nothing happened. Not an entry, a count — *when you do not know what a thing meant, you do not do the next thing; you write down that you do not know, and you wait, and every day you wait you write that down too* — so that on the day something does happen, the file will say how long it had been true that nothing did. [*Nothing Happened* (S11E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c02/) is that count kept: Nakagawa lays the boulder shifts in a row and sees that the adults announce themselves on every arrival and the party has never once done the same, files it as an observation and not a finding; reads on the imager that the adults withdraw from the light's moving shadow before the light, and reads that as a people who live where no shadow has ever moved; refuses Egede's gift on the surface rule (*a thing left at the line is a thing left*); hears Aravena's question on the sound channel every shift and says nothing, because a thing said to the dark is not a thing said to the survey lead; and, when the third reading is finally given, answers *not today*, and means exactly that. [*Something Happened* (S11E02C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c03/) is the morning after: *we knock* — a stone of the upper levels, the planet's own, so nothing is left the planet would keep; set down first, before the work; by one person, because a knock is one person at a door; Aravena, who fits and who asked. Nakagawa hears the answering rock from the junction, writes the log in the survey lead's own hand — *something happened*, and under it the count, sixty-three shifts on which nothing had — and gives the standing order that replaces the other: the party knocks before every shift, and works only after. What was answered, in Nakagawa's reading, is that the party may stand in the doorway. Two years to learn to knock, and Nakagawa did not think it had been slow.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

**Which reading Nakagawa believes.** Both were filed as equals because that is what the survey knew, and Nakagawa has not said more. What the chapter adds is only that Nakagawa noticed the third reading had not been given, and by whom.

## Character Notes

Nakagawa strikes things out, and lets things go, and knows the difference: *young* is struck because a guess in the log corrupts the log, and the party's *he* is let by because it does no harm in the party's mouth and there are only so many things a survey lead can strike in one evening before people stop hearing it. The posture is the record's own — filed, unhurried, nobody exclaiming — applied by the one person on Fliade whose job is to hold the party still. On a world where the mechanism of invitation has nothing to grip, the only protection the survey can offer is restraint, and Nakagawa is where the restraint lives.
