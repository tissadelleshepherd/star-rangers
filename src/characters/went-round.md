The name is a telling, on the pattern by which [the Told](/star-rangers/glossary/the-told/) name everyone: the first true thing said of a person that others went on repeating. What was said of this one is that they go round. Where a passage is blocked, Went-Round does not clear it but finds another; where a thought is hard, Went-Round does not finish it but comes at it from the far side to see whether it looks easier from there. It is a good way to be, and [Stone-First](/star-rangers/characters/stone-first/) has always said so, and it is not Stone-First's way. The name is rendered in translation, as every Pandoid name in the record is; the people's word is theirs.

## What the Record Holds

Nothing, from the survey's side. The Fliade survey cannot tell one adult from another at the distance it sees them, and has no name for any.

From inside, [*Someone to Ask* (S11E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c03/): the adult who said, after the humming stone had been heard and nothing had followed, that a stranger who cannot hear the ground cannot ask, so that *leave it to them until they ask* is leaving them alone forever and calling it manners — and who, when the youngest brought down the telling of an up-person speaking to the dark, said the truest thing anyone said that night: that nobody had been there for that part, and nobody could be, so it was not a thing the people's way of settling arguments could settle. And, from the far side of the amber in [*Waiting to Be Asked* (S11E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c04/), the reading of the waiting that the warm kept: *they ask the dark, and we are in the dark, and nobody answers* — everybody below the roof keeping manners at everybody else. And in [*Theirs to Move* (S11E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c05/), when Stone-First told the up-people's stone and would say only *they began*, the reply that the warm kept beside it: *they asked* — and, for once, not from the far side: *nobody knows what anybody says until the telling goes on; that is what a telling is.*

In the thread's third season the going round is tested. In [*Nobody Came* (S14E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c01/) Went-Round has the small ones play their game of the manners in front of everyone and reads it from the far side: nobody is told how to move a stone, the hand learns on stones moved for nobody, and the manners began as a game somebody played seriously until everyone was playing it. In [*Not a Place You Tell From* (S14E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c02/) Went-Round answers the warm's objections one by one, and sees the thing the warm cannot get past coming from a long way off. And in [*Keeps a Hand on It* (S14E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c03/), for the first time in a whole telling, Went-Round goes all the way round a thing and comes back without a far side, sits still with the rest of the warm while the smallest keeps a hand on a stone, and does not see what the stillness is until an old one says it; which Went-Round told afterwards, against themself, which is the only way a thing like that gets told at all. In [*Been Told* (S14E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c04/), when Stone-First comes down and says the up-person stayed and the telling was told, Went-Round says the thing the warm was waiting for someone to say, *somebody has told them something*, and, asked whether the up-people are Told now, for once goes straight through it: being told one thing is not being Told, it is being a small one, at the beginning, which is all anybody is ever given first.

Went-Round is also the author of the one history of the Told the record holds in the people's own voice, [*Where the Tellings Run Out*](/star-rangers/codex/where-the-tellings-run-out/): told standing up, in the warm, to everyone, going round the old tellings in their order from where they run out to the coming of the up-people, and stopping there; kept by the ones who heard it, taken down by nobody, and rendered by the record the way the thread is.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

**Their name.** A translation of a telling; the people's word is not held.

**Their age, and their sex.** The record's *they* is its translation default.

**Whether they were right.** The chapter gives the argument to nobody. Stone-First's answer was to go and stand on the line, which is neither asking nor waiting for it; Went-Round's objection stands unanswered beside it, and the record has not been told what the Told decided, if they decided anything.
