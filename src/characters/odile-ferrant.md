Odile Ferrant reads boundary instruments. That is the whole of her trade, and the record holds her for the one thing it says about such people: a rank tab says a Ranger is trusted to command, and a certification patch says a Ranger is trusted to be right about a specific, narrow thing, and the uniform is built so the two can be checked separately. Ferrant's tab is a Section Lead's. Her patch is the [certified boundary analyst's](/star-rangers/lore/rank-insignia-and-uniform/), issued by the Safety Corps for Etheric-layer assessment at [Threshold-class stations](/star-rangers/lore/boundary-zones/), and it is the patch and not the tab that [Hesper Nakagawa](/star-rangers/characters/nakagawa/) wrote to the Survey Corps' boundary side for.

## What the Record Holds

She came to the [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) station in the survey's third year on the ground, on the supply run, in uniform, with one pack, and read the party's file standing in the wind before she had taken the pack off. The file was seven weeks of an optical return at the surface party's furthest working point with nothing on any other channel, filed seven times as an imager artefact and then rewritten by the survey lead to the fact. Her first question was not about the imager. It was about the third shelf of the station's instrument rack.

The rack carried a cross-membrane gravimeter, installed under the [Frontier Transformation Protocols'](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/) boundary-proximity requirements when the station went up, dormant, sealed, logging since the first year, boundary-side property the survey was not entitled to read and had ticked *nominal* on two manifests. Ferrant broke the seal, because the seal was hers to break, and read two years of a log that had said nominal every day and had meant, every day, *inside the band I was told to report in*. What was inside the band is the subject of [*Nominal* (S12E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c02/), and the record holds it there and not here.

Her filing is the thing the record keeps her for. She wrote her reading in the boundary side's own file, at a stated grade, under the name of the page she reads for a living, and she wrote nothing of it in the survey's log, which carries one line in her hand: *read by boundary officer; reading filed boundary-side at stated confidence; fact unchanged.* Asked by the survey lead what the boundary feature under study near Fliade is, she said it was in her file and not in the survey's and that she was not going to put it there, and the Fliade entry's sentence on the feature stands as it stood.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

What she wrote the reading's grade as. What she thinks the shape at the third passage mouth is, beyond what the grade says. Whether she has read the party's account of the people below the margin as anything but a survey's observations, which is all the file calls it. The record notes that she asked [Aravena](/star-rangers/characters/aravena/) what was said to the one on the line at the knock, was told it was not in the log, and said *good*.

## Character Notes

Ferrant is the season's instance of a figure the record has needed since Threshold: the person who knows the page and declines to let knowing it stand in for a finding. Her discipline is the Archive's own, carried by one officer onto a roof where the survey had two words, *somebody* and *nothing*, and a thing that was neither. She does not supply the third word to the survey. She supplies it to her own file, at its grade, where it will be read by people who know what the grade means, and she leaves the survey's log saying what the imager saw and not what it was.
