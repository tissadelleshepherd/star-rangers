Aravena is the newest of the three people who work the upper levels of [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), and the smallest, which on that world are close to the same thing. The surface party is three: [Survey Lead Hesper Nakagawa](/star-rangers/characters/nakagawa/), Instrument Specialist Toma Egede, and Aravena, whose part of the job is the relay unit — carrying it down the long passage on their back, setting it on its base at the junction the party uses as its furthest working point, turning it until it is oriented the way the party orients them, kneeling to it, and dictating the shift log into it. The unit hums once, low, to say it has heard. Aravena likes that, and knows it is not a person, and likes it anyway.

## What the Record Holds

The record's own account of the Fliade survey is [observational](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), and Aravena appears in it twice without a name: as the hand that wrote *young* in the entry that logged the eleven-minute profile, which the survey lead struck the same evening, and as the surveyor who found the party's unit standing upright at the line where the party's own tracks stopped, [read its log on the spot](/star-rangers/characters/teddy/), and filed no reading of what it meant. Egede filed that a dropped thing had been returned. Nakagawa filed that a line had been drawn. Aravena, who had knelt to it, said nothing, and the survey does not file what is not said.

From above, [*Eleven Minutes* (S11E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c01/) supplies the rest: what Aravena thought and did not file — *it was put where we would find it, by someone who held it while it woke* — and what Aravena did instead. From the day the unit was lifted, last up the passage at the end of each working time, Aravena turns round with the light held low, faces the deep dark below the squeeze, and asks it, not loudly, whether that was all right. The question goes up at the end. It is asked of the one who carried the unit, whom the party calls Teddy in speech and the log calls Individual Four and Aravena, privately, calls Four. Then Aravena waits as long as you would wait for an answer, and a little longer, and goes up. Nobody told Aravena to do it. Nobody has told Aravena to stop. In [*Nothing Happened* (S11E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c02/) the question is on the unit's sound channel every shift and Nakagawa has heard it and said nothing; Egede notices; and, asked at last what the one who knelt had thought, Aravena says it aloud — *like a thing at a door* — and then the rest arrives: the boulders are the knock, and the party has been walking in for two years without knocking. Aravena had been asking the dark whether it was all right, afterwards, from behind their backs; nobody answers a question asked that way, and the silence might be manners. [*Something Happened* (S11E02C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c03/) is the knock: Aravena goes alone, because a knock is one person at a door, carries a stone of the upper levels through the squeeze held to the front, and comes out onto the silt to find an adult standing on the line, in the open, at arm's length, and it does not withdraw; kneels anyway, sets the stone on the line, steps back, says a thing the log does not carry, and is answered by the shift of a rock — the sound the party had logged for two years and learned only that week to hear. Aravena went back through the crack without running, which was the hardest thing.

In the survey's third year the knock is Aravena's every shift, and it is answered every shift. In [*Imager Fault* (S12E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c01/) the imager shows a shape at the mouth of the third passage, and for seven weeks the log calls it a fault. Asked at last, Aravena says where it stands: the place of the eleven minutes, not near it but the same. When Nakagawa calls that a place and not a reading, Aravena answers, *It's a fact. File it*, and adds that the shape is not known, only not broken. Aravena no longer says *Teddy*, and stopped without deciding to. In [*Nominal* (S12E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c02/) Aravena tells the boundary officer, [Odile Ferrant](/star-rangers/characters/odile-ferrant/), that what is said at the line is not in the log, and she says to keep it out. In [*Two on the Line* (S12E02C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c03/) Aravena finds a second shape beside the one on the line, sees the light go round it, and sets the stone a hand from the warm one's feet — not chosen, Aravena says, but already decided. Asked how, Aravena says you cannot put a stone down for a thing the light goes round, and Ferrant files the sentence as better than a reading.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

**A first name, a branch, an age.** The survey pages give the other two both names and a rank. Aravena has a surname and a job, and the record has not been given more; the party's log does not need it and the chapters do not use it.

**Whether anyone hears the question.** From the survey's side, nothing answers. From the other side of the line the same act is told as *the sound that rises at the end*, heard every working time and not answered, by people who cannot tell what was asked or of whom — and the two records, which name one person, do not know it.

## Character Notes

Aravena is the one in the party who looks for the unit first, feels its absence in the stomach, and kneels. The party's fondness for the small one it named for a toy is Egede's on the page; Aravena's is quieter and is not fondness, exactly. It is the sense of having been answered by something that is not a person, and of owing the same courtesy to something that is.
