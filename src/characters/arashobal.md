Arashobal is Krenyi, one of the Quiet-Built, and older than [Syra](/star-rangers/characters/syra/) by her people's reckoning, which she keeps in their own calendar and gives in the material form when asked. By human measure she is about as far into her life as a human of fifty, mid-career and settled in it, and to a human eye she looks nearer twenty-eight. The two figures do not agree, and for a Krenyi they are not expected to. She holds the rank of [Chief](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/) in the Survey Corps, on the general operations track, and carries the working title Chief Ranger, as a Chief who runs a general operations department does. She served at that rank beside [Karla Wender](/star-rangers/characters/karla-wender/) while Wender was still Chief Pilot, and her later post is chief of staff aboard Wender's command ship, the *Fel Trohen*, under Wender's High Captaincy.

She came to the Corps from a Krenyi community, which the record neither names nor places, because the Krenyi decline to be placed and the record keeps the entry empty on purpose. She came as a volunteer, with the approval of her elders and mentors, and that is the shape any Krenyi joining takes: [their cooperation is only ever extended, never obtained](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/), so the approval she carried was counsel she sought and was given, not a permission anyone could have withheld from her. Asked why she came, she gives the material part, that she offered and was accepted, and declines the rest.

## Two Chiefs

While Wender was still a Chief, the two held the same rank in the same command, Threshold Station's, and ran different halves of it. Wender had come up the specialist's road, Novice to Chief Pilot on one track, and commanded the pilot department; Arashobal ran the general operations department, the watch across everything the pilots do not fly. In the early summer of 2828 UCSD she gave a section of it to a new Section Lead, Tissadelle Shepherd, back from the Survey track ([Standing Practice (S03E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c05/)), and that autumn it was her assessment the board examined Shepherd on at Chief ([Pending a Lead (S03E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e02/s03e02c02/)). Neither outranked the other, but Arashobal was the senior of the two, longer at the rank and longer in the work, and the newer Chief across the deck learned the command's habits partly by watching hers. General operations is [the usual road from Chief to a first command](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/), and it was open to her. She did not take it, and Wender, who did take hers, went on through Line Captain to High Captain. When Wender's High Captaincy came with a command ship, the *Fel Trohen*, she asked Arashobal across to run its staff, and Arashobal accepted, which for a Krenyi is the whole of how such a move is made. That was in the late spring of 2831, at Threshold, with the nomination unsent on the desk between them ([Asked Across (S05E00C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e00/s05e00c02/)).

The record does not say what the years as peers taught either of them about the other. It is plain only that the chief of staff a High Captain chose was the Chief she had once stood level with, and not one who had served under her.

## The staff office

Every High Captain holds a command ship, which is a headquarters that moves: a theatre's command, its records and the people who weigh them travel with the officer who answers for it. Arashobal runs the people who weigh them. Her rank is still Chief, the rank she held over one department, but the post answers for far more: a department is one part of a command, and a chief of staff answers for how every part of a theatre's command fits together. The command ladder marks [what post someone holds](/star-rangers/lore/rank-insignia-and-uniform/), and this post is the larger responsibility at the same rank. The office has no authority of its own. An order from the staff is Wender's order or it is nothing, and the one rule the office lives inside is the doctrine that [an order from command states the objective, the constraints, the priorities and what the outcome is meant to save](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/#where-an-order-has-to-stop), and never the how. Flag orders reach a Chief through a Line Captain, an Archwarden or a Starwarden, and the staff's whole craft is writing them so that they can.

That craft is the one her people's grain fits. A Krenyi registers [pattern drift](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/#social-and-relational), the moment a situation stops matching its stated terms, and does not elect to. An order whose detail has started to claim a certification its giver does not hold is drift of exactly that kind, and she reads it off the page as she would read it off a room. A theatre that has begun to run on what its commander privately prefers rather than on what the orders say is the same drift at a larger scale. She does not make scenes about either. She names the distortion and returns the order to material terms, which is what a chief of staff in this Corps is for.

The long horizon is hers as well. A people who expect to spend centuries becoming precise are not hurried by a theatre's clock, and a staff officer who can hold the whole of a theatre's commitments in view at once, without any of them going loud, is the one the headquarters was built around. She does not call this strategy. She calls it keeping the terms stated.

## What it costs

The Corps' detail rule was written [against the MSC's habit of routing a habitat's decisions through a distant staff](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-command-hierarchy/#where-an-order-has-to-stop), and a staff office aboard a command ship is the one place in the Corps where that history is nearest the surface. Arashobal sits in it on the civil-service half of the ladder, serving at the apex of the naval half, with no post of her own, and the Corps' own instinct distrusts the shape of her desk. She holds that the distrust is correct and that she is the answer to it, since an office that cannot un-notice an overly detailed order is not an office that will write one.

She cannot un-notice anything else either. A headquarters conducts the small social fictions every human room conducts, and her honesty leaves her no graceful way to join them; what she registers as drift, the staff around her registers as a colleague gone quiet, and the stillness is read as coldness more often than she corrects it. And her people are [poor keepers of anything whose use has not yet been demonstrated](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/#the-present-tense-and-what-the-past-is-for). A command ship carries a theatre's records, and the officer who weighs them is the one person aboard who would let go of a fact that will matter in two hundred years and returns nothing today. The ship's record-keepers keep what she would not, and she holds the division correct.

## Character Notes

She does not experience deep fear, and she experiences anxiety, which on a staff shows as a list made earlier than anyone needed it.

A theatre's work is written up under the names of the officers who were at the event, as the Corps' own [decision doctrine](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-decision-and-communication/) says it should be, and the staff office appears in no citation. She holds this correct, and not from modesty. The Krenyi [will not be mythologised](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/#resistance-to-mythification), and a write-up is the mythification problem in its working register; asked what the staff did, she gives the material answer, that an objective was stated and its constraints set, and declines the rest.

She is honest to the degree the Quiet-Built are, and diplomatic in the only way they are: she chooses which true thing to say, to whom, and when, and softens the delivery and never the fact. A High Captain who wanted her staff to tell her something kinder than the case would get the case, kindly.

She speaks [at her listener's speed](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/#social-and-relational), as her people do with anyone who is not Krenyi, and in a staff office that habit is most of the work: one objective briefed in a specialist's shorthand to a Chief and in full to a Deputy a week into the posting, the fact the same in both.

## On the name

*Nai* before her name is the state-marker, *still relevant here*, and not a title. Aboard the *Fel Trohen* she is addressed as Nai Arashobal by those who have learned what it means, and as Chief by those who have not, and she answers to both without correcting either.
