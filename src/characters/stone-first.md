The name is a telling. Among the people of the deep networks a person is called by the first thing said of them that others went on repeating, and what was said of this one, long enough ago that nobody now tells it firsthand, is that they move a stone first — every time, into every place, so that the ground announces them before they are seen. It is the people's manners for entering, and Stone-First is the one who kept them when others were careless, which is how a habit becomes a name. The name is rendered here in translation, as the record renders every word of a people whose own words it does not hold; the people's word for it is theirs.

They keep the margin: the line where the upper levels, which the runners graze and the up-people now work, shelve down into the deep that belongs to the people. Keeping it means being there. Not guarding — nothing has ever come down the margin that needed guarding against — but being present on all the times when nothing happens, so as to be present on the time when something does. Stone-First has kept it since before the youngest's telling begins.

## What the Record Holds

Nothing, from the survey's side. The Fliade survey logs adults at the margin on the days the smaller one comes up and on the days they do not, announced when they are announced at all by the shift of a boulder, and has no way to tell one adult from another at that distance. Stone-First is one of those boulders. Which one, the survey does not know and this page does not say.

From inside, [*Nobody Looked Up* (S11E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c01/): the adult who let the youngest go up unannounced, felt the warm change when they went, waited to be told, and asked for the telling a second time with one thing left out. And [*Carried It Sleeping* (S11E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c02/): the adult who answered *why don't they ask* with *nobody has told them anything*, heard the telling of the humming stone standing up, said only *you held still — good*, and carried the telling down into the warm, leaving the reason out, which is how the youngest came to be named. And [*Someone to Ask* (S11E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c03/): the adult who, when the manners were argued for the first time, finished the kept thought and went up through the shelving to stand on the line where the up-people's feet had stopped, and stayed there. And [*Waiting to Be Asked* (S11E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c04/): the adult who kept the line on every time, heard the up-people's rising sound come down through the squeeze at the end of each working time and did not answer it, because a thing answered that was not asked of you is interrupted, not answered; who counted the askings, the first count of a whole life; and who, when the first person to stand beside them on the line was a small one from below, sent the small one down to tell it. And [*Theirs to Move* (S11E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c05/): the adult who was on the line when the light came through the squeeze for the first time since the humming stone was lifted, and the smallest of the up-people came out of the crack with a stone of the upper levels and set it down on the line — late, and kneeling, and theirs — and stepped back and said a thing that rose at the end; who answered the only way the Told answer a stone, by moving one; and whose name grew in the warm that night, on the rule that a truer thing said later is added: *the one who moves a stone first, and went up and stood on the line, and waited to be asked, and was*. The record renders the grown name **Stone-First-Who-Waited** and does not shorten it; this page keeps its title because a name is not replaced.

The season after brings things that move no stone, and the chapters call Stone-First the oldest of the people. In [*No Warmth in It* (S12E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c01/), when the smallest tells of a shape at the seep, Stone-First asks who was there, says the telling is the smallest's, and will not make a rule from one telling. In [*Someone Is Coming* (S12E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c02/) a stone is moved well, by nobody; Stone-First leaves the line to look at it, which Stone-First had never done, and does not move it back, because a stone moved for you is not yours to move. In [*Where Someone Always Stands* (S12E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c03/), told that they were seen on the line while they sat telling in the warm, Stone-First is seen for the first time to not know what to say, and to say nothing. In [*Out of the Wall* (S12E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c04/), when the people are not wary of a shape made of light, Stone-First tells them: *Do not give it a thing it did not do.* In [*Where the Rock Is* (S12E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c05/) Stone-First steps off the line to let a small one find out whether they still fit the squeeze, and when the rock holds them can only say *breathe out*. And in [*The Light Went Round It* (S12E01C06)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c06/), with a shape standing beside them on the line, Stone-First watches the up-people, who cannot hear the ground, tell the two apart with nothing but a light, and comes down and says so in the warm: the Told could not.

The third season begins with the small ones playing the manners as a game. In [*Nobody Came* (S14E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c01/) Stone-First hears it from the line as the ground saying *someone is coming* over and over with nobody coming, and puts aside, for a time when nothing happens, the thought that the up-people were there for the telling of their own first stone and have never heard it. In [*Not a Place You Tell From* (S14E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c02/) Stone-First brings that thought down and says it standing up; the warm settles that the telling goes up and that Stone-First tells it, and cannot settle where, because Stone-First holds that the line is not a place you tell from and nobody has ever told from it. Then the small ones play their game in front of Stone-First for the first time, and the one on the line says who knocked, and Stone-First has it played again and does not say the other thing twice. In [*Keeps a Hand on It* (S14E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c03/) Stone-First comes down a second time in one season and says one short thing, that the up-people wait for as long as it takes a hand to be filled and a telling is longer than a hand, and leaves it in the warm; an old one's answer comes back up with Carried-It-Sleeping, a hand kept on the answering stone for as long as the telling takes, and Stone-First, at the next knock, feels the wanting to keep the hand there and find out, and moves the stone at once, because a thing done to see whether it works is neither manners nor a telling. In [*Been Told* (S14E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c04/) the hand stays: the smallest of the up-people waits a hand and does not go, and Stone-First tells them the telling of their own first stone from the line, bent to the rock rather than standing, which is not how a telling is told and is how the line let it be told, leaving nothing out and not looking to see whether it is understood, and moves the stone at the end. It is the first word any of the Told has said to an up-person. Stone-First came down and told it standing up, and said plainly how it had been told so that nobody would make a rule of it, and went back up, and at the next knock answered at once, because a telling told is not told twice to the same people.

## The Question They Kept

The people's manners for a stranger in your place are short: leave it to them until they ask. The up-people came a long time ago and have never asked, and the people have kept the manners anyway. Stone-First thought about that on the times when nothing happens, which is most of them, for longer than the youngest's whole telling, and said the thought was for keeping.

It was finished in the third chapter, after [Went-Round](/star-rangers/characters/went-round/) said the manners were leaving the up-people alone forever. *Leave it to them until they ask* assumed a stranger who knew you were there, and the Told had kept its second half and forgotten its first: they had left the place to the up-people so thoroughly that when the up-people looked into the dark for someone to ask, nobody was there. So the margin had moved — from the dark where the deep begins to the line in the open where the up-people's feet stopped — and Stone-First went and kept it there, on all the times when nothing happens. Not asking, and not answering. Being someone to ask.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

**Their name.** *Stone-First* is a translation of a telling, on the pattern by which [the Told](/star-rangers/glossary/the-told/) name everyone — the first true thing said of a person that others went on repeating; the people's word is not held.

**Their age, and their sex.** The *they* on this page is the record's translation default, declining to claim a pronoun for a people who may keep a system of their own.

**Whether keeping the margin is an office.** The chapter shows one person doing it. Whether the people have a word for the doing, or a rule about who does it, the record has not been told.
