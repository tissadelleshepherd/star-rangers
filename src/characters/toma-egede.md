Toma Egede is the instrument specialist of the surface party that works the upper levels of [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/). The party is three: [Survey Lead Hesper Nakagawa](/star-rangers/characters/nakagawa/), who reads the ground; [Aravena](/star-rangers/characters/aravena/), who carries the relay unit and dictates the log; and Egede, at the far wall with the imager, saying things. Egede always said things, and was usually worth listening to.

Egede's one Codex document is a proposal that was not adopted, with a note Egede asked to have appended after the refusal. The chapters add the rest: the specialist argues for choosing, reaches for an explanation and is relieved when it arrives, and, when the survey lead strikes the explanation out, says nothing for a while and then asks the better question.

## Known History

- 2832 UCSD: wrote the prospectus in *[A Proposal to Collar the Anstolik, Refused at Countersign](/star-rangers/codex/proposal-to-collar-the-anstolik/)*: twelve Anstolik fitted with a passive detection collar, no cognitive subsystem, released and left alone, to watch the corridors the herds already cross. Egede states the power problem plainly and says the 96.7% figure is bench performance, not a field figure. Nakagawa withheld countersign and filed the prospectus anyway. Egede's appended note accepts it and disputes one word: the herd would not be conscripted; it would be *unasked, which is worse*.
- 2832 UCSD, the survey's second year, *[Eleven Minutes](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c01/)*: Egede read the eleven-minute thermal profile as the small one, [Teddy](/star-rangers/characters/teddy/). When the relay unit was found standing at the line, Egede filed that a dropped thing had been returned, a courtesy; Nakagawa filed that a line had been drawn; both were kept. Egede argued for choosing, did not like the standing order that the log say every shift that nothing happened, and said *unasked* aloud, the way Egede said a word that was going to end up in a document.
- The same year, *[Nothing Happened](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c02/)* and *[Something Happened](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c03/)*: Egede found on the imager that the adults withdrew from the light's moving shadow and not the light; proposed a gift left at the line and was refused on the surface rule; offered to be the one to go down.
- 2833 UCSD, the third year, *[Imager Fault](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c01/)*: Egede filed seven occurrences of a dark, dense-edged, upright shape at the third passage mouth as *imager artefact, cause under investigation*, through two recalibrations, a lens change, the test card and the spare imager, and ticked the boundary-side gravimeter nominal on the manifest. After the seventh, Nakagawa struck *artefact* and *fault* with Egede present. In *[Two on the Line](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e02/s12e02c03/)* Egede, for once, said nothing.

## Character Notes

Egede writes a proposal the way Egede reads an instrument: the failure modes first, the honest problem not buried, the figure named for exactly what it is a figure of. The seven *artefact* entries were the same habit at its limit, honest every one, and each a reading entered as a fact. The survey lead's judgement on them is also the record's on Egede: the better reading, let stand because it was better, and still a reading.

The record gives Egede a first name, a rank and a job, and nothing else: no origin, no species stated, no posting before Fliade. The record places Egede on Fliade in 2832 and 2833 UCSD and nowhere else.
