Pren Halvard was a sub-officer of the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/)' detachment at [Threshold Station](/star-rangers/lore/threshold-station/) in 2815 UCSD. The one document in Halvard's name is the first entry in the longest-running file the station has: Incident Report 001, on two wall chronometers in Dock Seven that would not agree on the hour.

The report is short and does what the [protocol in force](/star-rangers/codex/survey-corps-protocol/) asked of it. It names the units, states the gap, says what was done, and recommends what to do next. The station's own lore calls its author *a reporting officer* and credits the finding to patience rather than alarm: two instruments that each read true, compared, and the difference filed.

## Known History

- 2815 UCSD, Late Summer, Day 47: filed *[Incident Report 001 — Dock Seven Chronometer Discrepancy](/star-rangers/codex/incident-report-001/)*. Units C-7A and C-7B differed by forty seconds. Both had been confirmed accurate within half a second on Day 12 of the same season. The discrepancy had been present at least three days, first noticed on Day 44, and no mechanical cause had been identified. Halvard recalibrated both units, recorded that the discrepancy cleared at the time of filing, and recommended monitoring for recurrence.
- The protocol asks for an Incident Report within twenty-four hours of an anomalous reading. The report was filed three days after the discrepancy was first noticed. The record does not say what happened in between.
- Day 49: the discrepancy returned, logged by [Technician N. Isern](/star-rangers/codex/maintenance-log-day-49-dock-seven/) at 03:10 with a recommendation to reopen the report. Day 52: Station Manager Cors Delveyne closed it as a calibration issue, and [Watch Officer Mera Soln](/star-rangers/characters/mera-soln/) filed an addendum the same day. The station's lore says the reporting officer *kept filing it after it had been closed*; the Codex carries the later filings under other names, and the record holds no second filing by Halvard.
- 2826 UCSD: the Survey Archive appended a note recording that the Day 49 return was never filed as an incident and survives only as one line in the maintenance log. The report itself is a single physical copy in the Threshold Station Records Vault with no off-station backup on file.

## Character Notes

Halvard's report is exact about what was measured and modest about what it meant. It claims no cause, offers no reading, and recommends monitoring, which is what the next eleven years did. The sentence *no mechanical cause has been identified* was true when it was filed and has stayed true through every recalibration since; the report's one recommendation was the right one, and the file it opened was closed over it.

The record holds nothing of Halvard beyond the one report: no origin, no species stated, no rank before or after, no later document. The record places Halvard in the Threshold Station detachment in Late Summer 2815 UCSD and nowhere after.
