Senna Okoye drafted the Exploration Budget doctrine for the Doctrine Office of the AI Governance Commission, the Concord body that holds the commissioning gate under the [Kernel Accords](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/). The doctrine was issued in 2701 UCSD, seven years after the Accords were ratified, and it superseded the narrow-scope [Allocation Units of Cognition](/star-rangers/glossary/ai-usage-credits-auc/) ledger, which had counted machine-assisted thought as if thought were fuel.

Her desk was a doctrinal one. The document she wrote is not a protocol and names no triggers; it sets out a mechanism in four layers, from entropy through variability to risk, and an accounting rule that splits every system's uncertainty into a necessary share and a contingent share. Its one sentence of doctrine is the line the Office still quotes: every engine of discovery spends order to buy possibility.

## Known History

- 2701 UCSD: drafted the [Exploration Budget (EB) Doctrine](/star-rangers/codex/exploration-budget-doctrine/), issued at Concord level in the doctrinal wake of the Kernel Accords of 2694 UCSD, and still in force. The AUC ledger remained in force as one instrument inside the larger framework. The doctrine places itself against the [Creative Entropy Index](/star-rangers/glossary/creative-entropy-index-cei/), the [Entropy Horizon](/star-rangers/glossary/entropy-horizon/) and the [Creative Entropy Framework](/star-rangers/lore/creative-entropy-framework/).
- 2704 UCSD: the [Redline Protocol](/star-rangers/codex/redline-protocol/) was issued under her doctrine by [Kwame Reyes-Osei](/star-rangers/characters/kwame-reyes-osei/) of the Commission's Contingency Division. It carries her four layers and her accounting identity into an operational trigger set. The record does not say whether she had any hand in it.
- 2826 UCSD: the Survey Archive's archive pass notes that field offices still complain the doctrine merely renamed AUC and made it philosophical, and that the Doctrine Office's standing reply has not changed in over a century: renaming was not the point; accounting for the contingent share of harm was.

## Character Notes

The temperament is in the drafting. Okoye does not argue that harm can be removed; she argues that some of it is structural and the rest is somebody's negligence, and that the job is to tell the two apart. Her fourth layer calls that both a measurement problem and an ethics problem in the same sentence, and declines to rank them. The doctrine seeks no zero and promises none. It asks for the minimum contingent entropy compatible with the mission, which is a modest sentence to put at the head of a Concord-level instrument, and a hard one to satisfy.

The record places her at the Doctrine Office in 2701 UCSD and nowhere else. It gives no birth, no age, no species, no earlier post and no later one, and does not say what became of her. The complaint the Office still answers in 2826 UCSD is answered by the Office, not by her.
