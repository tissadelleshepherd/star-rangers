Petra Solano is remembered for nine seconds and owed credit for four years, and she never once asked for it.

Halyard Station's own consultative council sent her to the Outer Stations Consolidation Hearing with one instruction: *come back with something the station can use*. She had spent four years learning to recognise, in that council, exactly the kind of arrangement the hearing spent eleven weeks describing.

## Known History

- In the eleventh week of testimony, 2719 UCSD, at Solar Command on Mars, General [Maren Solveig Krast](/star-rangers/characters/maren-solveig-krast/) answered the chair's question honestly, and Solano's thumb was already on the recording unit before she had consciously decided anything. She filed the nine seconds for release to Halyard's own station feed within the hour, with a two-line note: *This is the sentence the hearing needed eleven weeks to produce. Everything else was true. This is the part that's memorable.* She was the first of the three civilian observers who recorded the address without the chair's authorisation; it reached twenty-seven outer-station news services within forty-eight hours. [The Volunteered Word (S00E02C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e02/s00e02c02/).
- She filed her formal testimony two days later. Nobody outside the chamber ever asked her about it. Everybody, eventually, asked her about the nine seconds, and her own unease, not fully examined at the time, was that a soundbite would do the political work the findings deserved to do on their own merits. [Sildron Drey](/star-rangers/characters/sildron-drey/) thought the release correct and thought it had taught every institution watching the wrong lesson about what makes a true thing land.
- On Signing Day, 2723 UCSD, in the Charter Ratification Chamber, she struck *in exceptional circumstances* from the jurisdiction limits for the third time that morning, against Commodore [Rutger Voss](/star-rangers/characters/rutger-voss/) of the Solar System Defence Command. *Every exception clause this body has ever written has become the paragraph the exception swallows the rule inside of.* And the mandate she wrote in its place: broad enough to save the habitat and narrow enough that it can never be pointed at one. *Humanitarian. Operational. No governance, no policing, no side in anyone's dispute.* The limits were not revised. She signed her delegation's copy without ceremony. [What They Were Forbidden to Become (S00E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e03/s00e03c01/).

## Character Notes

The outer-station delegations demanded the charter's constitutional limits as the price of their support, and without their votes the Accords and the charter would have failed. Solano is the record's named instance of that demand, and the jurisdiction limits that still bind every Ranger are, by the prose's own account, in her words.

Halyard Station appears in no lore page. Governor [Petra Voss](/star-rangers/characters/petra-voss/) of Eden, a century later, shares her given name; whether it honours the Delegate, no record settles, and this page claims nothing either way. Solano is placed nowhere after 2723 UCSD.
