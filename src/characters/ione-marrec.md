Sister Ione Marrec is a contemplative of the [Fellowship of Light](/star-rangers/lore/fellowship-of-light/) and a correspondent to its comparative archive: the cross-chapter filing of accounts of a witness who names a boundary truth no local authority will, catalogued under the shorthand [Arilon](/star-rangers/lore/arilon/). Her filing is held at the [Eden](/star-rangers/lore/eden-space-habitat/) chapter house. The record holds two documents of hers from 2831 UCSD, one written and one spoken, and they are the same argument twice: once in the archive's register, and once by mouth to a cadet at the back of a hall.

The written one is a comparative-archive note. It draws a line from an Old-Earth scriptural pair — the prophet Elijah, already among the Arilon comparanda, and the captain Jehu, the hand that answered him and stopped short — to two figures in Solar System political memory: the outer-station critics who named a truth at the time and to no avail, and General [Maren Solveig Krast](/star-rangers/characters/maren-solveig-krast/), whose one volunteered word founded an order that ended one failure by keeping its deeper one. The note claims only what the file ever claims: the shape recurs, and the shape is not a verdict.

## Known History

- Comparative-archive correspondent of the Fellowship's contemplative chapter. Her note was circulated for cross-chapter comment in 2831 UCSD, not adopted and not settled, and its current filing is held at the Eden chapter house. [Jehu Among the Comparanda](/star-rangers/codex/jehu-among-the-comparanda/).
- Spoke at the Eden Space Habitat cadet commissioning in 2831 UCSD, when a cadet asked why the Rangers close with the [Hymn of the Thin Places](/star-rangers/codex/hymn-of-the-thin-places/), a song about a prophet nobody in the room worships. She told the twinned story and named the general nowhere in it; the cadet put the name in herself. The transcription was set down at Sister Ione's request, with the cadet's question kept in it, since in her account the question is the better half. [The Captain Who Drove Furiously](/star-rangers/codex/the-captain-who-drove-furiously/).
- Named in Early Spring 2833 UCSD by [Sen](/star-rangers/characters/sen/), Senior Archivist of the Survey Archive, in a submission to the same archive, prepared and withdrawn: "Sister Marrec's comparandum carries her name on its first line, as it should, because a cross-reference is a claim and a claim needs somebody accountable for it." [Shepherd Among the Comparanda](/star-rangers/codex/shepherd-among-the-comparanda/).

## Character Notes

She signs what she files and considers the telling nobody's possession, hers least of all. The note extends the Cascade's discipline past where the file requires it, declining to read the hand that answers the witness as a verdict any more than the witness; the telling hands a cadet the half of the story no song is written for — "you have to keep the second yourself, quietly". Both documents say what they do not claim, and stop there.

The record does not give her species, her age, how long she has been at the Eden chapter house, or where she was before it. The two filings of 2831 UCSD and Sen's mention of 2833 UCSD are the whole of what it holds.
