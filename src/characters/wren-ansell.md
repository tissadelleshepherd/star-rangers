Wren Ansell holds the office of Chief Surveyor at the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-survey-corps/) Standards Office on Earth. The title is a standards-and-doctrine authority, not a rung on the rank ladder: a Chief Surveyor outranks no one on the tab count and can still overrule a Line Captain's instrument-certification decision, because tab seniority and subject-matter authority answer different questions. That is [the record's description of the office](/star-rangers/lore/rank-insignia-and-uniform/), and Ansell is the name it attaches to it.

The one document in Ansell's name is a protocol. It is written as protocols are, in numbered sections, and argues nothing; it tells a Threshold-class station what to keep, what to log, and whom to tell.

## Known History

- 2811 UCSD: issued the *[Survey Corps Boundary Monitoring Protocol — Threshold Class Stations](/star-rangers/codex/survey-corps-protocol/)*, master copy at the Standards Office, mirrored to every Threshold-class station by courier. Section 2 requires two independently calibrated chronometers and the logging of any discrepancy over one second. Section 3 gives station managers full authority to close an Incident Report without external review. Section 4 reserves Etheric-layer assessment to a certified analyst.
- The protocol's current version is 2.3. The record does not say who revised it or when.
- 2815 UCSD: [Incident Report 001](/star-rangers/codex/incident-report-001/) at Threshold Station was filed under this protocol and closed under its Section 3 as a calibration issue. The protocol's margin carries an unsigned note that the two-chronometer requirement was never followed with guidance on a discrepancy that does not resolve on recalibration. The note is not in Ansell's name.
- 2826 UCSD: an unsigned appendix note records that Threshold Station has no certified analyst on its establishment, and that the certification of the Krenyi analyst [Syra](/star-rangers/characters/syra/) is pending review within Survey Corps jurisdiction.

## Character Notes

What the record shows of Ansell is the shape of the document, and the document is careful about instruments and silent about what to do when the instruments disagree and stay disagreed. The protocol asks for two chronometers so that one can check the other; it does not say what a station should conclude when both read true and still differ. The margin note, by another hand, says exactly that, and the eleven years of Dock Seven are what the gap looked like from inside. None of this is a finding about the Chief Surveyor; it is what one protocol, read beside the file it governed, does and does not provide for.

The record holds nothing of Ansell beyond the office and the document: no origin, no species stated, no other filing. The rank page names Ansell as holding the office in the record's own present; by date, the Codex places Ansell at the Standards Office in 2811 UCSD and nowhere else.
