Rutger Voss asked for the worst posting a junior lieutenant could get, because it was the one closest to the paper. Eight years later he signed away the argument he had made for his own service, on the merits, and the record has him at both desks.

He was younger than Adaeze Okafor expected. He had read the file: six names, one classification, and a committee structure that had taken eleven months to answer a fold-route anomaly report before her sister ever launched. He was not defending it to her. He did not currently have a rank that let him close it.

## Known History

- Fresh from training in 2715 UCSD, he asked for the [Military Space Command's](/star-rangers/lore/military-space-command/) Casualty Correspondence Office at Solar Command, Mars, because the alternatives were further from the file and he wanted to be close to the actual paper this particular institutional failure generated. [Adaeze Okafor](/star-rangers/characters/adaeze-okafor/) was the fourth family member to sit across his desk in eleven months. He filed her visit, verbatim where he could manage it, into a running correspondence log the office kept for exactly this purpose and forwarded to no one who had ever asked to see it. *An institution that keeps refusing to answer the same question, from six different families, in writing, for years, is building a record whether it means to or not.* [Unauthorised, Therefore (S00E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e02/s00e02c01/).
- He was in the gallery of the Outer Stations Consolidation Hearing in 2719 UCSD, eleven weeks into a hearing he had expected to end in three, when General [Maren Solveig Krast](/star-rangers/characters/maren-solveig-krast/), three ranks above him for most of his service, said the sentence.
- As a Commodore of the Solar System Defence Command on Signing Day, 2723 UCSD, he argued for an exceptional-circumstances clause in the charter's jurisdiction limits, citing Kessler Anchorage eleven years earlier, where two engineers died waiting for an authorisation chain to clear under the command structure's own predecessor. *Strike it and you get a corps that has to stand outside a burning habitat and ask permission to enter.* He lost the argument to Delegate [Petra Solano](/star-rangers/characters/petra-solano/), who had never once raised her voice to win it, and signed his section of the draft that afternoon without the clause in it. He did not describe this to anyone, afterward, as a concession. [What They Were Forbidden to Become (S00E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e03/s00e03c01/).

## Character Notes

He had spent his whole service believing that competence, properly credentialed and properly supervised, was the load-bearing wall of any institution worth trusting with other people's safety. He still believed it on Signing Day. What he no longer believed was that competence was sufficient by itself, and the difference is the charter.

He shares a surname with Governor [Petra Voss](/star-rangers/characters/petra-voss/) of Eden a century later. Whether the Governor descends from the Commodore, and whether her given name honours the Delegate, no record that has surfaced settles; her page claims neither and declines to claim their absence, and this page keeps the same silence. Kessler Anchorage appears nowhere else in the record. He is placed nowhere after 2723 UCSD.
