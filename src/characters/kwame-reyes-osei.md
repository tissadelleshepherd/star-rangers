Kwame Reyes-Osei drafted the Redline Protocol for the Contingency Division of the AI Governance Commission, issued in 2704 UCSD under the [Exploration Budget doctrine](/star-rangers/codex/exploration-budget-doctrine/) that [Senna Okoye](/star-rangers/characters/senna-okoye/) had written three years before, and under the Doctrine Office's authority as exercised within the [Kernel Accords](/star-rangers/lore/ai-safety-kernel/) invariant hierarchy.

Where the doctrine describes, the protocol decides. Its first sentence is its whole rule: when contingent harm rises faster than verified capability gain, command shifts from acceleration to containment. The rest is the machinery for knowing when that has happened, three triggers, four mandatory actions, and the condition for opening the channels again.

## Known History

- 2704 UCSD: drafted the [Redline Protocol](/star-rangers/codex/redline-protocol/), Variability Governance. It takes Okoye's four layers and her entropy identity as given, and adds what the doctrine lacked: an escalation rule (escalate when the contingent component dominates the trendline), three named triggers, and the actions a redline obliges, from freezing open-ended generation to requiring adversarial checks before any re-opening. It closes on a standing ethical test, which asks what fraction of suffering is structurally required and what fraction is reducible, and states that policy failure begins when the question goes unmeasured.
- The protocol has been maintained through the 2826 UCSD archive pass, which records that it has been invoked eleven times since ratification. In nine of the eleven, the trigger was the third, an incentive loop rewarding novelty over reliability, rather than any technical failure.

## Character Notes

Reyes-Osei writes in imperatives and lists, and the shortest sentences on the page are the ones with the most weight. His third trigger, the incentive loop, is the one the record shows firing most often, and it is the only trigger of the three that names a human arrangement rather than a system's output. That is a choice about where to look for harm, and the hundred and twenty years of invocations since have borne it out. His closing test is written as an ethical question and filed as an operational one; he does not separate the two.

The record places him at the Contingency Division in 2704 UCSD and nowhere else. It gives no birth, no age, no species, no earlier post and no later one, and does not say what became of him. Whether he and Okoye worked together, or ever met, the record does not say.
