She arrives after the physicians have gone, which is not a criticism of the physicians. They treated what was there to treat. Sorcha's work begins with the part of the case that did not resolve when the injury did.

She carries a satchel with two compartments, and she is unsentimental about the difference. One holds ordinary medicine — sutures, tinctures, clean cloth, the boring apparatus that saves most of the lives anyone saves. The other holds chalk, thread, measured water, and a notebook. She does not confuse them, and she is short with anyone who does.

## What She Actually Does

Sorcha is **[constraint-literate](/star-rangers/glossary/constraint-literacy/)**, self-taught and incomplete, in the same tradition as [Elvira's](/star-rangers/characters/elvira/) boundary practice — with one difference that matters. Elvira reads the [Etheric](/star-rangers/glossary/etheric/) structures expressed through a *place*. Sorcha reads them as they express through a *person*.

The mechanism is the same and lawful. Pattern persists after the physical trigger is removed; that is the Etheric layer's documented signature, not a mystery. In a boundary-proximate settlement, where local conditions already make [Etheric-based practice unreliable](/star-rangers/glossary/boundary-zone/), a grief or a terror can hold a shape long after the event that made it has stopped happening. Sorcha maps that shape, tests where it will and will not move, and works at the edges she can find. Sometimes it releases. She writes down the times it does not, in the same hand and the same detail, which is the part of the notebook most people never ask to see.

**She does not heal the spirit.** People say she does, and she has stopped correcting them in the moment, because a person in the worst week of their life does not need a lecture on the [Five Layers](/star-rangers/lore/five-layers/). But the Spiritual layer is an origin condition without agency or intervention semantics — nothing there answers, and nothing there can be treated. What she works on sits at the Conceptual and Etheric layers, where patterns of meaning hold or fail to hold. The distinction is not pedantry to her. It is the difference between a practice with a mechanism and a practice with a reputation.

## Why She Goes Toward the Bad Places

Her reason is not compassion in the abstract, though she has that too. It is that unresolved pain is a **feeding condition**, and she learned this before she had the vocabulary for it.

[Threnes](/star-rangers/lore/meta-dimensional-beings/) feed on grief that is prevented from resolving. [Gilded Saints](/star-rangers/glossary/gilded-saints/) work the same trick keyed to virtue rather than sorrow. Both prosper exactly where mourning is held open and never closed — which means a settlement that cannot finish grieving is not merely sad. It is *provisioned*.

Sorcha's practice, whatever else it is, is closure work. That puts her in direct functional opposition to an entire class of meta-dimensional predator, and she has been doing it for years without filing a single report that uses any of those words. Survey Corps has no record of her. Nothing about her method requires knowing what she is starving.

## Character Notes

She does not judge, and this is a working method rather than a virtue. Judgement closes a case early. She has watched a settlement decide what happened to someone, and watched the decision harden into a story, and watched the story become the thing that would not let the person put it down. She goes in without a verdict because a verdict is one more shape the pattern can hold.

She wants the truth, and she is exact about why. Not for justice, and never for vengeance — vengeance keeps the wound articulate, which is the opposite of her purpose. She wants the truth because a person cannot set down what they cannot yet describe. Naming a thing accurately is the first move of letting go of it, and every step after that is easier.

Her gentleness is not softness. She has stood between an armed man and a frightened one and not raised her voice, on the working theory that whoever escalates first has agreed to be the problem. It has failed at least once. She does not talk about that.

## The Cost

Her weakness is **central fatigue**, and the word is exact rather than figurative.

The Medical Corps already screens for it. [Teleportation Constraints](/star-rangers/lore/teleportation-limitations/) documents it as a consequence of repeated jump exposure; [Dr. Iona Vale](/star-rangers/characters/dr-iona-vale/) gates jumps on it at Threshold Station — *if the crew member shows unresolved central fatigue markers, the jump does not happen* — and her clinical case is blunt about the arithmetic: repeated exposure burns through central fatigue reserves faster than any rest cycle replaces them. It is not tiredness. It is a nervous system spending down a reserve, measurably, on a curve.

Sorcha runs the same deficit from a different cause. Reading Etheric structure as it expresses through a distressed person is sustained perceptual work, and hers has no off switch — she has never learned to hold a case at arm's length, and by now it is not obvious the skill would still be available to her if she tried. Caring is not incidental to her method. It is the instrument. So the instrument is what wears.

**Nobody screens her.** The protocol Iona Vale fought a room to establish covers Rangers, and Sorcha is not one, has no certification, and answers to no medical officer anywhere. The measure exists, the thresholds exist, the mandatory intake exists — and the one practitioner in the setting whose entire method is a sustained draw on that reserve is outside every last one of them. She keeps her own notebook. It records what she treated. It does not record what it cost her, because she has never thought to make that column.

What this looks like from outside is a woman who works until she is grey and then works the next case. What it looks like from inside is not documented anywhere, by anyone, including her.

## Open Threads

**She has no institutional standing at all.** No Survey Corps certification, no guild, no charter, no chain of command. This makes her useful in places those institutions cannot reach and completely unprotected in every other respect. Nothing has yet tested what happens when her practice meets an authority that wants an accounting from her.

**She has never been told what she is counter to.** She has read enough boundary literature to have a working model of Etheric expression, and none at all of the entities that profit from it. What happens the first time something she is starving notices her is unwritten.

**The two facts above multiply, and nothing in the record has tested it.** Her practice starves a class of predator that feeds on prevented closure. Her practice also spends down a reserve that rest does not fully replace. Which means the hours when she is least able to read what is in a room are, precisely, the hours she has earned by working — and a thing that feeds on unresolved grief has every reason to prefer a closure practitioner who has run herself empty. Whether anything has ever waited for that is not established. Only that the arithmetic permits it.

**Where she is from is not established here**, deliberately. She works boundary-proximate settlements across more than one world, and no page yet places her origin, her training, or her arrival.

*A note on naming, for anyone cross-referencing the roster:* this is the Sorcha the record files under the name unqualified, which reflects the order things were written down rather than any precedence. Two other women carry it — [Sorcha Nic Dhiarmada](/star-rangers/characters/sorcha-nic-dhiarmada/), valley warden of Gleann na gCaorach on Drithane, and [Sorcha Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/sorcha-shepherd/), land-registry surveyor on Tír na nÓg — and those two are unrelated to each other as well. Note what the coincidence does and does not settle: the shared name is not a thread back to either of those worlds, and it is equally not evidence about where this Sorcha is from, which the paragraph above leaves open on purpose. A name occurring three times establishes nothing about any of the three.
