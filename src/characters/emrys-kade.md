Emrys Kade is a theorist of the Doctrinal Division of the [Survey Archive](/star-rangers/lore/the-archive/) at [Threshold Station](/star-rangers/lore/threshold-station/), and the author of five working papers in the Archive's doctrinal series, filed between 2822 and 2824 UCSD. The Division keeps the doctrinal file on the [Cascade](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/), and the papers do what Kade says its instruments cannot: state the shape the record shows, name what the Division cannot test, and file the inability with everything else it cannot close.

The desk is a correspondence desk as much as a theorist's. The same letter arrives every few cycles, Kade writes, asking why a template that permits life did not forbid pain, and the papers are written for the people who send it and for the devotional traditions whose annotations sit on the working copies.

## Known History

- 2822 UCSD: the current revision of [The Cosmic Limitation on Evil](/star-rangers/codex/cosmic-limitation-on-evil/), the paper the Division revises every few cycles and has never once shortened. It calls the Limitation the one confirmed exception to the Cascade's no-moral-cosmology rule. Filed contested.
- 2823 UCSD: an unattributed devotional correspondent answered the revised filing in [The Better Side of Intervention](/star-rangers/codex/the-better-side-of-intervention/), addressed to Theorist Kade by name and filed unendorsed. The Division's position was unchanged.
- 2823 UCSD: [Neither Author Nor Warden](/star-rangers/codex/neither-author-nor-warden/), the answer to the petitions: biological life is a [formtype](/star-rangers/glossary/formtype/), a template holds neither the warden's office nor the author's, and the record's one law with a moral face is kept higher than the tier the petitions address. Filed as doctrine.
- 2823 UCSD: [The Borrowed Silhouette](/star-rangers/codex/the-borrowed-silhouette/), formerly *The False Dragon*: the adversary-figure of the devotional codices would have to be founded in order to command and [Unfounded](/star-rangers/glossary/unfounded/) in order to be the adversary, so the description picks nothing out.
- 2824 UCSD: [Called, Permitted, Forbidden](/star-rangers/codex/called-permitted-forbidden/): the forbidden is enforced impersonally, the calling is personal but unenforced, and nothing in between is judged at all. The archivist's note calls it the third of Kade's papers to end by admitting the file cannot be closed.
- 2824 UCSD: [The House on the Sand](/star-rangers/codex/the-house-on-the-sand/), the parable of the two builders read as the scriptures' most exact statement of the Unfounded, closing on an Arilonian annotation to which the Division entered no reply.

## Character Notes

Kade writes in the plural of a division and seldom in the singular. The one place the singular surfaces is the admission, in *Neither Author Nor Warden*, that the section on the ledger is the part its author is least comfortable writing. That discomfort runs through all five papers: the Limitation looks like a moral law, the Invariants say there is none, and Kade will neither rephrase the law away nor adopt the devotional reading. The habit of declining to name the institutions that make the misreading, because the pattern matters more than the examples, is of a piece. So is the readiness to let a correct annotation stand unanswered, on the grounds that a doctrine office which could not recognise one would be building on sand.

The record gives Kade no species, no age, no home and no history before the Division. The papers run from 2822 to 2824 UCSD; the record places their author nowhere after, and carries no status.
