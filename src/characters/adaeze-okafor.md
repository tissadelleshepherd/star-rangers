Adaeze Okafor is the civilian voice of the founding era: one sister, one form letter, ten replies, and a desk on Mars.

The form letter arrived eleven months after *Patience First* vanished. *The Military Space Command regrets to confirm that the vessel Patience First, having departed Eden Space Habitat without operational authorisation, falls outside the scope of Command liability, inquiry findings, or survivor support. This correspondence closes the matter absent new information.* She wrote back nine times, and the tenth letter went unanswered entirely, so she took the transit to Mars herself, on savings that would not survive a second trip.

## Known History

- Sister of [Wren Okafor](/star-rangers/characters/wren-okafor/), boundary analyst of *Patience First*, which departed Eden in 2714 UCSD with six aboard and did not return.
- In 2715 UCSD, one year after, she sat across a desk in the MSC Casualty Correspondence Office at Solar Command, Mars, from Lieutenant [Rutger Voss](/star-rangers/characters/rutger-voss/), the fourth family member to do so in eleven months and the only one who had made the trip in person. *I'm not here about liability, Lieutenant. I'm here because "unauthorised, therefore" is not an answer to the only question I actually asked, which was what happened to her.* [Unauthorised, Therefore (S00E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s00/e02/s00e02c01/).
- Her last line to him is the one the record keeps: *Then keep the record. Since apparently that's the only thing either of us actually has the authority to do.* He did.
- The sustained correspondence and legal pressure of the six crew members' families, unresolved for years, is part of what eventually forced the Outer Stations Consolidation Hearing of 2719 UCSD. Her letters are the record's instance of it.

## Character Notes

She appears in one scene, and the Archive names her nowhere else. The record places her nowhere after 2715 UCSD, and says nothing of what she learned in 2732, when her sister's ship was found and the crew were alive.
