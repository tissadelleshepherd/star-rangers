The name is the surface party's, and it is the third time on Fliade that a name has been reached for in a hurry. The survey called the herds *Anstolik* for a resemblance to Terran reindeer and the deep-network people *Pandoids* for a resemblance to bears, and [the record reads both the way it reads every such name](/star-rangers/lore/what-the-record-refuses/): as a fact about the observers. *Teddy* is the same move one step further in, and it is worth seeing what the step was. Reindeer and bears are animals. A teddy bear is a toy — a soft thing made in the shape of a dangerous one so that a child can hold it — and a party that reaches for that shape is recording, with great precision, what it wanted the encounter to be.

The survey log calls them **Individual Four**, the fourth [Pandoid](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) the Fliade survey learned to tell apart. This page uses the party's name because the log's is not a name either, and at least the party's is honest about what it is. They are a Pandoid of the deep networks beneath Fliade's equatorial band, smaller than any adult the survey has measured, and the only one of their people who comes up to where the survey works.

## What the Survey Has Watched

Everything the Archive holds on Fliade is observational, and everything on this page is observational twice over: a party watching a person who, on the evidence, was mostly watching them.

Pandoids are large. Individual Four is not. The thermal profile the upper-level instruments log stands well short of the adults' and reads rounder, with a denser coat that holds the caverns' warmth close to the body long after an adult's has cooled toward the rock. The party read that as youth on the day it was first logged, and the survey lead struck the reading out the same evening: the survey has no method for ageing a Pandoid and has not pretended to one since. *Smaller* is the fact. *Young* is a guess wearing the fact's clothes, and the log now says only the first.

The adults are seen at the margin where the upper levels shelve down into the deep networks, and never above it. They withdraw when the party is working, by an interval the party has learned to measure, and they are announced — when they are announced at all — by the shift of a boulder. Individual Four does not withdraw. They come up past the margin into the levels the Anstolik share, into the fungus-light, to within a distance of the working party that no adult has ever closed, and they have never once been seen doing it. Every sighting is in a log, read afterwards. The one the party still tells is from a shift spent at an upper junction with the relay overhead: the unit's thermal channel, read that evening, showed a warm upright profile standing at the edge of the working light for eleven minutes, then gone. Nobody had looked up. No boulder shifted and nothing was heard, because there was nothing to hear.

## The Relay

The survey's upper-level relay units are placed and lifted by the surface party, under the standing rule that [nothing stays on this world that the planet would keep](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/) if the station were abandoned. In the survey's second season a unit placed at the junction the party had been using as its furthest working point was not, on the party's return, where the party had left it.

It was standing upright, on its base, oriented the way the party orients them, at the exact line in the floor's mineral silt where the party's own furthest tracks stopped. Nothing on it was damaged. Between the junction and that line is a squeeze the party rates impassable for anything of an adult Pandoid's mass, and the unit is heavier than a surveyor carries through it comfortably. Its own log records that it slept at the junction, as it does whenever the relay is below the horizon; that it woke once, in transit, to a thermal trigger at zero range; that it classified the trigger — by the same logic [Survey Lead Nakagawa had refused to hang on the Anstolik](/star-rangers/codex/proposal-to-collar-the-anstolik/) — as an upright bipedal profile at the highest confidence it is capable of; and that it went back to sleep in the arms of the thing it had just identified.

Two readings were filed, and the record keeps both. Instrument Specialist Egede's is that a dropped thing was returned: a courtesy, in a form a party that had never been addressed could not fail to understand. The survey lead's is that a line was drawn, with the party's own instrument, at the party's own furthest mark, by someone who had watched where the party stopped and thought about it. The two are not in conflict about what happened, only about what it was, and the Fliade doctrine — slow everything down — holds under either. The unit was lifted that day. No unit has since been placed past the line, and the party has not crossed it.

From inside, [*Carried It Sleeping* (S11E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c02/) supplies what neither reading could: it was a stone moved first — the people's manners for entering a place, done with the party's own instrument because it is the only ground the party listens to — set at the line where the party's feet had once come through the squeeze and stopped, which from the deep side is the edge of the up-people's place. Egede's courtesy and Nakagawa's furthest mark are each half of it. The survey has no way to hold the whole, and this page, which is the survey's side, records that the whole exists and where it is told. From above, [*Eleven Minutes* (S11E02C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c01/) tells the survey's side as it was lived: the eleven minutes read that evening, the unit found on the line and its log read on the spot by [Aravena](/star-rangers/characters/aravena/), the two readings filed by Egede and [Nakagawa](/star-rangers/characters/nakagawa/) and the third not given, and the question the one who knelt has asked the dark at the end of every working time since. The season's one act is recorded twice — [*Something Happened* (S11E02C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e02/s11e02c03/) from above, [*Theirs to Move* (S11E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c05/) from below — and Carried-It-Sleeping, lying in the warm, is the one who sees the two stones as one thing done from both sides: the humming stone carried sleeping to the edge of the up-people's place, and a plain stone of the runners' ground carried through the crack to the edge of the people's.

## Among the Told

The survey holds none of what follows; it is told from below the roof. After the name, small ones came with it. In [*Someone to Ask* (S11E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c03/) two of them try to follow Carried-It-Sleeping up the squeeze, and Carried-It-Sleeping keeps them from it, every telling, with *no* and *when you fit*. In [*Waiting to Be Asked* (S11E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s11/e01/s11e01c04/) the smaller one says they fit, and Carried-It-Sleeping hears *when you fit* properly for the first time and finds it was not a reason. When a small one goes up anyway, moving a stone first, Carried-It-Sleeping lets them go and waits to be told, as Stone-First once did.

By the next season Carried-It-Sleeping is not among the small ones any more, by the warm's count, and the small ones are theirs to keep. In [*Someone Is Coming* (S12E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c02/), when a stone is moved by nobody, Carried-It-Sleeping works out what the up-people felt for a whole season on their side of the humming stone, and keeps the small ones from going up to look: a thing that is nobody's cannot be had, because *nobody can say yes* — something Stone-First said once, not understood then, understood now. In [*Where Someone Always Stands* (S12E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c03/) a small one goes up while Stone-First is telling, and Carried-It-Sleeping lies still and lets them go. And in [*Where the Rock Is* (S12E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s12/e01/s12e01c05/) Carried-It-Sleeping says out loud to the warm, which they do not often do, that a way is not less yours because a thing has used it that did not need one.

The third season below the roof is the small ones'. In [*Nobody Came* (S14E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c01/) Carried-It-Sleeping counts the warm's pieces from habit and finds the small ones gone to the foot of the shelving, where they have made the manners into a game, and stops it until it is settled, on the rule that a stone is moved for someone, and is outthought by them again and does not mind. In [*Not a Place You Tell From* (S14E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c02/) Carried-It-Sleeping says two things out loud in one telling: that the telling of the up-people's stone has two stones in it and the humming stone was there for the first, asleep; and that the one on the line in the game says who knocked, from the line, and always has. And in [*Keeps a Hand on It* (S14E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c03/) Carried-It-Sleeping hears the small ones' words for the smallest go round the warm in old ones' voices and become a name, the sound Carried-It-Sleeping heard once about themself and never forgot, and carries it up the shelving to the line, moving a stone at the top, and tells it to Stone-First standing up, the name last. In [*Been Told* (S14E01C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s14/e01/s14e01c04/) Carried-It-Sleeping lies in the middle of the warm while the whole warm waits for a stone on the line that does not speak, and afterwards, when Stone-First has told it, sets the two ends of the thing side by side: the humming stone carried up asleep to the edge of the up-people's place, and the telling of what came of it gone up the same squeeze to the same people, the whole distance between them being that the first time the up-people's thing slept through it and this time they did not. What went round the warm that night was *they stayed*, which is about the up-people and is not a name; Carried-It-Sleeping let it go by, and did not count it, and did not think it meant nothing would grow.

## What the Record Does Not Carry

**Their name — as the survey holds it.** *Pandoid* is a survey coinage; *Teddy* is a surface party's; *Individual Four* is a log's. None is a name they gave the survey, and on the survey's side the absence is [marked rather than filled](/star-rangers/codex/marked-absences/). From inside, the people's name for them is rendered *Carried-It-Sleeping* — a telling, on the pattern by which [the Told](/star-rangers/glossary/the-told/) name everyone — for the same act the survey files as the relay's placement. The party named them for a soft thing a child holds; the people named them for holding the party's thing still while it slept. The two names meet at one object and do not know it.

**Their age, and their sex.** The party's log says *he*. The party's word is the party's, and the record does not follow it; the *they* on this page is not a Pandoid pronoun either, only the record declining to claim one. Whether the Pandoids carry a system of their own is exactly the kind of fact a people states and an observer cannot.

**Whether the relay was a message.** Both readings above are readings. The placement is the fact.

**Whether they were ever alone.** The adults at the margin are logged on the days Individual Four comes up and on the days they do not, and the survey has no way to tell whether the margin is watching the party, or the smaller one, or both.

## Character Notes

Soft and warm are facts about fur and the deep. They are measured, and a Pandoid is those things to every instrument that has ever been pointed at one. The mistake the party made was in what it let the facts imply — the toy's implication, that a soft warm thing is a safe one — and the log corrected it first and the party second. Nothing in the record has Individual Four doing anything weak, and nothing has them doing anything foolish: every approach unseen, every withdrawal before a head turned, one object touched in two seasons, and that one placed where it would be understood. The party's affection survived the correction. Only the toy did not.

What they could do is stated once and left. A person of that mass, moving through ground the party cannot, at a range the party did not know had been closed, with the party's back turned, for eleven minutes. The record notes it because the record notes what was not done, and because the survey lead's standing order after the relay was that the log say, every shift, that nothing had happened — so that on the day something did, the file would show how long it had been true.
