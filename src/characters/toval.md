Toval is Krenyi, one of the Quiet-Built, and young by his people's reckoning, which they keep in their own calendar and he gives in the material form when asked: younger than sixty, by the Corps' conversion, which for a Krenyi is the beginning of a life rather than the middle of one. He holds Cadet standing in the [Survey Corps](/star-rangers/lore/star-rangers-academy/), confirmed by the college in 2831 UCSD, and is posted to [Threshold Station's](/star-rangers/lore/boundary-zones/) boundary desk under [Syra](/star-rangers/characters/syra/), who is training him.

He is the second Krenyi in the record to serve at Threshold and the first to arrive there as a trainee rather than as an answer to a wrong classification. [Krenyi are rare in the Corps](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/#in-the-corps-rare-and-valued), and a Cadet who is Krenyi is rarer still, not because the Corps declines them but because a people with a slow birth rate produces few candidates of any age. He came to Threshold the way his people come to most places, by deciding the work there was worth doing, and the Survey Corps recommended him from the desk where he had already been doing it.

## The habit of rounding up

The one thing Syra is training out of him is the habit of rounding up, and it is worth being exact about what that is, because it is not carelessness. The Krenyi are a people who expect to spend centuries becoming precise, and Toval has not had the centuries. What he has is the whole-body sensitivity of his kind, which reads a boundary more finely than any instrument on the desk, and a young Krenyi's reasonable wish for the number he writes down to sound as certain as the reading felt. Rounding up is what that wish does to a figure. Syra's correction, repeated until it stops being needed, is the Corps' own discipline in one line: *say only what the instruments carry.* A boundary finding is a measurement with error bars, and a sensation, however accurate, is not one.

He is quick, and the quickness is not eagerness; nothing in him jumps first. He is honest to the degree the Quiet-Built are, which means his questions come out before he has decided whether they are safe to ask, and Syra answers them for the same reason. He is briefly taken in by human social deceptions he has not met, recalibrates, and does not hold it against anyone. He has not yet learned to stop noticing when a room has started performing, and the Corps will not teach him that, since it is the thing it values him for.

## Dock Seven

On the morning of the [loss at Dock Seven](/star-rangers/seasons/s05/e02/s05e02c03/), 2831 UCSD, Toval was the second person on the boundary desk when the *Sufficient Cause* reported an aperture at forty kilometres, held open past transit. He started the question everyone on the station wanted asked, *and the*, and Syra finished it for him in the form that would survive review: Class III aggregate, in transit toward the aperture, non-sapient, no Cascade standing, nothing wrong with taking them, and the aperture the whole of the breach. He wrote it down. Syra kept her voice level so that he would write it well, and he did.

The record holds his log of that morning as one of the three instrument-side accounts of the event, beside [Rook-7's](/star-rangers/characters/rook-7/) uncertified arrays and the *Sufficient Cause*'s own telemetry, and it is the plainest of the three: readings, times, the floor under the fold signature going back four months, and nothing about dragons. He was not on the outer gallery. He saw nothing but the desk, and the desk carried what it carried. When the nine witnesses gave their statements he took two of them down, and the word they used went into the file exactly as they said it, because a Krenyi does not round a witness down any more than he rounds a reading up.

## Character Notes

He does not experience deep fear, and on that morning it showed as what it always shows as in his people, which is that his handwriting did not change.

He will go to the record for a measurement and decline it as an explanation, and he is already poor at keeping anything whose use has not been demonstrated, which Syra has noted as the second thing to train, after the rounding.

Asked where he is from, he gives the material answer and declines the rest. Asked why he chose the boundary desk, he says that the boundary is where the record is thinnest and the instruments are most often wrong, which is where a person who cannot help noticing is of most use.

## On the name

*Nai* before his name is the state-marker, *still relevant here*, and not a title. He is addressed as Nai Toval by the humans on the desk who have learned what it means, and as Cadet by the ones who have not, and he answers to both without correcting either, which Syra has told him is a habit he may keep.
