  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Verdani Delegate First Holds Verdance's Federation Seat</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2822 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2822 UCSD [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/), a [Verdani](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/) raised in the Understory Assembly's territory, first holds [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/)'s rotating seat in the [Federation](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) assembly, which the world takes in turn between other member worlds. She had been seated as the Canopy Concord's senior Fourfold Accord delegate in 2819 UCSD, and returns to the assembly seat twice afterwards, most recently in 2826 UCSD.

Her page counts her argument over the assembly's weighted-vote formula in Verdance's local years, eleven of them; the record holds no conversion of those years to UCSD.

**Canon facts:**
- Verdance is a full Federation member, represented on the assembly's rotation through the Fourfold Accord, the standing council of one delegate from each of its four governments.
- Farrowkin sponsored the Federation's first formal data-sharing proposal with Survey Corps fold-route mapping.

See: [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/), [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/), [Verdani](/star-rangers/lore/verdani/).
