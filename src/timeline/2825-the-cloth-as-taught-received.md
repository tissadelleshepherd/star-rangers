  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Krenyi Analyst's Account of the Cloth Received</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2825 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2825 UCSD the Survey Archive's Doctrinal Division, at Threshold Station, receives [*The Cloth, as Taught*](/star-rangers/codex/the-cloth-as-taught/) from its author, [Ilvane](/star-rangers/characters/ilvane/), a [Krenyi](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/) boundary analyst of the Survey Corps and a Champion selected by field signature. The Division had asked for it after her fold-approach recertification log called the fold she was certifying *a privilege of the cloth*. It is held as the source for the registry's entries on [Wholecloth](/star-rangers/lore/wholecloth-formcluster/) and [Selvage](/star-rangers/lore/selvage-archecluster/), graded *carried*.

The author dates the account by the station's calendar, as she says, because it is the one the reader has. The record holds no date for it in her people's own reckoning.

**Canon facts:**
- The teaching came over four sittings with a Levril at a boundary post, in the year after the post's instability.
- She set it down on two conditions, both kept: what was taught in the terms it was taught in and nothing of the teacher, and a grade no higher than the account holds.
- The record places Ilvane in the Survey Corps in 2825 UCSD and carries nothing later.

See: [The Cloth, as Taught](/star-rangers/codex/the-cloth-as-taught/), [Ilvane](/star-rangers/characters/ilvane/), [Krenyi](/star-rangers/lore/krenyi/).
