  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">Corryn's Expansion Reaches the Spiralites' Bands</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">~2796 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In about 2796 UCSD the human colony on [Corryn Prime](/star-rangers/lore/planets/corryn/), operated by the [Halvern Combine](/star-rangers/lore/halvern-combine/) under a charter the Imperium issued before it fell in 2609 UCSD, begins the mining and agricultural expansion that the planet does not absorb. Its emissions cross the electromagnetic bands the **Spiralites** use, degrading both the medium of their communication and the one their reproduction depends on. The year is the record's conversion from *roughly the last thirty years*, as the Corryn and Combine pages put it in 2826 UCSD.

The Spiralites' own dating of anything is not held by the record.

**Canon facts:**
- The settlement stood for the better part of two centuries at a scale the planet absorbed; the harm is recent.
- The Spiralites were recognised as sapient long ago and are not in dispute, so the Frontier Transformation Protocols' sapience clause applies on the plain reading.
- No office has standing to review the charter, and nobody with standing has visited.

See: [The Corryn System](/star-rangers/lore/planets/corryn/), [The Halvern Combine](/star-rangers/lore/halvern-combine/), [Frontier Transformation Protocols](/star-rangers/lore/frontier-transformation-protocols/).
