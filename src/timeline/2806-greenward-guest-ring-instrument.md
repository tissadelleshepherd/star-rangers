  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Reciprocal Instrument Concluded: Greenward and the Guest Ring</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">~2806 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

About 2806 UCSD the [Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) and the Star Rangers conclude a reciprocal instrument. Its human half is [Greenward](/star-rangers/lore/greenward-habitat/), a single ring above [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/), and its other half is [the Guest Ring](/star-rangers/lore/the-guest-ring/). The instrument belongs to the second generation of contact, after the material-support compact's first renewal. By then the parties had a working relationship, a compact and no dispute worth housing.

The year is the record's own arithmetic, not a date on file. Both pages say the arrangement has stood for twenty years at the record's present.

**Canon facts:**
- The instrument was concluded after the material-support compact's first renewal, and after the Federation's Oversight Council seat was first filled in 2791 UCSD.

See: [Greenward](/star-rangers/lore/greenward-habitat/), [The Guest Ring](/star-rangers/lore/the-guest-ring/), [The Federation's Seat on the Oversight Council](/star-rangers/timeline/2791-federation-council-seat/).
