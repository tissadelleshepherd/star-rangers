  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Proposal to Collar the Anstolik Refused</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2832 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2832 UCSD, the survey's second year on the ground on [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), Instrument Specialist [Toma Egede](/star-rangers/characters/toma-egede/) proposes fitting twelve Anstolik with passive detection collars against unauthorised taking. Survey Lead [Hesper Nakagawa](/star-rangers/characters/nakagawa/) withholds the countersign, because the herds range into the upper cavern levels and the instrument would walk toward the deep networks on a schedule nobody sets, and files the prospectus complete with the refusal. A copy is lodged with the Survey Archive at Threshold Station the same year, at the survey lead's request.

**Canon facts:**
- The proposal was not adopted. Neither half of the filed document is the Archive's voice.
- The ground the survey lead gave, in the survey lead's own words, is that the response had been written before the finding.

See: [*A Proposal to Collar the Anstolik, Refused at Countersign*](/star-rangers/codex/proposal-to-collar-the-anstolik/), [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/).
