  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Chthonari Crew's Log Line on Eden's Mount Three</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2828 UCSD, Spring</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In the spring of 2828 UCSD four [Chthonari](/star-rangers/lore/chthonari/) on a [Federation](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) contractor crew, on their first posting off the [Undersong Belt](/star-rangers/lore/undersong-belt/), cut new members under [Eden Space Habitat](/star-rangers/lore/eden-space-habitat/)'s dock ring, the understructure the fold-approach monitoring hangs from. The habitat certifies the work sound. The youngest, [Tikket](/star-rangers/characters/tikket/), hears approach mount three ringing as a mount does under more load than its record names, and the crew's own work log, kept by [Kattik](/star-rangers/characters/kattik/), carries the line the habitat's certification copy omits.

Re-baselined against an independent reference, Eden's six fold-approach instruments show their zero had absorbed the mount's deflection: it carries roughly a third again over its certified load. The Corps' fold-approach watch refuses its countersign on that ground; Orbital Five-O invites the Star Rangers' Engineering Corps to inspect jointly with Eden's engineers; and the inspection finds a bracket deformed by a 2825 UCSD berth strike under the original plating. Eden issues a corrected certification, and the watch countersigns.

**Canon facts:**
- A Chthonari crew keeps its own work log on a job, separate from a habitat's certification copy.
- The crew learned of the outcome only through the habitat's changed reading, given in answer to [Tsikk](/star-rangers/characters/tsikk/)'s standing question; it met neither the watch nor the engineers.
- The crew's new members were sound; the load they carried was the old damage. The Compact's audit found no intent.

See: [Sound, as Certified (S10E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s10/e01/s10e01c02/), [What Settles It (S10E01C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s10/e01/s10e01c05/), [Record in Order (S10E01C07)](/star-rangers/seasons/s10/e01/s10e01c07/), [Tekka](/star-rangers/characters/tekka/).
