  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">Eden's Register Gains a Second Cat</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2827 UCSD, Late Spring</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

Eden's quarterly small-livestock return carries a conduct box per animal, and a return marked *unacceptable* opens a conduct review at the habitat's animal-welfare office, whose standard outcome is rehoming off-habitat on the next transfer. In the late spring of 2827 the box was ticked against the habitat's one registered cat, [Agent Barsik](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/), after fourteen small objects went from the bureau floor's desks and nine were recovered from somewhere high or somewhere enclosed. The deck's monitoring officer's finding, *consistent with station animal (cat)*, was correct in every particular but the cat.

A second, unregistered cat, [Zhulik](/star-rangers/characters/zhulik/), belonging to a family whose small-livestock application was still in Eden's compliance queue, had been taken for the station mascot by the whole bureau floor for three weeks. The review was withdrawn on Superintendent [Rasa Oyelaran](/star-rangers/characters/rasa-oyelaran/)'s own note. Zhulik entered Eden's record the same afternoon, as a note on the first cat's welfare return, and had a line of his own by the end of the week. It reads *pet (cat), one*. The register now carries two cats.

**Canon facts:**
- Eden's quarterly small-livestock return carries a conduct box per animal; a return marked *unacceptable* opens a conduct review at the habitat's animal-welfare office, whose standard outcome is rehoming off-habitat on the next transfer.
- In late spring 2827 UCSD a second, unregistered cat, Zhulik, was taken for the station mascot by the whole bureau floor for three weeks; the review against the mascot was withdrawn on Superintendent Oyelaran's own note, and the second cat first entered Eden's record as a line on the first cat's welfare return.
