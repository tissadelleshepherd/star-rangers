  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Sentinel Reclassified</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2828 UCSD, Late Winter</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In the late winter of 2828 UCSD, reviewing [Cerebraun Hegemony](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/) survey data under the standing Survey Corps data-exchange arrangement, [Tissadelle Shepherd](/star-rangers/characters/tissadelle-shepherd/) runs [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/)'s pulse as audio and hears structure in it. Administrator-Voice [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/) authorises independent verification the same night, the only time in three centuries of service Sohrel has reopened a filing closed that long. Six days later the reclassification survives Survey Corps review, reproduced under supervision, and the Sentinel moves from *automated hazard beacon, low priority* to an active anomaly docket.

**Canon facts:**
- The finding is structured repetition with variation, and nothing more: musical structure is evidence the signal is not understood, not evidence of intelligent origin.
- The Prime Ganglion's office *formally noted* Sohrel's decision, neither sanctioning nor clearing it.
- Sohrel declined four internal Hegemony requests to raise the question with Meridian's reef-cities, the fourth the same week as the review.

See: [The Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/), [Filed Under Noise (S03E01C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c01/), [Independent Verification (S03E01C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c02/).
