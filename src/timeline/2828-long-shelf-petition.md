  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Long Shelf Asks to Be Consulted</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2828 UCSD, Early Spring</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In the early spring of 2828 UCSD the council of the Long Shelf, a reef-city of [Meridian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/), sends a formal petition to be consulted on [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/), through Meridian's customary chemical-and-percussion channel and the relay station's translation instrumentation ([Tavesh](/star-rangers/characters/tavesh/)'s channel). It is the fifth request Administrator-Voice [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/) has received to consult the reef-cities, and the first from outside the Hegemony. Sohrel authorises the consultation on the council's own terms.

The Long Shelf's own analysts, [Sixth Course](/star-rangers/characters/sixth-course/) among them, compare the Sentinel's structured phrasing with Meridian claw-percussion. They find points of resemblance in phrasing and rhythm, and establish no authorship, origin or connection, and the report to the council says so in those words.

**Canon facts:**
- The consultation was narrowly technical, and kept apart from the reef-cities' standing objections over extraction pace, a separation the council itself insisted on.
- Shepherd's audio-routing method was shared with Meridian's analysts as a documented technique, not an endorsement of any conclusion.
- The Prime Ganglion's office answered this second authorisation with a reference to the extraction portfolio's exposure, sharper than its first notice.

See: [What Meridian Asked (S03E01C03)](/star-rangers/seasons/s03/e01/s03e01c03/), [Meridian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/), [Sixth Course](/star-rangers/characters/sixth-course/).
