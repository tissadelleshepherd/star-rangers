  <p><span class="character-badge">Shortly before 2626 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

Shortly before 2626 UCSD, the [Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/)' assembly first requests [Ulessa](/star-rangers/characters/ulessa/), a [Mnemari](/star-rangers/lore/mnemari/) of [Ilenne](/star-rangers/lore/planets/ilenne/), as a witness to a session revisiting an old, contested compact. The year is the record's conversion from her span of service: in 2826 UCSD she has been requested for a little over two hundred years.

The sessions are on the Federation record, and the record does not list them. Ilenne's own dating of the year is not held.

**Canon facts:**
- Mnemari mediators and treaty-witnesses are requested for assembly sessions revisiting old compacts, on the strength of a memory that does not drift.
- Ulessa is the first Mnemari the record names. Nothing makes her the first of her kind to be requested; she is one of a routine.
- In 2826 UCSD she is on the roll of witnesses the assembly requests.

See: [Ulessa](/star-rangers/characters/ulessa/), [Mnemari](/star-rangers/lore/mnemari/), [The Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/).
