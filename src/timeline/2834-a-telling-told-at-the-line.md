  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Telling Told at the Line</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2834 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2834 UCSD, the survey's fourth year on the ground on [Fliade](/star-rangers/lore/planets/fliade/), something is said at the line by the one who answers to the one who knocked, for a stated interval, and is heard by the surface party and not recorded. The survey holds no word of it and asked for none.

The people's side of the same act is held in the Archive by way of the *Below the Roof* thread and not by anything the survey has been told: one of the up-people was told a telling at the line, and the Told no longer hold the up-people to be *people who have not been told*. Being told one thing does not make a person one of [the Told](/star-rangers/glossary/the-told/), and what the people say of the up-people since, the record has not been told.

**Canon facts:**
- The survey's name for the people, *Pandoid*, stays in every filing; nobody has yet been in a position to be told otherwise.
- The two names belong to the two sides of one encounter, and the record keeps each where it belongs.
