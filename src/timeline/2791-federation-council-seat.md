  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Federation's Seat on the Oversight Council First Filled</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2791 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In 2791 UCSD the [Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) first fills its seat on the Star Rangers' [Oversight Council](/star-rangers/lore/the-oversight-council/): a seat that hears every inquiry, may put questions to the Triumvirate and enter a dissent on the record, and carries no vote. It comes within a generation of the Federation's first contact with the Rangers and after the first renewal of the material-support compact.

The year of first contact itself is not fixed by the record, which places it only decades after the 2723 UCSD charter.

**Canon facts:**
- The Federation's is the longest-held of the non-voting seats.
- Its delegate is appointed by the assembly and replaced at each renewal of the material-support compact, which the assembly renews by vote each generation.
- The seat buys the Federation no influence over Ranger conduct; the Rangers' neutrality applies to it as to every polity.

See: [The Oversight Council](/star-rangers/lore/the-oversight-council/), [The Federation of Sentient Beings](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/).
