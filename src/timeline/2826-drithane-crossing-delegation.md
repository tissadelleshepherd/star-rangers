  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">A Survey Delegation Keeps a Crossing Night on Drithane</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2826 UCSD, Deep Winter</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In the deep winter of 2826, a Survey Corps follow-up team continuing the Corps' documentation of [Drithane](/star-rangers/lore/planets/drithane/)'s debris-band crossings was billeted in the Gleann na gCaorach valley chain for a crossing night. Under the Celtic Union's survey-hospitality arrangements the team lodged with valley households rather than in a delegation compound; Ranger presence on-world remains by invitation under the 2723 Charter, and the valley's warden, [Sorcha Nic Dhiarmada](/star-rangers/characters/sorcha-nic-dhiarmada/), kept the billet ledger.

The settlements went dark for the crossing, valley by valley, as they do. The warden walked the dark-down, and the valleys' working animals quieted with the households. The record notes, as the valley's own account does, that this is a kept custom and not a law: no record of the custom ever needing one exists.

The delegation's manifest carries one courtesy animal, a trainee of Eden's detective agency, [Bubochka](/star-rangers/characters/bubochka/), on her first off-station assignment. Nothing else that travelled with the delegation is on the manifest.

**Canon facts:**
- Drithane hosts Survey Corps follow-up teams under the Union's survey-hospitality arrangements, billeted with valley households rather than in delegation compounds; Ranger presence on-world remains invitation-only under the 2723 Charter.
- On a crossing night, Drithane's dark-down is walked by a valley warden, a kept custom and not a law, and the valleys' working animals quiet with the settlements.
- The delegation's manifest records one courtesy animal.

See: [The Dark-Down (S02E03C01)](/star-rangers/seasons/s02/e03/s02e03c01/), and for what the manifest does not carry, [Not From This House (S02E03C02)](/star-rangers/seasons/s02/e03/s02e03c02/).
