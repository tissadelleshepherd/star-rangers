  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Sentinel Filed as a Hazard Beacon</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">~2528 UCSD</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In about 2528 UCSD, three centuries before the file was reopened, the [Cerebraun Hegemony](/star-rangers/lore/cerebraun-hegemony/)'s classification of [the Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/)'s electromagnetic pulse as an automated hazard beacon, low priority, is signed and the case closed to further review. The signature is [Sohrel](/star-rangers/characters/sohrel/)'s, as Administrator-Voice for the Halyx System, a portfolio Sohrel has held for about three centuries by 2828 UCSD.

The year is the record's conversion from Sohrel's span of service. The Hegemony's own dating of the filing is not held by the record.

**Canon facts:**
- Hegemony survey passes catalogued the Sentinel's pulse as an automated hazard beacon; it matches no known beacon or communication protocol.
- Sohrel signed the original classification that closed the file.
- The classification stood, through generations of classifiers, until 2828 UCSD.

See: [The Sentinel](/star-rangers/lore/planets/sentinel/), [Meridian](/star-rangers/lore/planets/meridian/), [The Sentinel Reclassified](/star-rangers/timeline/2828-sentinel-reclassified/).
