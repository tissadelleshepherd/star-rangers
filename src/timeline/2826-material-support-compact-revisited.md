  <h1 class="lore-entry__title">The Material-Support Compact Revisited</h1>
  <p><span class="character-badge">2826 UCSD, Early Summer</span></p>
</header>
<div class="lore-entry__body">

In the early summer of 2826 UCSD a session of the [Federation](/star-rangers/lore/federation-of-sentient-beings/) assembly revisits the standing material-support compact with the Star Rangers, on a proposal sponsored by [Isren Farrowkin](/star-rangers/characters/isren-farrowkin/), holding [Verdance](/star-rangers/lore/planets/verdance/)'s seat, that the assembly formally ask the Survey Corps for its fold-route survey data of each member world's own approaches. [Prismere](/star-rangers/lore/planets/prismere/)'s seat opposes it on the compact's words, which run one way, toward the Corps. The assembly requests [Ulessa](/star-rangers/characters/ulessa/) of Ilenne as witness.

Ulessa, who was present at the compact's first table, gives the session its data term as it was spoken, and the words after it: a mark from [Cirrane](/star-rangers/lore/planets/cirrane/), read into the record after the term was taken, asking where what the Corps learns of a lane is carried, and whether a member that cannot come for it may have it. The year of that first table is not fixed by the record, and how this session decided is not on it.

**Canon facts:**
- The compact's data term runs as the compact runs: materials, fabrication capacity and fold-route survey data toward the Corps' operations.
- It is the first table the record places Ulessa at by name.
